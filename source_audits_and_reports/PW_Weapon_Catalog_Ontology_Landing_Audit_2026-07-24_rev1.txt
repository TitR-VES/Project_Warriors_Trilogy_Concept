================================================================================
01. PURPOSE
================================================================================

Create a stable, machine-readable and human-friendly catalog for portable weapons
and ammunition occurring in Project Warriors, including:

- player-issued and signature weapons;
- mission-only and scripted-perspective weapons;
- allied NPC equipment;
- standard, elite, support, and named-enemy weapons;
- hidden caches and fixed world finds;
- optional or conditional pickups;
- temporary handoffs;
- collectible-only equipment;
- equipment that occurs in the game but is not player-accessible;
- optional-objective / achievement reward lanes where source-landed.

The catalog is additive to the named-weapon registry. It does not replace named
build ownership, configuration, narrative placement, or source authority.

05. DATA MODEL
================================================================================

A. Base weapon
Stable PW-WEP-* identity for a portable weapon/platform family or an explicitly
source-preserved generic family.

B. Ammunition
Stable PW-AMM-* identity for a cartridge, shell, dart, grenade, rocket, missile,
or thrown munition. Ammunition economy is recorded separately from the weapon.

C. Occurrence
Stable PW-WOC-* identity recording:
- installment and campaign placement;
- mission labels where known;
- presence mode;
- player access;
- acquisition method;
- retention;
- drop policy;
- faction/carrier labels;
- ammunition overrides;
- unresolved fields.

D. Named-build binding
Crosswalk from PW-WPN-* named identities to PW-WEP-* base platforms.
Named build configuration and identity remain controlled by the named-build
registry.

================================================================================
06. IMPORTANT AVAILABILITY DISTINCTIONS
================================================================================

The projection does not treat occurrence as access.

Examples preserved:

- SR-3M in Hull Breach is conditional on raising the alarm.
- SR-1 Vektor in Low Ground is available only through the failed-observation branch.
- CMMG Mk47 and Five-seveN in Down Rodeo are missable if the elite target escapes.
- APS and SPP-1M in Wirecutter are collectible/limited specialist finds.
- Tikka T3x TAC A1 in Cold Plunge is scripted and explicitly not recoverable.
- War Within SUCKER PUNCH is a temporary handoff, not persistent ownership.
- Blommaert's DUE DILIGENCE occurs as named NPC equipment but player acquisition
  remains open.
- RPG-7 in the Rotterdam finale occurs as enemy equipment but does not become a
  player pickup in the locked sequence.
- allied NPC weapons remain present even where the player cannot directly access them.
- Troy Barry's reward lane exists, but the exact item/objective table remains open.

================================================================================
07. AMMUNITION / ECONOMY NOTES
================================================================================

The catalog preserves the source-landed economy:

- 5.56x45mm NATO is the broad baseline.
- 7.62x39mm is a rich cross-faction scavenge pool.
- 9x39mm forms a self-contained specialist pool.
- .300 BLK is deliberately premium and scarce.
- 12GA DB is a locked specialty-ammunition concept with open timing and
  fire-propagation implementation dependencies.

No complete projectile-stat table, damage table, or real-world instruction set is
introduced.

08. OPEN FLAGS
================================================================================

1. Campaigns with incomplete weapon-development passes may add weapons and
   occurrences through additive deltas.
2. Who Dares Wins has no newly invented arsenal; its weapon pass remains open.
3. Exact Troy Barry achievement/optional-objective reward mapping remains open.
4. Dragon's Breath availability timing and compatible level list remain open.
5. Enemy-type carry, drop, ammo, and encounter mappings are reserved for the next pass.
6. Nine named weapon identities retain platform-recovery-required bindings.
7. Some roster-level/prototype weapons retain caliber and placement flags.
8. Equipment listed only as an attachment remains outside the base weapon catalog
   unless it functions as a separately usable weapon.
9. Vehicle-mounted, aircraft, artillery, and off-map fire-support weapons are outside
   this portable-arsenal pass.
