SUPPRESSOR IDENTITY CHECK

VISUAL / NAME LOGIC CHECK
PASS. The tan/FDE finish provides a restrained visual connection to the SANDMAN name.
The black grips preserve a strong two-tone recognition cue. The Hudson H9's unusual
near-futuristic profile supports the Logan's Run association without replica styling,
and the black flat-sided Osprey creates the build's most distinctive elongated outline.

HANDLING BALANCE CHECK
PASS. Bare SANDMAN's exceptional recoil control, low muzzle rise, very fast ADS and high
aim stability form one coherent control/speed identity. The source does not also give
the pistol excessive raw damage, armor defeat or long-range performance. Faster and
more accurate follow-up shots are the intended unsuppressed reward.

PASS. The Osprey SHS creates a clear suppressed-state trade-off. It provides exceptional
signature reduction but adds mass ahead of the muzzle, shifts the center of gravity
forward, makes ADS noticeably slower and makes rapid repeated follow-up shots slightly
less stable. Recoil remains soft, but the player must manage the visible front weight.
This prevents the suppressor from becoming a no-cost universal upgrade.

AMMUNITION PROGRESSION CHECK
PASS. Standard 9x19mm is a common, relatively inexpensive baseline suitable for soft
and lightly armored targets. Later subsonic 9x19mm is not a pure upgrade: with the
Osprey SHS it provides the strongest sound reduction while accepting slight damage and
range reductions. The suppressor's ADS and repeated-shot stability penalties still
apply. Exact numerical values and unlock economy remain open.

RELIABILITY / MALFUNCTION CHECK
PASS. The M1911 comparison is treated as a project gameplay reference lane rather
than a claim that SANDMAN should jam frequently. The pistol retains low absolute risk
when maintained, but has a slightly higher weapon-specific baseline than that reference.
Risk remains condition-driven through damage, contamination, poor ammunition,
suppressor use and incomplete mechanical state.

Simple stoppages use Hawthorne's practiced rapid immediate-action animation through
the contextual reload / restore-to-ready input. Complex stoppages retain longer,
physically honest remedial clears and persistent magazine/slide/chamber state. Hard
failures remain exceptional, and routine randomness cannot permanently erase the
named weapon.

LITERARY-REFERENCE CHECK
PASS. The Logan's Run connection is preserved as Hawthorne's personal naming logic.
The source avoids inventing decorative quotes, logos or replica styling.
