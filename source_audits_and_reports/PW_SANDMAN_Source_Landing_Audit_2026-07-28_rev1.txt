  ergonomics, very fast ADS, high aim stability and rapid accurate follow-up shots.

  soft and lightly armored targets within intended pistol range.
- Later ammunition: 9x19mm subsonic; strongest sound reduction with the Osprey 45 2.0;
  slight damage and effective-range reduction.
- Guide-rod walk-out: 4% per shot in the first consecutive rapid-fire magazine;
  50% chance during the second; 96% chance per third and later sustained-fire magazine.
- Fresh-magazine first-round choke: 10% per eligible reload; tactical reload excluded.
- Stiff-magazine extraction: 20% per reload involving magazine removal.
- Slide-catch slingshot: 12.5% per eligible non-tactical reload.
- In-cycle feed choke: 2.5% per shot.

HANDLING BALANCE CHECK
PASS. Bare SANDMAN's exceptional recoil control, low muzzle rise, very fast ADS and high
aim stability form one coherent control/speed identity. The source does not also give
the pistol excessive raw damage, armor defeat or long-range performance. Faster and
more accurate follow-up shots are the intended unsuppressed reward.

PASS. The compact TLR-3 does not overwrite the base handling identity. Its low mounting
position is a visual and control detail rather than an excuse to assign the same major
front-weight penalty as the suppressor. Any small numerical light-equipped effect
remains open for tuning.

PASS. The Osprey 45 2.0 creates a clear suppressed-state trade-off. It provides
exceptional signature reduction but adds mass ahead of the muzzle, shifts the center
of gravity forward, makes ADS noticeably slower and makes rapid repeated follow-up
shots slightly less stable. Recoil remains soft, but the player must manage the visible
front weight. This prevents the suppressor from becoming a no-cost universal upgrade.

AMMUNITION PROGRESSION CHECK
PASS. Standard 9x19mm is a common, relatively inexpensive baseline suitable for soft
and lightly armored targets. Later subsonic 9x19mm is not a pure upgrade: with the
Osprey 45 2.0 it provides the strongest sound reduction while accepting slight damage
and range reductions. The suppressor's ADS and repeated-shot stability penalties still
apply. Exact numerical values and unlock economy remain open.

PASS. Guide-rod walk-out uses the intended mixed probability scope:

PASS. Fresh-magazine first-round choke uses a 10% eligible-reload check. Tactical

PASS. Stiff-magazine extraction uses a 20% check for every reload involving magazine

changing the chambered-round state by itself.

PASS. Slide-catch slingshot uses a 12.5% eligible non-tactical reload check. A

restore-to-ready after magazine insertion so Hawthorne can chamber the first round.
The exact post-insertion branch remains open.

PASS. In-cycle feed choke uses a 2.5% per-shot check and a state-dependent clear that
is shorter than the guide-rod walk-out recovery.
