PACING RESULT

The first three nodes require vehicle-led or compressed movement. Base Defense and
Tower have distinct information functions. Combat must occur between briefings.
The final return is no longer an open implementation choice: Delta leaves Zuñiga
on foot, moves northeast only a short distance, is met by an arriving Humvee, and
is taken directly to NCTS. The player observes the ride but does not drive. This
removes the long clean retrace while preserving a consequence-viewing transition.

COMBAT-DENSITY RESULT

The KMZ is not a complete finale ladder by itself. The source now requires combat
pressure in the following spaces:
- bridge / approach;
- gate or perimeter;
- hangar and service-road corridor;
- UAV-guidance engagement;
- range-sector handoff;
- Zuñiga observation-post defense.

The Humvee return is not a new final combat arena. It displays the changed
battlespace: reduced UAV accuracy, lighter naval fire, alert aircraft airborne,
and defenders regaining movement.

FIRE-SUPPORT RESULT

The player does not personally launch NMESIS. Delta identifies and tracks the
correct hostile vessels, passes the fire-control solution, and confirms the launch
window. The missile strike breaks the naval formation and creates a temporary
window for already armed alert aircraft to launch and attack.

RETURN AND CLOSEOUT RESULT

- Delta begins the return from the Zuñiga sector on foot.
- An arriving Humvee reaches the team a short distance into the northeast trek.
- The full team boards and is transported directly to NCTS.
- The player does not drive the Humvee.

- Delta enters the NCTS-hosted emergency cell and delivers the final report.

RESULT

The map is a viable and visually strong geographic backbone for TIDEFIRE. Four
spatial / naming corrections and one pacing lock are necessary: correct the
adjacent residential terminology, fictionalize the first command post, move the
range handoff southwest, move the designation objective to the Zuñiga sector, and
close the finale with a short on-foot departure followed by an arriving Humvee
pickup and direct NCTS transport. With those changes, the objective flow supports
a credible Game 1 climax and cleanly preserves the Coronado-to-Calloway bridge.
