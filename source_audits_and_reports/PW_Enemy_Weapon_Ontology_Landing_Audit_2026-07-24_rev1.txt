04. ACCESS / DROP GOVERNANCE
================================================================================
Carried weapon, drop behavior, and player pickup state are represented independently.
The ontology preserves:
- conditional alarm spawns;
- failed-objective pickups;
- named-enemy drops;
- fixed-world or unbolted emplacements;
- collectible-only underwater equipment;
- scripted non-accessible weapons;
- missable escape-timer rewards;
- carried equipment whose drop state is not source-locked.

No enemy equipment is promoted to persistent ownership merely because an enemy carries it.
