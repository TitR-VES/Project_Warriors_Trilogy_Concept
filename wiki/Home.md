# Project Warriors — Knowledge Wiki

Project Warriors is a grounded tactical-shooter narrative spanning **four main games**, three recurring protagonists and nine DLC/side-story workstreams. The original trilogy is shaped by **Fracture • Resistance • Restoration**; Game 4 examines what viable restoration means.
