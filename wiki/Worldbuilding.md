# Worldbuilding and factions

The game world spans post-PANDORA North America, European NATO operations, the Russian Far East, the Pacific/Asian strategic front, and deniable contest in Latin America.

**DAOG** includes OLYMPUS command, AEGIS, CORYS, SHIELD and MINERVA (formal TRU/SIGHT). **AFC** emerges from the Kentucky resistance, using Republic Shield as the main emblem, Breach Star as field tag and Union Mark as older Boston mark. **PROCELLA** is a Far East survival-route network, while **NAF/Vanguard** develops into a later domestic armed movement.

Taiwanese semiconductor disruption remains a long-term strategic limit. The later limited China–Israel nuclear exchange leaves the Scorched Crescent rather than an undifferentiated global apocalypse.
