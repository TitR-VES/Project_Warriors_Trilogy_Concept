# Gameplay systems

Game 1 uses first-person gameplay, with third-person reserved for framing cinematics. Frost/Lupin perspective switches are scripted in missions where their capabilities matter. A restrained narrative-prompt vocabulary reflects each playable character.

Magazines retain physical state, partial loads and chamber occupancy; remaining ammunition can be inspected without a constant HUD ammo counter. Source references also cover scavenging, consolidation, feed types, heat/barrel states, malfunction, first-person weapon animation, hit-zone/armor systems, injury, stealth, UAV patrols and NPC weapon parity.
