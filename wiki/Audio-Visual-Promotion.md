# Audio, visual references and promotion

Music is diegetic world texture wherever practical. Game 2 teaser anchors include *Good Morning, America* (Romeo), *Kyiv* (Ducky) and *King Nothing* for Spook's promotional tone. Licensing is a separate future production requirement.

Text-based image prompt packets are durable visual references, while individual generated renders and promotional art must not silently override identity/mission canon. Aesthetic restraint, natural sound and silence are recurring creative principles.
