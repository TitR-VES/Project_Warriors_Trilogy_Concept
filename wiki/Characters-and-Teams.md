# Characters and teams

Recurring playable protagonists: [[Character-Romeo]], [[Character-Ducky]] and [[Character-Spook]].

**Fire Team Delta:** Romeo, Dingo, Howler, Beaver. **AEGIS Squad:** Ducky, Frost, Lupin, Switchblade. **Victor Two Bravo / The Tourists:** Spook, Tripper, Smoke. **SHIELD:** Lock, Mallet, Suture, Splice. **MINERVA/TRU/SIGHT:** DAOG intelligence/counterintelligence, separate from AEGIS and CORYS. **Nereid Black:** Maritime special operations, including DLC playable Grim.

Other playable characters include Frost and Lupin's temporary mission perspectives and DLC leads La Avispa, André Bridges, Hawthorne and Sakhalin.
