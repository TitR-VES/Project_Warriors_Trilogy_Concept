# DLC and side stories

- [[DLC-HomeDefense]] — Australian/Pacific resistance campaign best placed after Game 4, emotionally centered on Lucas Hill. Switchblade is connective; preserve the J-20/RHIB correction.
- [[DLC-Nightfall]] — Late post-network-state era with Deluvia/Damhaven, the Scorched Crescent and Cheyenne. LONG SHADOWS is a singular, restrained memorial-like environmental scene.
- [[DLC-SeaSpear]] — Nereid Black maritime DLC led by playable Grim, including DEEP CUT and a crossover with AEGIS FALLS.
- [[DLC-SemperFi]] — West Coast U.S. Marine Corps perspective led by Staff Sergeant André Nathaniel Bridges.
- [[DLC-TheCollapse]] — Federal authority erodes roughly two to three years after Game 4; AID AND COMFORT and MARKED SAFE address targeting of relief infrastructure.
- [[DLC-TheStorm]] — Denis Malinin/SAKHALIN supports PROCELLA's Russian Far East littoral routes; FURTHER EAST synchronizes with OUTER PORT.
- [[DLC-TheWasp]] — La Avispa's intelligence/underworld story uses MANIFEST, an aftermath-first selective flashback from Bruges to Brussels to a sabotaged Vienna flight.
- [[DLC-WarWithin]] — Nicholas Wayne Hawthorne/CROATOAN joins the New American Frontier Vanguard; his story includes the VIATECH '31 assassination and a death-or-capture branch.
- [[DLC-WhoDaresWins]] — British SAS/home-front and Boston-support DLC, with Mace, Vickers, hostage extraction and harbor-action packets.
