# PANDORA / TETHERBREAK

PANDORA's **7 September 2028** cyber-physical military disaster grew out of CIA TETHERBREAK vulnerability research. The Palisade inference ASIC carried a covert credential-enrollment pathway; SentinelMesh software delivered a preparatory OTA payload; a shadow PKI and ARGUS-RN (“Tombstone”) mesh let criminal commands appear cryptographically valid. Targeting and IFF were altered while autonomous coordination amplified the attack. Fake China-attribution evidence was inserted through routing and fabricated intelligence.

Y. T. Lin's knowledge helps neutralize the active shadow-credential system, but revocation does **not** remove the hardware vulnerability; remediation is prolonged.
