================================================================================
LOCKED FINALE STRUCTURE
================================================================================

AEGIS FALLS remains Mission 13 and the final mission of Ducky's Game 2 campaign,
PERSISTENT THREAT.

It is a single synchronized compound operation with two simultaneous arms:

1. AEGIS conducts an unauthorized operation at Tremblay's GenCorp-connected estate near
   Aix-en-Provence.
2. Nereid Black and SHIELD intercept a Russian-backed shadow-fleet attack on a strategic
   undersea cable terminal in Danish territorial waters, while the Royal Danish Navy
   intercepts the operation's support vessel.

The maritime target is Denmark, not a generic French-coast target.

AEGIS squad does not participate in the maritime assault and then travel to Aix. The
squad is already positioned at the estate. Ducky enters Tremblay's study alone while the
rest of AEGIS secures the property, escape routes, communications, and evidence.

ORACLE is the compartmented command bridge between the two arms.
TRU/SIGHT is the real-time intelligence and evidentiary bridge.

================================================================================
AEGIS SYSTEM / TRU-SIGHT MECHANISM
================================================================================

Both shadow-fleet vessels disable their transponders. AEGIS the system continues to track
them non-cooperatively through fused sensor data.

The autonomous threat watcher identifies pre-attack behavior consistent with an
undersea-infrastructure sabotage operation and would normally propagate a warning through
DAOG and NATO response channels.

Tremblay uses his ZEUS-level credential to suppress warning propagation and normalize the
ships as legitimate traffic posing no actionable threat.

He does not erase the raw tracks or make the ships invisible.

TRU/SIGHT has less-powerful emergency contingency authorities that preserve covert human
analysis, raw event access, and an off-book track. TRU/SIGHT cannot globally countermand
Tremblay's senior order, but it detects and records the override while continuing to
observe the ships.

Tremblay's act is knowing facilitation of the attack. He is a culpable accessory, not the
operation's mastermind.

================================================================================
RUSSIAN LEVERAGE AND PRIOR OVERRIDES
================================================================================

An FSB / SVR handler or associated cutout coerces Tremblay before the attack.

Russian intelligence holds evidence of analogous past overrides, including:

- remnants Tremblay failed to remove completely from distributed AEGIS event and activity
  audit logs; and
- backup copies acquired through the AEGIS compromise before Tremblay successfully
  scrubbed the corresponding internal records.

The preferred recovery path for the Russian-held copies is HULL BREACH exploitation of
the Akademik Volkov.

The prior records establish a pattern and provide blackmail leverage. Tremblay reluctantly
waves through the Danish attack because the handler threatens disclosure to his
superiors, allied governments, and NATO command.

His coercion explains but does not excuse the decision. His previous concealment and
unauthorized management of the compromise created the leverage.

After the override, Tremblay waits in his study for a secure completion call confirming
success and instructing him to remove the remaining record.

================================================================================
INTERVENTION AND EVIDENCE
================================================================================

The red line is reached when staging is complete and the sabotage device begins
preparation for deployment.

The maritime elements are already in an assured interception position. They do not permit
the device to be placed near the cable terminal merely to gather more evidence.

ORACLE triggers both arms at the red line.

The final proof package combines:

- TRU/SIGHT's prior shadow-fleet assessment;
- raw AEGIS non-cooperative tracks after transponder shutdown;
- the original heuristic threat event;
- Tremblay's authenticated ZEUS override;
- the false legitimate-traffic disposition propagated up normal channels;
- TRU/SIGHT's preserved covert track;
- attacker communications;
- observed staging and device-preparation activity;
- SHIELD's seizure of the device, control systems, targeting data, and technical records;
- Nereid Black and SHIELD helmet-camera records;
- Royal Danish Navy boarding and detention records;
- prior AEGIS audit remnants and Russian backup copies;
- synchronized timestamps tying Tremblay's intervention to the hostile attack window.

This closes the distinction between HULL BREACH and AEGIS FALLS:

- HULL BREACH proves Tremblay's credential was embedded in the hostile architecture.
- AEGIS FALLS proves Tremblay personally uses it in real time to protect a specific
  foreign attack.

================================================================================
MARITIME PACKAGE
================================================================================

DEPLOYMENT VESSEL

The primary vessel carries the covert sabotage team, underwater deployment equipment,
the sabotage device, communications and control systems, and targeting records.

Nereid Black leads the assault and clears the vessel after the sabotage team resists.
SHIELD secures the device and performs technical exploitation.

SUPPORT VESSEL

The second ship supplies fuel, stores, logistical cover, concealed weapons, and
additional armed manpower. It is not configured to deploy the device.

The Royal Danish Navy intercepts it shortly after execution begins. Most contractor
security personnel surrender when Danish forces establish decisive control.

The contractor contingent is chosen for affordability, adequate competence, deniability,
and social and linguistic isolation in European ports. Personnel may come from the
Russian Far East, Southeast Asia, India, Pakistan, or other regions and have varied
backgrounds.

Nationality is not proof of criminality. The Russian operation exploits European racial
and cultural assumptions so unfamiliar non-European personnel will be misread as ordinary
foreign merchant crew working for an Asian commercial carrier.

Distinguish:

- legitimate, deceived, or coerced maritime crew;
- falsely registered contractor security personnel;
- a small Russian or Russian-aligned coordination element.

================================================================================
DUCKY / TREMBLAY LIVE-FEED BEAT
================================================================================

Ducky enters Tremblay's study alone.

When Ducky states that the attack has been stopped, he removes a hardened secure device
from his uniform shirt's chest pocket and opens an ORACLE-routed live feed from Nereid
Black and SHIELD helmet cameras.

The feed shows the post-assault sweep of the deployment vessel, SHIELD securing the
sabotage device and technical spaces, and confirmation that the Royal Danish Navy controls
the support vessel.

The feed is understated psychological pressure, not the sole proof. It demonstrates to
Tremblay that the attack has failed, the physical evidence is being preserved, the
Russian operation cannot warn him, and his records can no longer be sanitized.

The confrontation proceeds to the protected exchange:

Tremblay: “AEGIS falls regardless.”
Ducky: “Then we build something better.”

Tremblay conditionally surrenders and agrees to Walker's process, with the condition that
Crodyn not be protected.
