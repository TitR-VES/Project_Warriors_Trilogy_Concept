CORE / TIMELINE
================================================================================
- SHADOW OF WAR is an introductory flashback mission for Project Warriors: The Storm.
- Mission opens September 6, 2028 at 2243 EDT at Kings Bay, Georgia, United States.
- Team receives a hard three-hour completion/exfil window, giving a 0143 EDT
  September 7 clear-by point.
- Palisade final compromised PANDORA enabling update occurs at 0230 EDT September 7.
- Existing September 4 maintenance/staging propagation remains compatible: 0230 is
  the final enabling/synchronization step, not the beginning of the whole compromise.
- The timebox implies compartmented GRU/GU high-command foreknowledge of the PANDORA
  timetable; Malinin and the tactical team do not know the reason.

================================================================================
CHARACTER / DLC
================================================================================
- Denis Nikolaevich Malinin is Senior Sergeant during SHADOW OF WAR.
- He is a highly competent Russian combat-diver / maritime-special-operations
  professional inside a seven-man military-intelligence sabotage team.
- Intended mission tasking lane: GRU/GU 8th Directorate; exact permanent parent
  unit remains open.
- SHADOW OF WAR is explicitly not his break with the regime.
- His later fracture is cumulative and post-WWIII: promised prosperity/national
  renewal/rewards of victory fail to materialize while deprivation, coercion, and
  totalitarian repression of Russian citizens deepen.
- Exact final break incident/threshold remains open.

MISSION / SCENE / CINEMATIC
================================================================================
- Mission title: SHADOW OF WAR.
- Target: USS Wyoming (SSBN-742), mission-kill / sortie-denial objective.
- Cold open begins already underwater; screen fades from black into dark Atlantic
  blue-green while the team is moving toward the objective.
- Base-wide detection before charge initiation is effectively mission failure;
  stealth kills are permitted but not glamorized.
- Kings Bay must look like an ordinary U.S./allied installation using the same
  visual grammar as friendly American bases elsewhere in the series. The inversion
  comes from playing the foreign intruder.
- Detonation is viewed from protected cover at distance after withdrawal from the
  immediate objective. Presentation emphasizes alarms, lighting changes, smoke,
  confusion, and consequence rather than graphic casualties or spectacle.
- Malinin exit-line intent: “We've done our work. Time is short. Let's move.”
- After detonation the mission changes from covert infiltration to fugitive exfil.
- Real-site security/perimeter/vulnerability details must be fictionalized.
