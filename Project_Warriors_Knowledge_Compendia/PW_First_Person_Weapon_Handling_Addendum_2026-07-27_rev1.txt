================================================================================
01. SHOULDER SWITCHING
================================================================================

PROPOSED DIRECTION
All credibly shoulderable weapons support a deliberate dominant-side / support-side shoulder state unless the weapon is fixed, mounted, bipod-locked, lacking a usable stock, or the player is in a body state that makes the transition impossible.

Key rules:
- shoulder state persists through ordinary movement and temporary centered/compressed animations
- the transition is quick but not instantaneous and briefly breaks the sight picture
- exact hand transfer is weapon- and character-specific rather than universally mirrored
- support-side use remains tactically valid, with only subtle realism-setting handling differences
- correct shoulder selection reduces actual body exposure around cover
- weapon length, suppressors, stock geometry, and local collision remain meaningful
- the camera cannot peek independently of the body, weapon, muzzle, and collision pose

================================================================================
02. AIMING / LEANING / CORNER PEEKING
================================================================================

PROPOSED DIRECTION
Project Warriors uses freeform ADS, lean, stance, movement, and shoulder selection rather than a sticky cover-snap system.

The player slices corners by:
- choosing the near-side shoulder
- controlling distance from the edge
- using slow lateral movement
- adding lean as required
- entering ADS only when both optic and muzzle can clear

Additional rules:
- standing, crouched, and physically credible prone variants are supported
- sprinting and high-mobility actions force a ready/compressed state
- long weapons compress instead of clipping through geometry
- optic clearance and muzzle clearance are evaluated separately
- blind fire is not a universal default mechanic
- ADS settings such as magnification, reticle, laser, and fire selector persist through shoulder changes
- NPC use scales by training tier, with elite enemies minimizing exposure and lower-tier enemies more likely to remain on the wrong shoulder

================================================================================
03. MALFUNCTIONS / RELOAD-CLEAR INTEGRATION
================================================================================

PROPOSED DIRECTION
Clearable malfunctions become uncommon persistent mechanical states rather than one-off canned jam animations.

The reload input becomes the contextual restore-to-ready input:
- normal reload when the weapon is otherwise functional
- resume incomplete reload from stored state
- short immediate-action clear for a simple stoppage
- longer remedial clear for a complex stoppage
- no dishonest clear animation when the weapon is hard-disabled

The exact animation depends on weapon family and state. Possible field states include:
- failure to fire
- improperly seated magazine
- feed / out-of-battery stoppage
- trapped ejected casing
- failure to extract
- double feed
- belt/feed-cover stoppage
- shotgun cycling/feed stoppage
- bolt-action feed/extraction/ejection stoppage

All clearing actions inherit the July 25 persistent mechanical-state lock. If a magazine is removed, the action is locked open, or a casing has been ejected before interruption, that physical condition remains when the player switches weapons, moves, is hit, or abandons the clear.

The player retains a tactical choice between clearing, transitioning to a secondary, moving to cover, or replacing/abandoning the weapon.

================================================================================
04. FREQUENCY / FAIRNESS / HARD FAILURES
================================================================================

PROPOSED DIRECTION
Malfunctions should follow fiction-visible causes rather than a flat per-shot random chance.

Risk sources include:
- damage, neglect, corrosion, dirt, heat, sand, mud, water, or ice
- damaged magazines, belts, links, or feed devices
- poor, contaminated, mismatched, or improvised ammunition
- suppressor/platform interactions
- severe impact or an interrupted/incomplete reload
- authored weapon-specific vulnerabilities

Maintained issued weapons and signature builds retain very low base risk.

Fully disabling failures remain exceptional:
- primarily scripted; or
- condition-gated behind severe damage, contamination, neglect, or unsuitable ammunition

Routine randomness must not permanently erase a named/signature weapon. Hard-disabled weapons may require safe-zone repair, armorer/workbench support, replacement, or authored recovery.

================================================================================
05. DIFFICULTY / ACCESSIBILITY / NPC PARITY
================================================================================

Standard or lower difficulty may:
- restrict malfunctions largely to authored/extreme-condition cases
- identify the clear input with a restrained prompt
- provide modest shoulder/cover alignment assistance
- offer optional near-cover shoulder suggestion or auto-selection

Maximum realism may:
- use condition-driven stoppages at intended frequency
- remove textual diagnosis
- enforce stricter body/muzzle/optic geometry
- retain full interrupted-state and ammunition/feed consequences

Accessibility options proposed:
- shoulder switch toggle/hold
- lean toggle/hold
- rebindable inputs
- optional near-cover shoulder suggestion or automatic selection
- reduced camera/weapon translation
- clearer obstruction and malfunction prompts
- simplified malfunction frequency

NPCs follow the same broad state rules. Irregulars may fumble or overexpose; trained troops seek cover and clear; elite enemies decide quickly between clearing, transition, and movement. NPCs do not instantly reset to ready weapons.

06. OWNER ADJUDICATION STILL REQUIRED
================================================================================

Open questions:
1. support-side handling penalty on default difficulty versus realism only
2. fully manual shoulder switching versus optional auto-near-shoulder behavior
3. automatic state-correct clearing on maximum realism versus repeated input separating immediate and remedial action
4. whether field maintenance/cleaning should be developed immediately as the principal prevention system
5. whether hard-disabled non-signature weapons are generally recoverable for later repair or normally discarded/replaced

Until those questions are resolved, this addendum records a proposed development direction rather than a final canon/mechanics lock.
