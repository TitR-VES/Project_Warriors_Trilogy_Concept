1. CORE LORE DELTA
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

The War Wolves are the three surviving core members of Oscar Four Delta, a highly
compartmentalized mid-2000s CIA Global Response Staff / Ground Branch-adjacent
action cell.

Oscar Four Delta was operationally elite and morally rotten. Its members crossed
from unauthorized clandestine violence into atrocity and criminal service work,
then accepted protection and continued employment from Goldberg's KINGMAKER /
Shadow Underground structure when exposure became likely.

The War Wolves are therefore not clean operators whom Goldberg corrupted. They are
men who had already demonstrated what they were willing to do and found a power
structure willing to protect and keep using them.
