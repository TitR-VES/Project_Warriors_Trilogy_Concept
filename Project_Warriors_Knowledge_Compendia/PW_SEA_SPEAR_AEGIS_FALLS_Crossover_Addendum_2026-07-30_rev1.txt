================================================================================
CANON DECISION
================================================================================

The Danish maritime interception that forms the second, off-site arm of Ducky's
PERSISTENT THREAT finale AEGIS FALLS is also a mandatory playable mainline mission
in PROJECT WARRIORS: SEA SPEAR.

The SEA SPEAR player controls Grim, the DLC's sole primary playable protagonist,
and experiences Nereid Black's assault on the covert deployment vessel first-hand.
Rime, Undertow, and Lantern remain squadmate NPCs. SHIELD follows into the sensitive
technical spaces to render safe the sabotage device and preserve the evidence. The
Royal Danish Navy intercepts the support vessel in parallel.

================================================================================
CONTINUITY RULE
================================================================================

This is one canon event depicted from two campaign perspectives:

- PERSISTENT THREAT / Ducky: the player remains with AEGIS at Tremblay's estate near
  Aix-en-Provence and sees the maritime success through ORACLE-routed live footage.
- SEA SPEAR / Grim: the player directly performs the maritime interception in Danish
  territorial waters and originates the authenticated helmet-camera feed used in the
  Tremblay confrontation.

AEGIS FALLS remains one synchronized two-arm finale in Ducky's thirteen-mission
ladder. The SEA SPEAR version does not create an extra Ducky mission, a sequential
travel handoff, or an alternate outcome.

================================================================================
LOCKED SHARED OUTCOME
================================================================================

- Tremblay suppresses warning propagation with his ZEUS-level credential but does
  not erase raw vessel tracks.
- TRU/SIGHT preserves the event and evidence stream covertly.
- ORACLE triggers intervention when hostile staging is complete and device
  preparation begins.
- Nereid Black secures the deployment vessel.
- SHIELD secures and exploits the device and technical systems.
- The Royal Danish Navy controls the support vessel.
- The sabotage device is stopped before deployment.
- The authenticated live feed is routed to Ducky.
- Tremblay's remaining deniability collapses and he conditionally surrenders to
  Walker's process.
