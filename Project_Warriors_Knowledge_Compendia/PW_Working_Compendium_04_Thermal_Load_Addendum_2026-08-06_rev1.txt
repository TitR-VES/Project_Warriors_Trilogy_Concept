GAMEPLAY / WEAPONS / STYLE — THERMAL-LOAD ADDENDUM
================================================================================

- Select-fire weapons accumulate continuous, platform-specific thermal load; the system does not use a flat “one penalty per dumped magazine” counter.
- Sustained automatic fire and repeated reload–magdump cycles raise heat much faster than disciplined semi-automatic fire or short bursts.
- Progressive heat consequences include physical dispersion, optic mirage, degraded recoil recovery, increased plausible stoppage risk, and only at extreme platform-specific states cyclic irregularity, component failure, runaway behavior, or cook-off.
- Heat state persists through reloads, weapon switches, mounting, dropping, and handoffs. Cooling occurs continuously while the weapon is not firing; reloading alone does not reset heat.
- Transient thermal penalties clear after sufficient cooling. Any permanent wear/damage remains a separate condition system.
- Suppressors accelerate and retain heat during sustained fire and may add backpressure, mirage, glow, visual-signature, handling, degradation, or failure consequences where the specific weapon/device supports them.
- Cook-off is rare and platform-specific, especially relevant to a live round left chambered in a critically overheated closed-bolt weapon. Open-bolt support weapons mitigate that particular risk but remain vulnerable to other heat failures.
- LMGs, GPMGs, HMGs, and other support weapons receive weapon-appropriate higher thermal capacity, heavier barrels, open-bolt behavior, and sustained-fire doctrine; they are not heat-immune.
- Appropriate machine guns may use a vulnerable quick-change-barrel interaction when a compatible spare barrel is actually available. A barrel change cools the barrel state, not the entire receiver, feed system, gas system, suppressor, or heat-soaked assembly.
- The mechanic must preserve legitimate full-auto use for emergency close contact, ambush breaking, room/vehicle fights, and suppression. It punishes abuse, not automatic fire itself.
- Feedback should remain animation-, audio-, inspect-, and behavior-led. Standard difficulty may use restrained WEAPON HOT / HOLD FIRE / CHANGE BARREL cues; maximum realism relies more heavily on physical observation.
- NPCs follow the same broad rules. Low-discipline enemies may overfire; trained and elite forces use bursts, rotate weapons, change barrels, or disengage before critical heat.
