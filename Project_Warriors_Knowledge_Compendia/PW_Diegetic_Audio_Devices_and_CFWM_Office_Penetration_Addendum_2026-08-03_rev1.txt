DIEGETIC AUDIO DEVICES

- Ordinary exposed radios, portable speakers, stereos, televisions used as audible
  media sources, and similar devices are physical, player-operable world objects.
- Reachable functional devices may expose TURN OFF and, where useful, TURN ON.
- Direct small-arms fire or damaging nearby blast can disable them.
- Local program audio stops immediately or through a very brief failure transient when
  the working threshold is crossed.
- The emitter follows a still-functioning movable device and never remains at an old
  position after the prop falls.
- Destroyed or disabled state persists through compatible encounter checkpoints.
- Reinforced, recessed, concealed, and protected installation hardware is exempt from
  casual small-arms destruction, but remains dependent on power, signal, and upstream
  equipment.

COME FLY WITH ME RADIO

- The exposed radio or compact sound system carrying the preferred Frank Sinatra
  "Come Fly With Me" cue inherits the game-wide interaction and damage rules.
- The player can switch it off at close range, including during or immediately after
  the relevant drawer-opening sequence when reach and line of sight permit.
- Direct gunfire and nearby explosions can disable or destroy it and end the music.
- The final mission pass must decide whether the safehouse and manager-office cue
  references represent one retained placement or two deliberately repeated uses.
- Music clearance and a mid-century lounge / big-band fallback remain production
  dependencies.

COME FLY WITH ME UNAWARE OFFICE ATTACK

- The manager-office door is closed but unlocked while the occupants remain unaware.
- BREACH remains the coordinated team-entry prompt, not a lock-defeat interaction.
- Firing through the door before entry cancels the semi-scripted unaware choreography
  and begins a freeform combat branch.
- The two guards and armed manager take cover and return fire through the same
  penetrable door.
- AI initially uses impact direction, sound, callouts, and last-known position rather
  than perfect sight through opaque material.
- Accuracy can improve as bullet holes, edge gaps, latch failure, hinge failure, or a
  falling door create real sightlines.
- Door states progress from intact to perforated, compromised, partially detached, and
  open / fully detached as damage warrants.
- A surviving unlocked door can still be opened normally; no forced breach is required.
- Stun grenades support a manager-survival solution.
- Fragmentation and blast-overpressure grenades remain valid but can kill the manager,
  damage the radio, remove the door, and trigger the existing deceased-manager key-
  recovery fallback.
- The branch must remain difficult but fair: shared penetration rules, viable cover and
  repositioning, no perfect wall-tracking, no one-way player-only penetration, and no
  automatic mission failure solely because the manager dies.
