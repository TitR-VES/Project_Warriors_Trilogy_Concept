HANDLING / PRESENTATION SUMMARY
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

TROOPER is a compact full-power rifle, not a 5.56mm carbine with a different skin.
Its identity requires:
- stronger recoil and muzzle disturbance
- effective semi-automatic precision from close to medium range
- credible short controlled automatic bursts at close range
- rapidly degrading control under undisciplined sustained automatic fire
- a real low-power / magnified trade-off through the 1-4x LPVO
- a fixed forward 3 o'clock white-light position
- a suppressor option that reduces signature while adding forward mass and slowing presentation
- no Hollywood-quiet treatment and no no-cost suppressed upgrade

Exact optic, mount, suppressor, muzzle interface, light model and controls, barrel
length, sling, furniture, finish, magazines, ammunition subtypes and numerical
tuning remain open.
