TIDEFIRE LOCK

TIDEFIRE uses a persistent peripheral terminal-air-defense layer around NAS North Island.

The layer is prominent in the mission’s total sensory identity, but it is not normally centered in the player’s attention. Land-based Phalanx / LPWS, C-RAM, C-UAS, and possible nearby shipboard Phalanx systems frequently engage UAVs, loitering munitions, and other threats while Romeo continues his immediate objectives.

The systems stop some threats, damage some, miss some, and remain unable to cover every attack. Their function is to communicate that extraordinary defensive effort is required merely to keep the installation operating.

Small and medium UAVs are the primary recurring targets. Attack helicopters may occasionally penetrate the terminal-defense envelope. Fast jets normally remain part of the wider layered air-defense and alert-fighter picture; CIWS engagement against them is exceptional rather than routine.

Presentation is irregular and spatially distributed:
- distant bursts and tracer streams;
- partial intercept flashes;
- damaged UAVs descending into other sectors;
- debris or near misses affecting the immediate route;
- separate batteries firing from different bearings;
- temporary silence caused by reload, damage, masking, or target loss.

Do not force repeated camera turns or stop gameplay to display the defensive layer.
