REGENT CACHE

The cache is on the western cape. A loose flagpole-base stone hides a weatherproof
package containing one encrypted USB drive and an unsigned handwritten note with
an unfamiliar symbol and coordinates unrelated to any Game 1 objective.

GOLDBERG SECURITY RULE

The guesthouse room controls Goldberg’s privately sourced property-security
architecture: cameras, drones, access control, automated weapons and local IFF
profiles. It is protected from PANDORA, fully under Goldberg’s control and isolated
from PANDORA, AEGIS, U.S. military systems and wider strategic networks.

The player may alter local IFF state against Graves. Direct combat remains valid.
Exact lethality, UI, network topology and balance remain open.

HELIPAD ENDING

Bishop orders Spook to stand down. Spook refuses. Bishop declares Victor Two Bravo
rogue. Goldberg and Stockbridge escape under the compromised command situation.
