WEAPON DISTINCTION

Two CheyTac M200 rifles may exist in the mission:

1. EXTREME PREJUDICE — Rip’s personal named build, recovered at his observation
   position after defeat.
2. A separate unnamed island-security M200 in Goldberg’s western temple pavilion,
   usable against Rip from the pavilion sightline.

The pavilion rifle must not inherit the EXTREME PREJUDICE name.

REGENT CACHE

The cache marker is on the western cape, not the eastern cape. A loose flagpole-base
stone hides a weatherproof package containing one encrypted USB drive and an
unsigned handwritten note with a specific symbol and coordinates not associated
with any Game 1 objective. Finding it sets the protected Game 2 hidden-quest state.

SECURITY-SYSTEM RULE

The guesthouse security room controls Goldberg’s closed local property-security
architecture: cameras, drones, access control, automated weapons and local IFF
profiles. It is not access to PANDORA, AEGIS, U.S. military systems or a broader
strategic network.

The player may use Underwood’s access card to alter the IFF state and materially
change the Graves encounter. Direct combat remains valid. Exact lethality, UI,
weapon types and balancing remain open.

PROTECTED HELIPAD ENDING

Bishop orders Spook to stand down and grant Goldberg safe passage. The protected
slip remains: “Stand down, Spook. This is bigger than Mullen told you.”

Spook refuses. Bishop declares Victor Two Bravo rogue. The compromised command
situation and helicopter departure prevent the team from stopping Goldberg and
Stockbridge. Do not describe Spook as willingly complying.
