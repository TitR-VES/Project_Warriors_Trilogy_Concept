NEW LOCK — HAWTHORNE / SANDMAN
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Stable ID: PW-WPN-HAWTHORNE-SANDMAN
Build: SANDMAN
Platform: 2019 Hudson H9, 9x19mm, striker-fired pistol
Owner: Nicholas Wayne Hawthorne / Croatoan
Campaign: War Within DLC
Role: persistent personal sidearm
Configuration: tan primary finish; black polymer grip panels; threaded barrel
Continuity origin: early Kill Devil Hills Detachment period
Status: locked core; lower-level implementation details remain open

SANDMAN fills Hawthorne's previously open sidearm slot. It is not a late New
American Frontier or Vanguard acquisition. Hawthorne has carried it since his early
Kill Devil Hills Detachment days, so the handgun should read as a trusted continuity
object linking his formative militia history to the War Within campaign.

The name is a literary nod to Logan's Run, which Hawthorne has read and considers
one of his favorite novels. The reference works through the Sandman identity and the
novel's armed protagonist; the pistol should not be decorated as a replica or fandom
prop.

The threaded barrel is part of the locked physical configuration. No permanently
fitted suppressor or other muzzle device is implied.
