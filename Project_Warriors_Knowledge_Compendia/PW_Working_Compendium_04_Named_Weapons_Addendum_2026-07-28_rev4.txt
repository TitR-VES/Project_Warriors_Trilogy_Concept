NEW LOCK — HAWTHORNE / SANDMAN
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Stable ID: PW-WPN-HAWTHORNE-SANDMAN
Build: SANDMAN
Platform: 2019 Hudson H9, 9x19mm, striker-fired pistol
Owner: Nicholas Wayne Hawthorne / Croatoan
Campaign: War Within DLC
Role: persistent personal sidearm
Base configuration: tan/FDE primary finish; black polymer grip panels; threaded barrel;
                    Streamlight TLR-3 weapon light
Suppressor configuration: removable black flat-sided just-under-seven-inch SilencerCo Osprey 45 2.0
Continuity origin: early Kill Devil Hills Detachment period
Status: locked core; lower-level production and malfunction-interaction details remain open

SANDMAN fills Hawthorne's previously open sidearm slot. It is not a late New
American Frontier or Vanguard acquisition. Hawthorne has carried it since his early
Kill Devil Hills Detachment days, so the handgun should read as a trusted continuity
object linking his formative militia history to the War Within campaign.

The name is a literary nod to Logan's Run, which Hawthorne has read and considers
one of his favorite novels. The reference works through the Sandman identity and the
novel's armed protagonist; the pistol should not be decorated as a replica or fandom
prop.

The tan/FDE finish plays deliberately against the SANDMAN name. Together with the
black polymer grips, it produces a restrained sand-and-black visual identity without
literal thematic graphics. The Hudson H9's unusual low-bore and forward-heavy shape
also carries a credible near-futuristic quality that evokes the fictional namesake
while remaining a real, grounded handgun.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
CANONICAL WEAPON LIGHT LOCK
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

SANDMAN carries a Streamlight TLR-3 as its persistent weapon light. The light body
sits distinctly low beneath the bore on the pistol's accessory rail, adding a visible
vertical layer below the Hudson H9's already unusual forward profile.

The control relationship is equally important:
- the compact light body remains visibly low below the bore
- the switch paddle sits high enough relative to the trigger guard and firing hand
  to remain naturally reachable by Hawthorne's fingers
- activation must not require an exaggerated grip shift or loss of firing control
- production should preserve practical momentary and steady activation behavior
- the light body must not be raised, enlarged or merged into the frame merely to
  simplify the animation

Exact beam tuning, battery behavior, finger choice, input mapping and activation
animation remain production details. The model, low mounting position and reachable
high paddle are locked.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
CANONICAL SUPPRESSOR / VISUAL LOCK
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

When Hawthorne suppresses SANDMAN, he fits a black, flat-sided SilencerCo Osprey
45 2.0 measuring just under seven inches. This is the build's canonical suppressor,
not a generic interchangeable cylindrical can.

The Osprey 45 2.0:
- remains removable rather than permanently fitted
- contrasts sharply with the tan/FDE pistol
- extends the Hudson H9 into SANDMAN's strongest and most distinctive silhouette
- provides exceptional shot-report dampening
- reaches its strongest stealth effect with later-game 9x19mm subsonic ammunition

The flat-sided body and elongated profile reinforce the Logan's Run visual echo
without turning the weapon into a literal replica. Unsuppressed carry remains valid
in missions and loadout states that do not require reduced signature.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
SANDMAN HANDLING / AMMUNITION LOCK
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

SANDMAN is a control-and-speed sidearm rather than a raw-damage pistol.

Unsuppressed handling identity:
- exceptionally soft recoil
- very low muzzle rise
- strong ergonomics and great general handling
- very fast ADS speed
- high aim stability
- fast, accurate follow-up shots and rapid return to the sight line

The compact TLR-3 does not replace this base handling identity. Its low body should
remain visible, but it does not create the pronounced front-balance cost associated
with the much larger suppressor. Any numerical light-equipped handling effect remains
an implementation field.

Suppressed handling identity:
- exceptional sound dampening
- recoil remains soft and muzzle rise remains controlled
- the just-under-seven-inch suppressor shifts the center of gravity forward
- ADS becomes noticeably slower than in the bare-pistol state
- repeated follow-up shots become slightly less stable because of the added front weight
- sight recovery remains good, but rapid strings require more deliberate correction

The Osprey 45 2.0 is therefore not a universal upgrade. It exchanges presentation
speed and some repeated-shot stability for exceptional signature reduction. The
penalty should be communicated through visible front-heavy handling rather than an
opaque stat adjustment.

Standard 9x19mm:
- effective against soft and lightly armored targets within intended pistol range
- common and relatively inexpensive to replenish
- less suitable against substantial armor or at extended range

Later-game 9x19mm subsonic:
- unlocked later rather than available in the opening inventory
- intended primarily for use with the Osprey 45 2.0
- produces SANDMAN's quietest suppressed state
- carries a slight damage and effective-range reduction
- remains a tactical alternative rather than a universal replacement for standard ammunition
- does not remove the suppressor's slower ADS and reduced repeated-shot stability

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
QUANTIFIED SANDMAN MALFUNCTION LOCK
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

SANDMAN's reliability identity is now defined by five explicit malfunction modes.
These rates supersede the earlier generic description that the weapon was only
slightly less reliable than a maintained M1911 or retained a low absolute malfunction
frequency. The pistol is mechanically temperamental; Hawthorne's practiced, efficient
clearing animations are what keep it usable under pressure.

All routine failures inherit the contextual reload / restore-to-ready input and the
persistent magazine, chamber, slide and feed-state system. They may interrupt or
extend an action, but they cannot permanently erase or discard SANDMAN.

1. Guide-rod walk-out / slide stick
- Cause lane: sustained rapid fire and magdump-reload-repeat behavior.
- Failure state: slide stuck rearward or half-open; weapon out of battery.
- First consecutive rapid-fire magazine: 2.5% per shot.
- Second consecutive rapid-fire magazine: 50% chance during that magazine.
- Third and later consecutive rapid-fire magazines: 96% chance per magazine.
- The 50% and 96% values are magazine-level event checks, not per-shot probabilities.
- Recovery: longer restore-to-ready animation in which Hawthorne controls and
  repositions the slide, returning the weapon to battery.
- Open: exact cadence/cooldown threshold that resets the sustained-fire chain.

2. Fresh-magazine first-round choke
- Cause lane: stiff or inconsistent magazine tolerances during a reload that must
  chamber a new first round.
- Rate: 10% per eligible reload.
- Exclusion: tactical reloads where a live round remains chambered.
- Failure state: first round does not seat fully; weapon remains out of battery.
- Recovery: short animation in which Hawthorne slightly retracts and releases the
  slide so the round loads correctly and the weapon returns to battery.

3. Stiff-magazine extraction
- Cause lane: inconsistent magazine tolerances prevent smooth removal.
- Rate: 20% per reload that includes magazine removal.
- Tactical reloads are included because they still require magazine extraction.
- Failure state: magazine resists dropping or withdrawing cleanly.
- Recovery: reload animation branches into positive manual removal before continuing.
- Chambered-round state is preserved by this event alone.

4. Slide-catch slingshot / premature slide closure
- Cause lane: slide-catch issue during a reload.
- Rate: 12.5% per eligible reload, equivalent to one in eight on average.
- Exclusion: tactical reloads where a live round remains chambered.
- Timing: may occur before or just after fresh-magazine insertion.
- Required pre-insertion branch: slide closes on an empty chamber; once the magazine
  is inserted, the player must re-initiate restore-to-ready so Hawthorne retracts and
  releases the slide to chamber the first round.
- Open: exact post-insertion consequence and recovery branch.

5. In-cycle feed choke
- Cause lane: magazine tolerance issue during firing.
- Rate: 2.5% per shot, approximately one in forty shots.
- Failure state: cartridge chokes during feeding and firing stops.
- Recovery: contextual state-dependent feed clear, shorter than the guide-rod
  walk-out branch; exact mechanical sub-state and animation duration remain open.

Probability and interaction rules:
- per-shot checks: first-magazine guide-rod walk-out and in-cycle feed choke
- per-eligible-reload checks: first-round choke and slide-catch slingshot
- per-magazine-removal check: stiff-magazine extraction
- per-sustained-fire-magazine checks: escalated guide-rod walk-out at 50% and 96%
- tactical-reload exclusions apply only where explicitly listed
- the locked base values must not be normalized downward to preserve older low-risk wording
- simultaneous reload-event priority and sequencing remain open
- additional condition, contamination, suppressor, ammunition-quality and difficulty
  modifiers remain open around the locked normal-condition base rates
- hard-disabling failures remain exceptional and story- or condition-gated

This profile makes weapon discipline mechanically important. SANDMAN remains very
fast and controllable in the player's hands, but sustained magdump behavior and
repeated reload cycling increasingly expose its mechanical weaknesses. Nick's skill
appears through confident diagnosis and differentiated recovery animations rather
than by removing the failures entirely.
