LOCK
MINERVA is now the normal player-facing, spoken, and unit-identity name for DAOG's intelligence / counterintelligence element.

The formal internal designation remains TRU/SIGHT / TRUSIGHT — Tactical Reconnaissance Unit / Signals Intelligence and Geospatial Heuristics Team.

USE
- MINERVA: dialogue, allied references, mission briefings, concise player-facing unit labels, and ordinary DAOG shorthand.
- TRU/SIGHT / TRUSIGHT: formal records, classified documents, technical systems, intelligence products, administrative references, and contexts where the formal organization name matters.
- MINERVA Detachment: acceptable expanded form when disambiguation is needed.

CONTINUITY
- Jaeger / Karl Alexander Stanzler remains assigned to this organization.
- Existing TRU/SIGHT references remain valid and should not be globally replaced.
- PYTHIA remains retired.
- ORACLE remains Lt. Tara Halliday's callsign.

MYTHOLOGY NOTE
Minerva is Roman; Athena is the Greek counterpart. MINERVA is intentional because DAOG's naming family is mythological rather than strictly Greek-only.
