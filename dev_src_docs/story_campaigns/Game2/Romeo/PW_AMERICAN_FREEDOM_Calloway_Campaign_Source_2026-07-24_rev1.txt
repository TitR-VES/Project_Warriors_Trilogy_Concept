CAMPAIGN THESIS
Romeo reaches an occupied American community after the Game 1 collapse and becomes responsible for turning local survival networks into a resistance capable of coordinated action. The story is not merely about retaking ground. It is about whether a force built under occupation can remain answerable to the people whose lives make it possible.

The campaign should feel:
- local before national;
- handmade before formal;
- civic as well as military;
- defined by routes, trust, medicine, food, records, radio discipline, and accumulated consequences;
- increasingly visible as the American Freedom Corps forms.

ACT I — GOOD MORNING, AMERICA
The first five missions are the strongest recovered ordered sequence and should be treated as locked working order unless a later dedicated campaign source changes it.

01. LOW GROUND
Purpose:
- establish occupied Calloway through reconnaissance rather than immediate assault;
- introduce Cody, Gene Marlow, Glushkin, and the occupation’s surveillance footprint.

Playable shape:
- pre-dawn movement with Romeo and Cody;
- water-tower or elevated observation;
- identify BTR/garrison positions, patrol staggering, the agricultural-office signals vehicle, and covered Redbud detainee vehicles at the medical-clinic parking structure;
- observe Glushkin’s unmarked officer presence during Marlow’s checkpoint interrogation;

02. DEAD AIR
Purpose:
Create a temporary communications window without announcing a full resistance offensive.

Player choice:
- disable the signals vehicle so the failure reads as equipment trouble; or
- remove a crew member during rotation, causing a shorter disruption window and higher suspicion.

Result:
Romeo reaches command and learns Calloway is a priority insurgency-support zone. A pre-positioned weapons cache exists southeast of town. Calloway is not meant to remain an isolated cell; it must become the first node of a network.

03. THE REDBUD VEHICLES
- Romeo and Cody reconnoiter the medical-clinic parking structure;
- fourteen or more civilian detainees are held behind temporary barriers;
- Cody recognizes Kelsey Pruett and restrains himself rather than compromising the operation;
- the mission ends on the knowledge that the cell cannot wait indefinitely.

04. FARM ROAD
- establish contact with Gene Marlow at the grain-elevator pull-off;
- reach and recover the Henderson-farm cache;
- move weapons, medical supplies, night-vision devices, and encrypted radios through the checkpoint in Marlow’s truck;
- preserve the handwritten note: “For whoever finds this — you’re not alone. Get to work.”
