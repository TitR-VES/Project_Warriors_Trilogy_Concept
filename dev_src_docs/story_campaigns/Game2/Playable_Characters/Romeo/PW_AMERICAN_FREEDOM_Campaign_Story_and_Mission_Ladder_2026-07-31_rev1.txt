CAMPAIGN THESIS
Romeo moves from displaced soldier to accountable resistance organizer. The campaign expands by causality rather than by arbitrary scale:

Calloway survival
-> local resistance
-> multi-community network
-> American Freedom Corps
-> public legitimacy crisis
-> cooperation with surviving conventional forces
-> local liberation
-> defeat of Glushkin’s counter-network
-> preservation and liberation of the Cumberland Gap corridor
-> a credible northeastward bridge into NO KINGS.

The campaign remains geographically concentrated in Kentucky and the adjoining Appalachian tri-state region. It does not depict Romeo personally leading a continent-spanning national counteroffensive in Game 2. The movement and corridor outgrow the local campaign and create the conditions for his Game 3 story.
