Protected geographic distinction:

CAMPAIGN FUNCTION
The battle completes the campaign scale progression:
Calloway survival -> resistance network -> AFC -> public legitimacy conflict -> conventional-force partnership -> strategically meaningful corridor liberation.

The AFC contributes local intelligence, civilian routes, checkpoint knowledge, concealed approaches, communications, and distributed cells. Forward U.S. Army elements contribute engineers, heavier weapons, aviation, casualty evacuation, and the force required to hold and exploit the corridor. Romeo is the bridge between the two structures; the Army does not arrive afterward to erase or replace the AFC.

SECOND TIGR — CONTROLLED CAUSALITY
- The driver reacts late, accelerates, and pulls hard toward the right shoulder.
- The evasive maneuver avoids a direct hit.
- The second rocket impacts the road surface immediately behind and to the left of the Tigr.
- The detonation occurs approximately one foot from the rear-left wheel area.
- The rollover is caused by multiple interacting factors, not blast pressure alone.

Protected destabilization chain:
1. Near-blast pressure and ejected road-surface debris strike the rear-left portion of the moving vehicle.
2. The rear wheels briefly unweight and lose effective traction.
3. The vehicle is already under aggressive steering input toward the right shoulder.
4. The rear becomes light and unstable while the heavy front pitches downward.
5. When the rear returns to the road, the Tigr is no longer stable in its steering line.
6. It slews or crab-slides sideways toward the shoulder.
7. The right-side tires hit the road-edge transition and uneven shoulder geometry.
8. The bump and slight slope beyond the shoulder trip the vehicle into a rollover.
9. The Tigr tumbles into the ditch and comes to rest on its right side.

The right-side resting position is the controlling spatial outcome.
