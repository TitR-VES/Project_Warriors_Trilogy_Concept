CAMPAIGN OVERVIEW
===============================================================================

Persistent Threat follows Sergeant First Class Ferris “Ducky” Ryan and AEGIS
Squad after the immediate PANDORA crisis has become a wider European war.
Ducky continues to lead Finnish sniper and arctic-warfare specialist Aki
“Frost” Peltola, Romanian assault specialist Ştefan “Lupin” Dragavei, and
Australian combat medic Cassandra “Switchblade” Stewart. Analyst Tara Halliday
serves as the campaign's principal institutional counterpart, preserving
anomalies and documentary links that other officials repeatedly prefer to treat
as unrelated incidents.

At the start of the campaign, AEGIS still understands its mission primarily as
the defense of European military and civil infrastructure against direct Russian
attack. That interpretation becomes progressively less adequate. The enemy is
not only striking protected sites from outside. Russian technical and intelligence
elements possess internal AEGIS parameters, use compromised credentials, and
conceal relay, certification, deception, and access systems inside the normal
commercial and administrative mechanisms through which Europe already functions.

The Torrejón attack broadens the threat further. Unmarked Chinese operators exploit
the same weakened security environment to attack the AEGIS satellite-command
layer and later target Europe's semiconductor-recovery capacity. The campaign
does not establish that every Russian and Chinese action belongs to one unified
command. Instead, it presents PANDORA's institutional damage as a strategic opening
that multiple adversaries can exploit through parallel operations.

The narrative therefore develops as both a military campaign and an investigation.
Each major discovery changes what AEGIS believes it is fighting:

- FAILURE CASCADE and DEAD FREQUENCY establish that the system is failing in ways
  the conventional war alone cannot explain.
- ORBITAL DECAY, SPLASHDOWN, PROCESS WINDOW, and HARD WINTER show that compromised
  access has become a multinational campaign against the physical and industrial
  foundations of European recovery.
- DEAD GROUND, DARK FIBER, and ASH RELAY convert Halliday's theory into physical
  proof of a repeatable relay-and-deception architecture.
- OPEN CIRCUIT and PERMAFROST force AEGIS to act on that proof before the northern
  strike package can be completed.
- HULL BREACH exposes a senior allied credential inside the hostile architecture.
- AEGIS FALLS turns the investigation inward and forces Ducky to decide whether
  protecting AEGIS still means protecting the institution that carries its name.

The campaign's central dramatic movement is therefore not simply from defense to
offense. It is from faith in a system, through proof that the system has been
penetrated, to the conclusion that the people and purpose behind AEGIS may have
to survive the institution itself.

MISSION-BY-MISSION PLOT
===============================================================================

-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. FAILURE CASCADE
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Persistent Threat opens on the Norwegian coast during a Russian naval-infantry
landing. AEGIS is caught inside a rapidly deteriorating defensive picture in
which military communications, coastal movement, civil evacuation, and friendly
coordination are all failing at once. The mission was previously developed under
the shorthand COLD OPEN, but FAILURE CASCADE is the controlling title.

Ducky leads AEGIS through the withdrawal while Norwegian coastal defenders attempt
to keep a route open for surviving personnel and civilians. The squad's immediate
problem is conventional and physical: hostile troops are landing, local positions
are being overrun, and the extraction corridor will close if the defenders cannot
delay the advance. The larger pattern, however, is already visible in the way
separate failures compound one another. Damaged infrastructure produces movement
delays; movement delays expose civilians; communications gaps create duplicated or
contradictory tasking; and the enemy exploits every new weakness before anyone can
restore a complete picture.

Switchblade takes a controlled risk to rescue an elderly civilian during the
withdrawal. The action establishes the squad's moral register without turning the
rescue into sentimentality. AEGIS succeeds in reaching extraction, but the mission
does not end on relief. Walker's message after exfiltration is brief: “Good exfil.
We have a problem.”

The line converts the opening from a self-contained battlefield escape into the
beginning of the campaign's investigation. The Norwegian action proves that AEGIS
can still function tactically. Walker's warning implies that the institution's
larger operational picture may already be compromised.

[DEVELOPMENT GAP — OPENING HANDOFF]
The exact anomaly Walker has received, the timing of Halliday's first analytical
intervention, and the operational reason AEGIS is sent from Norway to the converted
facility in northern Estonia remain insufficiently explicit in the current
synopsis-level source set. The handoff needs one concrete piece of evidence or one
failed defensive event that makes DEAD FREQUENCY feel like the necessary next move
rather than a new assignment.

-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
2. DEAD FREQUENCY
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------

AEGIS deploys to a converted NATO facility in northern Estonia. The mission's
primary tactical objective remains controlled by its dedicated source, but its
campaign function is clear: Ducky's team enters a site whose communications and
technical behavior suggest that hostile forces know more about AEGIS than an
external attacker should.

After completing the main objective, the squad begins a pressured withdrawal.
Russian pursuit elements close on the facility while Swedish Archer artillery,
operating under the ARTEMIS callsign, strikes the approach routes behind AEGIS.
The artillery support is not presented as a detached spectacle. It creates the
brief intervals in which the squad can continue breaking contact.

During the withdrawal, Lupin photographs technical parameters that use internal
AEGIS nomenclature and configuration language. The material is too precise to be
a generic intelligence estimate or battlefield improvisation. Russian personnel
possess information that should have remained inside protected development,
certification, or command channels.

The discovery becomes Investigative Beat 1, “The Parameters.” Halliday begins
treating the Norwegian failures and the Estonian evidence as parts of a wider
pattern. Ducky's problem is no longer only that enemy forces are attacking AEGIS.
Someone has given them a map of how the system thinks.

[DEVELOPMENT GAP — MISSION PREMISE DETAIL]
DEAD FREQUENCY has a strong ending and investigative payoff, but its primary
objective and the specific relationship between that objective and the recovered
parameters need to remain legible in the final mission synopsis. Without that
connection, the mission risks feeling as though its most important evidence is
found incidentally during an otherwise unrelated operation.

-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
3. ORBITAL DECAY
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------

The investigation is interrupted by a direct attack on the AEGIS orbital layer.
Unmarked Chinese operators penetrate the NATO and AEGIS satellite-command facility
at Torrejón Air Base near Madrid. Their objective is not to hold the base. They use
compromised access and a prepared sequence to force satellites out of orbit before
the defenders can determine how the protected site was penetrated.

Ducky and AEGIS fight alongside local Spanish and allied personnel to regain the
command spaces, preserve authentication and telemetry evidence, and interrupt the
deorbit procedure. The mission broadens the campaign's attribution picture. The
Russian possession of internal parameters remains central, but Torrejón proves that
the compromised architecture is not useful only to Russia and that the official
war map does not explain every hostile operation.

AEGIS stops the full sequence, preserving most of the constellation. One satellite,
however, has already passed the point at which control can be restored. Its descent
track carries it toward the Julian Alps in Slovenia.

The mission ends with a partial success that immediately creates the next objective.
The loss is limited, but the falling satellite turns an orbital-security breach into
a physical recovery race. ORBITAL DECAY and SPLASHDOWN therefore function as one
continuous cause-and-effect block rather than two independent episodes.

-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
4. SPLASHDOWN
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------

AEGIS deploys into the Julian Alps with Slovenian special-operations, civil-defense,
resistance, and technical-recovery partners. The satellite has broken apart across
difficult mountain terrain, scattering components and security-sensitive wreckage
through an Alpine debris corridor before hostile teams can be fully identified.

The mission is not an attempt to “save” the destroyed satellite. Ducky's force must
locate the decisive surviving sections, prevent hostile recovery of authentication,
telemetry, storage, and architectural material, and preserve enough evidence to
reconstruct who initiated the deorbit and who expected to exploit the wreckage.

Local knowledge is indispensable. Slovenian partners understand the roads, slopes,
weather, debris movement, and civilian-access problem in ways AEGIS does not. Ducky
provides the playable tactical spine and the technical connection to Torrejón, but
the operation succeeds because the local force can turn an uncontrolled impact zone
into a recoverable search-and-denial problem.

The allies secure the most important surviving material before the hostile recovery
teams can remove it. The mission preserves evidence and limits the compromise, but
it does not undo the loss in orbit. Analysis of the recovered package reveals
references to Dutch contractor credentials, precision-optics movements, and a narrow
transfer window at a high-value technology complex in Veldhoven.

That lead carries the campaign directly into PROCESS WINDOW. It also changes the
meaning of the Torrejón attack. The deorbit was not only an attempt to damage AEGIS.
The wreckage and associated target package indicate a broader campaign against the
industrial capacity required to rebuild compromised systems.

[DEVELOPMENT GAP — EVIDENCE SPECIFICITY]
The handoff is logically sound at campaign level, but the final source should define
one memorable, reproducible evidence item linking the satellite package to Veldhoven.
A contractor certificate, movement schedule, serialized transport identifier, or
partial target matrix would allow the player to understand the connection without
depending entirely on a post-mission briefing.

-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
5. PROCESS WINDOW
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------

PROCESS WINDOW, currently a working title, takes place at a fictionalized
ASML-associated campus and adjacent integration and logistics complex in Veldhoven.
Taiwan's annexation and the wider wartime semiconductor disruption have made the
remaining European lithography, calibration, qualification, tooling, and engineering
capacity strategically critical to the recovery of AEGIS and other Western defense
systems.

The protected target is not one magical machine. It is a combined recovery path:
a critical exposure-system module, precision optics, calibration and process-control
data, specialist tooling, contamination-control infrastructure, and a small group of
engineers capable of restoring and certifying the line. The attackers need not occupy
the campus. They need only destroy or separate enough of those interdependent parts
to make recovery nonviable.

A deniable Chinese-directed technical-action cell penetrates the service side using
compromised contractor access, locally sourced vehicles, commercial drones, prepared
maintenance credentials, and a small number of disciplined assault personnel. Dutch
defenders already control the public approaches and are moving staff and technical
packages before AEGIS arrives. Ducky's squad reinforces a local operation rather than
replacing it.

The mission's broad established flow carries AEGIS through service roads, loading
courts, utility spaces, transfer areas, and controlled technical corridors while the
continuity team attempts to move the irreplaceable combination out of the threatened
campus. The attackers use the environment's own safety, access, contamination, and
fire-control systems to make the technical recovery fail even if they cannot win a
conventional firefight.

When hostile overwatch closes the protected convoy route, Ducky tasks Lupin to an
elevated blocking position. Control shifts briefly to Lupin for a fast, pressurized
rooftop and service-structure engagement. His section emphasizes moving threats,
shorter designated-marksman ranges, rapid prioritization, and visible protection of
the convoy below rather than Frost's slower precision grammar. Once the route is
reopened, control returns to Ducky.

AEGIS, Dutch personnel, engineers, and drivers preserve the critical module,
qualification archive, and essential technical team. The attackers nevertheless
destroy secondary tooling, contaminate part of the environment, damage support
infrastructure, and further disrupt the service chain. The result is a costly partial
success. Europe retains a viable recovery path, but the semiconductor bottleneck
survives into later installments.

Recovered routing and credential material points toward a mobile electronic-warfare
and logistics architecture moving through Latvia. Halliday uses that lead to task
HARD WINTER. PROCESS WINDOW therefore supplies the target and movement lead, but
does not replace the next mission's investigative discovery.

[DEVELOPMENT GAP — FULL MISSION DESIGN]
PROCESS WINDOW is not production-ready. Its placement, strategic function, partial
success, and broad Lupin transition are established. The exact protected package,
Dutch cast, attacker composition, objective order, campus route, technical
interactions, dialogue, cinematics, failure logic, and precise rooftop staging
remain provisional.

[DEVELOPMENT GAP — LATVIA TRACE]
The campaign needs an exact surviving trace that links the Veldhoven cell to the
mobile convoy. The evidence should be operational rather than purely financial:
for example, a routing token, maintenance credential family, equipment serial block,
vehicle-support schedule, or encrypted movement reference that Halliday can use to
locate a physical target.

-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
6. HARD WINTER
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------

AEGIS tracks the mobile electronic-warfare convoy across the Latvian interior over
two in-game days. The operation moves the campaign away from fixed high-value sites
and into a mobile target problem. The hostile architecture can no longer be treated
as a collection of infiltrated facilities. It has logistics, protection, movement,
and technical continuity of its own.

Ducky's team follows the convoy through winter terrain while hostile screening
elements attempt to separate surveillance from engagement. The long pursuit gives
Halliday time to compare the convoy's equipment, contractor relationships, and
movement records against the evidence accumulated since Estonia.

The mission culminates in the recovery of material that reveals Investigative Beat 2,
“The Architecture.” Halliday traces AEGIS development contracts and associated access
through Nexus Meridian Holdings and financial structures connected to the wider
KINGMAKER network already visible in the Montoya Farm records. The compromise is not
the work of one bribed technician or one stolen file. It sits inside a durable web of
contractors, credentials, financial intermediaries, and technical services.

The recovered architecture also identifies Bakhmut as the location of a protected
forward site where the hostile system is being tested and certified. AEGIS is not
being sent into the ruined city simply to destroy another relay. It is going there
to determine what the architecture is designed to do.

[DEVELOPMENT GAP — BAKHMUT TASKING]
The final HARD WINTER ending should make the Bakhmut lead concrete. The campaign
needs a specific site identifier, service dependency, test schedule, or certification
reference that explains why the ruined municipal complex is the next indispensable
target and why the opportunity cannot be deferred.

-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
7. DEAD GROUND
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------

AEGIS enters Bakhmut and links up with a Ukrainian veteran cell led by Captain
Oleksandr Honcharenko. His team includes signals and electronic-warfare specialist
Kateryna Melnyk, reconnaissance and UAS NCO Mykhailo Vorobei, engineer and breacher
Taras Savchuk, and medic Olena Kysil.

The city is presented as watched geometry rather than a generic ruined combat
sandbox. Exposed concrete frames, broken balcony grids, damaged courtyards, dirty
snow, service passages, and long sight lines determine who can move, who can observe,
and which structures can still carry technical infrastructure.

Ducky and Honcharenko begin by establishing the route and testing the enemy's outer
awareness. The Russian-led detachment is protecting controllers, observers, drone
spotters, and technical access rather than merely holding symbolic ground. AEGIS
recognizes that the municipal communications-and-utilities complex has been shaped
to conceal a more important function.

Frost becomes temporarily playable to remove overwatch and controller positions whose
survival would expose the joint force before it can reach the site's dependencies.
His section is deliberate and methodical. The player reads range, stability, route
timing, and the consequence of each shot before control returns to Ducky.

By the end of the mission, the combined force has not yet reached the inner relay
architecture. It has learned how the site watches the city and has created a viable
approach for the shaping operation in DARK FIBER.

-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
8. DARK FIBER
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------

The joint force attacks an outer dependency supporting the Bakhmut site and exploits
buried communications, cable routes, service infrastructure, and timing systems. The
mission is designed to strip away one layer of the target without prematurely forcing
the decisive assault.

Ducky continues to command the larger tactical problem while Melnyk and the Ukrainian
cell interpret the technical infrastructure. The enemy reacts by moving a local
interdiction element toward the approach corridor.

Control shifts temporarily to Lupin. Unlike Frost's previous section, Lupin's
engagement is closer, faster, and organized around breaking movement before it
reaches the squad. He uses aggressive designated-marksman fire and rapid repositioning
to hold the approach while Ducky's element completes the exploitation.

The recovered timing, relay, and certification material establishes that the target
is not a local communications node. It is a forward certification facility used to
validate attack logic, rehearse a relay chain under contested-spectrum conditions,
and tune a deception envelope against AEGIS-shaped defensive interpretation.

The discovery confirms Halliday's architecture theory but still leaves the decisive
technical packet inside the municipal complex. DARK FIBER therefore ends by turning
ASH RELAY from a destruction mission into a controlled seizure-and-denial operation:
the relay must be dismantled in the correct order so that enough evidence survives
to identify what the certified strike package is intended to attack.

-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
9. ASH RELAY
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------

AEGIS and the Ukrainian cell launch the decisive assault on the municipal
communications-and-utilities complex. Ducky remains the sole playable perspective
through the conclusion of the Bakhmut arc, restoring the campaign's command viewpoint
after the Frost and Lupin transitions.

The target combines a ruined civic shell with an attached industrial service wing.
The inner technical cell attempts to preserve or erase different parts of the relay
architecture while the outer screen closes on the site. Destroying the wrong element
too early would either allow the network to route around the loss or erase the
evidence required to understand the wider operation.

Ducky directs the team through the required denial sequence. AEGIS secures the inner
spaces, the Ukrainian cell controls routes and structural access, and the surviving
technical packet is removed before the reaction force can seal the area.

The recovered material is the campaign's first complete physical proof of a repeatable
relay-and-deception kill chain built with compromised AEGIS-adjacent knowledge. It
shows how hostile operators can certify a strike package, move it through contested
communications, and cause AEGIS-shaped defensive systems to misread or delay their
response.

The packet identifies Svalbard as the probable northern target. The discovery pays
off the campaign's investigative escalation: the parameters found in Estonia have
become a tested architecture with a named objective.

Before AEGIS can deploy north, however, Halliday identifies an active continental
node in Brno that is connected to the same movement and routing chain. This creates
the immediate OPEN CIRCUIT tasking.

[DEVELOPMENT GAP — BRNO NECESSITY]
This is the most important unresolved middle-to-late campaign handoff. ASH RELAY
already identifies Svalbard, so OPEN CIRCUIT can read as a detour unless Brno
contains something AEGIS must obtain or deny before the northern operation can
succeed. The final bridge should establish that Brno holds a live routing core,
activation timetable, current credential set, target-confirmation channel, or other
time-sensitive element that cannot be bypassed merely by deploying directly to
Svalbard.

-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
10. OPEN CIRCUIT
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------

OPEN CIRCUIT moves the technical war from Bakhmut's openly ruined environment into
the service side of the Brno Exhibition Centre. The district initially appears to
be operating under partial civilian normality: closed tram stops, contractor
vehicles, intermittent work lights, stacked event equipment, loading access, plant
rooms, freight doors, and temporary installations.

AEGIS enters with SHIELD, DAOG's sensitive-site exploitation and recovery squad.
The obvious technical floor contains staged clutter. Ducky notices that the service
route is protected more heavily than the visible hardware and concludes that the
public-facing site is a decoy.

The force follows the protected service artery into a repurposed hall and conference
interface containing portable racks, cooling, hardened power, fiber, cased
components, workstations, and prepared wipe procedures. AEGIS secures the perimeter
while SHIELD images the floor, classifies the hardware, and removes the routing core.

The seizure triggers a hard lockdown. Shutters close, power and lighting states
change, vehicle barriers rise, and deniable contractor-cover security gives way to
a more overt military reaction. The building separates AEGIS and SHIELD onto
parallel eastward routes while the package must continue moving.

Both elements are pinned near the Velodrom-facing service-road choke by vehicles,
barriers, and elevated firing positions. Ducky confirms the friendly geometry and
calls French Rafale support under the KERAUNOS callsign. The precision strike breaks
the lead barrier mass and strips the upper firing line without destroying the wider
district.

Ducky does not pause to watch the aircraft. AEGIS moves through the breach, recombines
with SHIELD, confirms that the routing core remains intact, and escapes before the
second containment ring closes.

The mission proves that the architecture discovered in Bakhmut is embedded inside
ordinary European commercial movement, contractor access, temporary power, cooling,
freight, and service systems. Its plot function should also be operational: the
routing core must make the Svalbard deployment possible, more accurate, or less
vulnerable than it would have been after ASH RELAY alone.

[DEVELOPMENT GAP — OPEN CIRCUIT TO PERMAFROST]
The source set has locked the order but not the exact payoff. The final version
should show what SHIELD extracts from the core and how it changes the northern
mission. That information could confirm the attack window, expose the current relay
route, provide a credential or counter-authentication method, reveal the insertion
vector, or prevent the enemy from remotely retasking the package. The choice remains
open, but the result must be visible in PERMAFROST.

-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
11. PERMAFROST
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------

AEGIS returns to Svalbard, where the northern strike package threatens the
infrastructure that survived the first game's opening crisis. The location gives
the campaign a deliberate circularity. AEGIS is no longer defending the site from
an isolated attack. Ducky now understands the relay, certification, credential, and
deception architecture assembled to make the strike succeed.

The exact entry route and the full benefit gained from OPEN CIRCUIT remain
production-working. At campaign level, the mission is the payoff to the Bakhmut
evidence and the final continental exploitation. AEGIS reaches the relevant node,
disrupts the attack sequence, and prevents the northern operation from achieving
its intended strategic effect.

During the withdrawal, Lupin is struck by a shoulder fragment. The injury is not
discarded at the end of the mission. Switchblade stabilizes him while the team
continues moving toward the coast.

Norwegian K239 Chunmoo rocket artillery under the TYPHON callsign collapses inland
pursuit routes and creates a narrow coastal extraction window. AEGIS reaches a
rigid-inflatable craft under fire and escapes before the enemy can reclose the route.

The mission appears to stop the certified northern strike package. It also produces
the next intelligence opportunity. Surviving material, enemy reaction, or the route
used to support the operation points AEGIS toward the Russian intelligence vessel
Akademik Volkov in Kaliningrad harbor.

[DEVELOPMENT GAP — KALININGRAD LEAD]
The PERMAFROST-to-HULL BREACH transition needs a specific causal link. The Volkov
should not appear as an unrelated intelligence target after the Svalbard climax.
The campaign needs one identifiable reason the ship matters now: it may be the
credential repository, command vessel, exploitation archive, personnel-evacuation
platform, or surviving control node associated with the failed northern operation.

-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
12. HULL BREACH
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------

AEGIS joins Nereid Black for a maritime intelligence operation in Kaliningrad harbor.
The force infiltrates aboard the ballistic-missile submarine USS District of Columbia
and assaults the Russian intelligence vessel Akademik Volkov.

The mission extends the campaign's infrastructure theme into a mobile naval archive.
AEGIS has destroyed field nodes and recovered local packets, but the Volkov holds
the higher-level records required to identify who inside the allied command structure
made those operations possible.

Lupin participates in the exploitation despite the shoulder wound sustained during
PERMAFROST. His condition is visible in movement, handling, and dialogue rather than
reset between missions. The continuity gives the late campaign a physical cost and
prevents the Svalbard escape from becoming consequence-free.

The recovered drive contains Lieutenant General Callum Tremblay's ZEUS-level
credential identifier and biometric profile. The result becomes Investigative Beat 3,
“The Credential.” It does not merely suggest that a senior official was negligent.
A credential at Tremblay's level appears inside the hostile architecture the team
has spent the campaign reconstructing.

At 0200 after the mission, Ducky, Walker, and Halliday confront the implication.
This is Investigative Beat 4, “What Ducky Does With It.” The team finally has enough
to move from analysis to action, but no fully trustworthy institutional path remains
through which to act. Walker authorizes an unauthorized confrontation and tasks
Ducky toward the campaign finale.

The HULL BREACH-to-AEGIS FALLS handoff is one of the campaign's strongest existing
transitions. The investigation has reached a person with authority over the system,
and the next mission is a direct consequence of evidence the player helped recover.

-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
13. AEGIS FALLS
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------

AEGIS FALLS is a compound final operation rather than two separate missions. Its
first phase preserves the existing Russian undersea-threat concept against France.
Its final movement carries Ducky and AEGIS to a GenCorp-connected estate near
Aix-en-Provence for the confrontation with Tremblay.

The operational phase forces the squad to act before the undersea threat can be used
against the French coast. The exact platform, route, staging, and technical
relationship between the device and Tremblay's credential remain production-working.
At plot level, the threat must function as the final live consequence of the
architecture AEGIS has uncovered: compromised authority has enabled a strategic
attack that cannot be stopped through ordinary command channels.

After preventing the attack, Ducky continues to Aix-en-Provence rather than returning
to debrief. The team reaches Tremblay outside the authorization structure he helped
control. The confrontation resolves the ambiguity surrounding his decisions instead
of carrying it into the next installment.

Tremblay argues that the institution cannot survive what Ducky has uncovered and
that removing him will not repair the strategic system already collapsing around
them. His protected final assertion is: “AEGIS falls regardless.”

Ducky answers: “Then we build something better.”

The exchange completes the campaign's thematic arc. At the beginning, Ducky fights
to preserve AEGIS as the system holding Europe's defensive picture together. By the
end, he distinguishes the purpose of AEGIS from the institution that has failed it.
The squad, Halliday's record, Walker's decision, local allies across Europe, and the
evidence they preserved represent the part worth carrying forward.

[DEVELOPMENT GAP — COMPOUND FINALE CAUSALITY]
The finale will only feel like one mission if the undersea-threat phase directly
produces the Aix confrontation. The operational phase should expose Tremblay's active
authorization, current location, escape attempt, command intervention, or personal
control over the threat. A purely geographic transition from stopping the device to
raiding an estate would make the finale feel like two missions joined for ladder
economy rather than one escalating resolution.

[DEVELOPMENT GAP — TREMBLAY CONSEQUENCE STATE]
The protected line pair resolves Ducky's thematic position, but the exact physical,
legal, and institutional consequence for Tremblay should be stated in the final
mission source. Capture, death, exposure, disappearance, or another state will carry
different implications into Game 3 and should not remain accidentally ambiguous.

COHESION TEST
===============================================================================

OVERALL RESULT

The current PERSISTENT THREAT ladder is coherent at campaign-spine level.

The thirteen missions form a readable escalation:
battlefield failure -> internal parameters -> orbital compromise -> physical
recovery -> industrial sabotage -> mobile architecture -> certification site ->
live continental node -> northern strike -> senior credential -> institutional
confrontation.

The campaign no longer reads as a loose anthology of European special-operations
missions. The investigative beats, the Bakhmut-to-Svalbard chain, the Lupin injury
continuity, and the HULL BREACH-to-finale decision create a defensible beginning,
middle, and end.

It is not yet fully production-cohesive. Several transitions are currently logical
in summary but not yet dramatized through a specific piece of evidence, decision,
or consequence. The greatest risk is not contradiction. It is that missions whose
placement is correct may still feel episodic because the player has not been shown
why the next operation must happen now.

-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
WHAT ALREADY WORKS
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------

1. THE INVESTIGATION ESCALATES THROUGH PLAYABLE CONSEQUENCES

The campaign's four investigative beats do not exist only in briefings. Lupin
photographs the parameters during DEAD FREQUENCY; HARD WINTER exposes the contractor
architecture; HULL BREACH produces Tremblay's credential; and the 0200 conversation
forces Ducky to choose what to do with it. This is a strong structural spine.

2. ORBITAL DECAY AND SPLASHDOWN FORM A CLEAN CAUSAL PAIR

The forced deorbit creates the mountain-recovery mission directly. The player sees
the consequence of the failed defense rather than receiving it as exposition.

3. PROCESS WINDOW BROADENS THE WAR WITHOUT SOLVING IT TOO EASILY

The partial success protects a recovery path while preserving the semiconductor
constraint. Its people-data-tooling-infrastructure model also supports the campaign's
theme that systems are human and material networks, not abstract machines.

4. THE BAKHMUT MINI-ARC HAS INTERNAL SHAPE

DEAD GROUND reads the site, DARK FIBER strips a dependency and identifies its
function, and ASH RELAY performs the decisive seizure and denial. Frost, Lupin, and
Ducky each receive a distinct playable role without displacing Ducky as campaign
lead.

5. OPEN CIRCUIT EXTENDS THE CAMPAIGN THESIS

Brno demonstrates that the same hostile architecture can exist inside ordinary
commercial systems. The change from Bakhmut's visible destruction to Brno's
back-of-house normality is thematically productive rather than repetitive.

6. PERMAFROST, HULL BREACH, AND AEGIS FALLS CARRY CONSEQUENCE FORWARD

Lupin's wound persists into HULL BREACH. The Volkov drive produces the credential.
The credential produces the unauthorized decision. The decision produces the
Tremblay confrontation. This late chain is substantially stronger than an ending
based only on stopping a strategic weapon.

7. THE ENDING COMPLETES DUCKY'S INSTITUTIONAL ARC

“Then we build something better” answers the campaign's central question. Ducky
does not abandon collective defense. He rejects the idea that loyalty to collective
defense requires loyalty to a compromised institutional shell.

-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
LOAD-BEARING GAPS
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------

A. FAILURE CASCADE -> DEAD FREQUENCY

The opening needs one concrete anomaly that turns Walker's warning into the Estonia
tasking.

B. SPLASHDOWN -> PROCESS WINDOW -> HARD WINTER

The sequence is strategically logical, but each handoff needs a visible evidence
object or operational trace. Otherwise the campaign may appear to jump from satellite
wreckage to a Dutch campus and then to a Latvian convoy through analyst exposition.

C. HARD WINTER -> BAKHMUT

The player needs to understand why Bakhmut contains the architecture's certification
site and why the window to reach it is immediate.

D. ASH RELAY -> OPEN CIRCUIT -> PERMAFROST

This is the highest-priority cohesion problem. Because ASH RELAY already identifies
Svalbard, Brno must be indispensable rather than merely informative. OPEN CIRCUIT
should remove a live capability, recover a current key, or prevent an activation
that would otherwise compromise the Svalbard operation.

E. PERMAFROST -> HULL BREACH

The Akademik Volkov requires a direct relationship to the failed northern operation.

F. AEGIS FALLS INTERNAL STRUCTURE

The French undersea phase and Aix confrontation must share one causal chain, one
escalating time pressure, and one antagonist decision. The first phase should expose
or force the second.

-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
CHARACTER AND THEMATIC RISKS
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------

1. DUCKY'S ARC IS PRESENT BUT TOO CONCENTRATED AT THE END

The institutional theme is strong in concept, but Ducky's visible internal movement
currently depends heavily on HULL BREACH and AEGIS FALLS. Earlier quiet beats should
show his changing relationship to Walker, Halliday, command authority, and the name
AEGIS itself.

A useful non-canon development target would be four recurring decision points:
- after DEAD FREQUENCY, Ducky accepts that the leak is internal;
- after HARD WINTER, he accepts that the compromise is architectural rather than local;
- after ASH RELAY or OPEN CIRCUIT, he begins acting faster than the institution can
  authorize;
- after HULL BREACH, he accepts that preserving the mission may require breaking the
  chain of command.

2. THE CHINESE OPERATIONAL LAYER MAY FEEL ABANDONED

ORBITAL DECAY and PROCESS WINDOW establish an important parallel threat, after which
the campaign returns primarily to Russian architecture. A later acknowledgment should
clarify that China exploited the same damaged ecosystem without requiring a unified
Russian-Chinese command structure. The campaign does not necessarily need a separate
Chinese finale, but it should not make those missions feel narratively disposable.

3. THE CROSS-CAMPAIGN CRODYN SYNCHRONIZATION IS NOT YET DRAMATIZED HERE

Crodyn's wartime address is the principal synchronization point across Game 2, but
the current PERSISTENT THREAT ladder does not yet have a clearly locked mission slot
or reaction beat for it. The campaign should establish when AEGIS hears the address,
what operational phase they are in, and how Ducky interprets a government publicly
defining independent resistance and disclosure as threats.

4. FROST, SWITCHBLADE, AND HALLIDAY NEED CONTINUOUS PRESENCE BETWEEN SET PIECES

Frost and Lupin have clear gameplay identities, and Switchblade has a strong opening
moral beat and late medical function. Halliday owns the investigation. The final
synopsis should ensure that all three squadmates and Halliday remain active in the
connective scenes so the campaign does not become Ducky moving between technical
briefings and guest-force missions.

5. LOCAL ALLIES MUST REMAIN PART OF THE STORY'S ARGUMENT

Norwegian, Estonian, Spanish, Slovenian, Dutch, Latvian, Ukrainian, Czech, French,
Nereid Black, and SHIELD personnel should not function as interchangeable support.
The campaign's collective-will theme depends on each location showing that AEGIS
succeeds because local actors possess authority, knowledge, infrastructure, and
costs of their own.

===============================================================================
RECOMMENDED NEXT DEVELOPMENT ORDER
===============================================================================

The synopsis suggests the following sequence for future work:

1. Build a one-page evidence-and-causality chain covering every mission handoff.
   Each arrow should identify:
   - what is recovered or observed;
   - who interprets it;
   - what it proves;
   - why the next target is urgent;
   - what would happen if AEGIS did not act.

2. Resolve the ASH RELAY -> OPEN CIRCUIT -> PERMAFROST bridge first. It is the only
   current placement that can still read as an avoidable detour despite being
   continuity-correct.

3. Complete the dedicated PROCESS WINDOW development pass, preserving its locked
   placement, partial-success result, strategic function, and Lupin transition.

4. Lock the PERMAFROST -> HULL BREACH target chain.

5. Develop AEGIS FALLS as one continuous operation and state Tremblay's final
   consequence.

6. Place Ducky's four institutional-arc beats and the Crodyn-address synchronization
   inside the ladder.

7. Perform a final dialogue and intermission pass focused on squad relationships,
   Halliday's paper trail, Walker's changing authorization posture, and the cumulative
   cost carried by local partners.

===============================================================================
CONCLUSION
===============================================================================

PERSISTENT THREAT now has a viable and substantially cohesive plot.

Its strongest idea is that the enemy's decisive advantage is not a single secret
weapon. It is access to the systems, contractors, credentials, movements, and habits
through which Europe protects itself. The campaign begins with AEGIS attempting to
hold that system together and ends with Ducky recognizing that its purpose must
survive even if its compromised form does not.

The remaining work is connective rather than reconstructive. No mission currently
needs to be removed or reordered to make the campaign function. The narrative now
requires explicit evidence handoffs, earlier dramatization of Ducky's institutional
arc, a non-detour justification for OPEN CIRCUIT, and a single causal spine through
the two phases of AEGIS FALLS.

With those elements developed, the current ladder can support a coherent campaign
rather than merely a coherent sequence of missions.
