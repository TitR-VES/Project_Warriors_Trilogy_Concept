DUCKY-SIDE SCENE LOCK

Ducky reads Crodyn's performance as evidence of panic rather than control. The

command call. His advance access implies contact with, or foreknowledge from,

CROSS-CAMPAIGN FUNCTION

All three campaigns experience the address simultaneously:

- Romeo receives it in a Cumberland hardware store in Kentucky. Its register is

  the command response and Tremblay's advance knowledge become evidence.
- Spook receives it alone in a Fort Lauderdale safehouse. Its register is
  operational; he reads it as an accelerant and recognizes that his window is
  closing.
