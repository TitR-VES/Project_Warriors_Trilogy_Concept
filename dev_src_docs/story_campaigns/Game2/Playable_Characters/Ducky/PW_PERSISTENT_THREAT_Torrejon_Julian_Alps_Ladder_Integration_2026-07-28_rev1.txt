MISSION 03 — JULIAN ALPS RECOVERY
Location:
Julian Alps, Slovenia.

Core premise:
The Torrejón-deorbited satellite impacts across a difficult Alpine debris field. Ducky and AEGIS deploy with Slovenian special-operations, civil-defense, resistance, and technical-recovery partners to secure the wreckage before hostile recovery teams can strip it for data or hardware.

Operational objective:
- locate the primary surviving satellite sections and security-critical components;
- establish control of the crash corridor and debris trail;
- deny hostile teams access to authentication, telemetry, storage, and AEGIS architectural material;
- recover enough evidence to reconstruct who initiated the deorbit and who intended to exploit the wreckage.

Outcome lock:
The allied force secures the decisive surviving material and denies hostile recovery. The recovery does not restore the lost satellite; it preserves evidence, limits compromise, and gives Halliday and DAOG a more dangerous but more accurate picture.

Narrative function:
- pays off Torrejón immediately rather than leaving the deorbit as an abstract briefing event;
- turns orbital systems warfare into a physical mountain recovery problem;
- reinforces that local Slovenian knowledge, authority, movement, and survival expertise are indispensable;
- provides evidence that feeds the attribution-collapse architecture leading into DEAD FREQUENCY and the later winter missions.
