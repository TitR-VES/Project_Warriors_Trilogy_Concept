PLOT
===============================================================================

Project Warriors follows three concurrent campaigns during the opening phase of a global war in 2028. On September 7, the PANDORA attacks exploit the compromised TETHERBREAK architecture to turn large numbers of American autonomous military systems against their legitimate command structure. The resulting friendly-fire catastrophe, communications disruption, and command paralysis destabilize the United States and its allies at the exact moment that hostile powers begin coordinated offensives in Europe, the Pacific, and along the American West Coast.

The game's three campaigns show different layers of the same crisis. Ramón “Romeo” Álvarez and his Ranger fire team experience the conventional war directly, first while retreating across a collapsing European front and later while defending California from invasion. Ferris “Ducky” Ryan and AEGIS Squad fight to preserve the multinational intelligence and communications infrastructure that allows European forces to remain coordinated. Trevor “Spook” Gartner and the CIA Ground Branch team Victor Two Bravo investigate the covert procurement network behind PANDORA, gradually uncovering evidence that the disaster is connected to a domestic conspiracy known as Riptide Red. Although the protagonists do not operate as a single combined unit, their campaigns overlap chronologically and reveal that the foreign offensives and the conspiracy inside the American security establishment are parts of the same conflict.

-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
LEAD THE WAY
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------

The first campaign follows Ramón “Romeo” Álvarez, the leader of Fire Team Delta, a 75th Ranger Regiment element whose other members are Dingo, Howler, and Beaver. At the beginning of the story, the team is stationed in eastern Poland as part of a NATO reinforcement effort intended to deter further Russian aggression. Their deployment initially follows familiar military routines, but the situation deteriorates when American autonomous systems begin misidentifying allied forces and attacking their own positions. As the PANDORA event spreads, normal command procedures collapse and the eastern defensive line begins to break.

Romeo's team is ordered to preserve an escape route and protect withdrawing personnel rather than hold an increasingly untenable position. The Rangers fight west through Poland and the Czech–Austrian corridor, moving with damaged convoys and fragmented allied units while the airspace above them becomes unreliable. The campaign's opening changes the meaning of its title: “Lead the Way” begins as a proud Ranger motto, but becomes an obligation to keep others moving through a retreat in which reliable orders, air support, and identification systems can no longer be assumed.

After reaching Ramstein Air Base, Fire Team Delta is reassigned to assist with the defense of AEGIS, a distributed multinational infrastructure system used to preserve European geospatial intelligence, communications, and operational coordination. Romeo's squad moves through a chain of vulnerable sites, including the primary AEGIS datacenter on Svalbard, a relay position in the French Alps, a coastal installation on Lemnos, and a concealed facility at Hindeloopen in the Netherlands. Each location presents a different form of infrastructure warfare, ranging from direct assault to spoofing, sabotage, and compromised access. At Hindeloopen, the recovery of a Shibboleth-linked device provides an early indication that the communications problem is not simply a side effect of the wider conflict.

With conventional air travel no longer dependable, the Rangers continue west through France and reach the naval base at Île Longue. There, they help defend the support infrastructure of the French ballistic-missile submarines Le Terrible and Le Vigilant from sabotage. The operation becomes the point at which the two military campaigns separate: AEGIS Squad is diverted toward the deteriorating situation around Rotterdam, while Romeo and his men board the USS District of Columbia for passage back to the United States.

The submarine cannot deliver the Rangers directly to a secure American port because of the naval blockade and the rapidly changing situation along the Pacific coast. After surfacing off Ventura, the team transfers ashore and joins surviving Army and Marine forces attempting to reopen the Southern California corridor. Romeo advances into Los Angeles through North Hollywood, where the mission “Down Rodeo” places the player in the middle of a three-sided urban fight involving American troops, North Korean forces, and the Los Sietes cartel. The encounter at K Lounge also provides one of the campaign's quieter links to the wider conspiracy, as cartel communications refer to a package in Miami that later becomes relevant to Spook's investigation.

Romeo subsequently reaches the Long Beach area and takes part in the Battle of Signal Hill, where the defense of Long Beach Airport brings the Rangers into contact with Staff Sergeant André Bridges and a Marine contingent. The fighting highlights the strain between units trying to push the counterattack forward and those responsible for consolidating positions that cannot be allowed to fall again. After the airport is secured, the surviving force organizes a southbound convoy toward San Diego, with Romeo manning a mounted M2 during the road battle through the contested coastal corridor.

The campaign culminates at North Island Naval Air Station on Coronado, where Russian, North Korean, and Iranian forces launch a major amphibious assault intended to destroy or capture the remaining American naval capability in the area. Romeo and Fire Team Delta defend the installation alongside the assembled American force as the invasion that has been developing throughout the game reaches its largest conventional battle. The defenders prevent the immediate loss of North Island, but the victory does not end the war or restore the country. Romeo finishes the campaign as a Ranger still operating inside the remnants of the military chain of command; his later transformation into a resistance leader belongs to the aftermath explored in the following installment.

-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
DO RIGHT, FEAR NONE
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------

The second campaign centers on Sergeant First Class Ferris “Ducky” Ryan and AEGIS Squad, a DAOG unit composed of Ducky, Finnish sniper and arctic-warfare specialist Aki “Frost” Peltola, Romanian assault specialist Ştefan “Lupin” Dragavei, and Australian combat medic Cassandra “Switchblade” Stewart. Whereas Romeo's story is primarily about movement through a conventional battlefield, Ducky's campaign concerns the survival of the systems that allow allied governments and military commands to see the battlefield at all.

The campaign begins before the PANDORA attacks with “Wirecutter,” a joint operation involving AEGIS Squad and the maritime special-operations team Nereid Black aboard the Sea Lion 7 in the North Sea. What initially appears to be an infrastructure-security mission becomes a close-quarters battle after Russian Vympel operators are discovered aboard the vessel. Although the allied teams secure the ship and the subsea objective, the aftermath leaves several unresolved anomalies. Ducky reports four confirmed Vympel operators and a possible fifth who cannot be accounted for, while the ship's manifest and diving equipment do not match its declared route or purpose.

AEGIS returns to DAOG's forward base at Innsworth, where Colonel Mark Walker conducts the formal debrief and intelligence analyst Tara Halliday, callsign ORACLE, begins comparing the Sea Lion 7 irregularities with other incidents. The sequence gives the player time to observe the squad outside immediate combat and establishes the wider DAOG environment, including its professional relationship with CORYS Squad. During the subsequent “Standing Watch” briefing, Halliday explains that the threat to AEGIS is distributed across civilian shells, relay sites, processing nodes, access systems, and multinational command structures. Her analysis suggests that the Sea Lion 7 operation was not an isolated encounter, but no one yet has enough evidence to identify the larger plan.

When PANDORA strikes, the infrastructure mission becomes critical to Europe's ability to function as a coalition. AEGIS Squad is sent through the same geographically dispersed defense chain that later intersects with Romeo's route: the primary datacenter on Svalbard, relay infrastructure in the French Alps, the Lemnos coastal node, and the concealed Hindeloopen site. The squad fights conventional attackers while also dealing with compromised communications, false identification, insider access, and sabotage intended to make the system fail from within. The campaign repeatedly emphasizes that preserving a server, relay, cable route, or access record can be strategically as important as defeating the force attacking it.

Ducky remains the principal playable character, but selected operations shift temporarily to Frost or Lupin when their specialist capabilities are required. Frost's sections emphasize long-range observation and deliberate target selection, while Lupin's place the player in shorter and more pressurized defensive actions. These transitions present AEGIS as a functioning team rather than a group that exists only to support its leader. Ducky's own characterization remains restrained throughout: as pressure increases, he becomes quieter, more exact, and more determined to complete the mission without treating allied lives or civilian infrastructure as expendable.

The squad is eventually redirected toward Rotterdam as Russian forces attempt to seize the Maasvlakte industrial and energy complex. The approach operation, “Retreat, Hell,” brings AEGIS into a collapsing coastal battlespace shaped by amphibious movement, damaged logistics, and the need to keep a route open for Dutch and allied defenders. It leads into a separate final battle at the Maasvlakte Oil and Gas Terminal, where AEGIS fights alongside the Dutch Marine Corps while the Russian destroyer Severomorsk threatens the position from offshore.

During the finale, Ducky reaches an NMESIS-type launcher and fires a Naval Strike Missile at Severomorsk. The attack forces the ship to withdraw, but the launch exposes the allied command position and is followed by an RPG strike and a renewed ground assault. AEGIS survives the post-launch pressure and prevents the immediate loss of the terminal, preserving a major logistical node at a moment when the wider European defense is close to fragmentation.

The campaign ends without resolving the anomalies that began on Sea Lion 7. Instead, it establishes that the systems protected by AEGIS are being attacked by both foreign forces and compromised actors with detailed knowledge of their architecture. As the West Coast invasion begins, Ducky's victory at Rotterdam demonstrates the campaign's central idea: Europe remains capable not because its institutions are flawless, but because different nations and individuals continue choosing to hold them together under pressure.

-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
RIPTIDE RED
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------

The third campaign follows Trevor “Spook” Gartner, Reid “Tripper” Bartlett, and Daniel “Smoke” Oakenfold, the CIA Ground Branch team Victor Two Bravo, known informally as “the Tourists.” Their handler is Senior Case Officer Matthew Mullen, callsign Regent. Unlike the two military campaigns, “Riptide Red” begins as a covert investigation into a restricted technology shipment and gradually expands into evidence of a conspiracy involving intelligence officers, defense contractors, criminal intermediaries, conditioned operatives, and senior figures inside the American national-security apparatus.

Spook arrives in Amsterdam and meets CIA officer Cameron Steele, callsign Emperor, who briefs Victor Two Bravo on a suspicious shipment connected to PANDORA. The trail leads to Vienna, where the team works with embassy officer Kathy Hughes, callsign Archduke, and raids a suspected safehouse in the Hernals district. The operation, later titled “Garden Path,” turns into a pursuit when the occupants are alerted. Spook chases the suspects across rooftops and through the transport network before escaping by motorcycle and recovering a tablet that links the cell to the German freight company Betta Logistics.

The tablet identifies Simenon Christophe Navarre and his partner Karyna Budanova as possible links in the shipment chain. During “Above My Pay Grade,” Victor Two Bravo infiltrates Navarre's residence outside Frankfurt and obtains access to his records. Depending on the player's choice, Spook may also arrange new identities for Navarre and Budanova before the team moves on the associated Warehouse 46 at Frankfurt Airport. The warehouse is guarded by Atlas personnel, but the shipment has already been removed, indicating that someone knew the CIA team was coming. Tripper recovers a hard drive containing partial TETHERBREAK references and an incomplete charter-flight schedule, which DAOG contact Karl “Jaeger” Stanzler helps connect to the next stage of the route.

The investigation continues in Abu Dhabi during “Infrastructure Day,” where Victor Two Bravo rappels into GenCorp's regional headquarters and searches its data infrastructure. The evidence identifies billionaire logistics intermediary Agarwal Berkhan, while the mission may also bring GenCorp data-security chief Leandro Blommaert into the player's path. The team follows Berkhan to the Lestari House compound near Medan, Indonesia, under Operation DRAGONSLAYER. Berkhan escapes the initial assault and attempts to flee by helicopter, but a local air-defense system abruptly destroys the aircraft before he can be captured. The apparent malfunction is recognized by the team as a deliberate act of remote sanitation intended to prevent Berkhan from being interrogated.

After Berkhan's death, Regent is said to have been “urgently reassigned,” and CIA Director of Operations Robert Macmillan, callsign Bishop, assumes direct control of Victor Two Bravo. Under the new authority, the team follows the surviving trail through Frankfurt and Orlando to South Florida. In “Pandora's Box,” surveillance of the abandoned Desautels resort leads to a confrontation with the Kali Yuga Surf Club and Los Sietes personnel. The five KYSC operatives are not ordinary mercenaries, but conditioned human assets whose repeated tattoos and behavioral profile point toward an older experimentation and control program. Victor Two Bravo recovers records and equipment linking the group to Montoya Farm in New Mexico, then escapes a violent containment effort before Bishop can fully control the evidence.

Acting on the Miami lead, the team infiltrates Montoya Farm during an elite gala in “Burning Daylight.” Above ground, the estate presents itself as an affluent and well-protected social venue. Beneath it, Spook discovers a large hidden complex containing prison cells, medical and conditioning facilities, dormitories, simulated environments, and spaces used to produce compromising or coercive material. Tripper implants extraction hardware in the server system while the team collects evidence connecting prisoners, handlers, criminal logistics, intelligence services, and private contractors. The recovered archive reveals the existence of the Shadow Underground and contains the first substantial references to Riptide Red, showing that PANDORA is part of a much larger architecture of blackmail, trafficking, covert influence, and institutional compromise.

Several details encountered during the campaign suggest that Regent had learned more than he was permitted to report. Spook intercepts part of a cut transmission in Regent's voice, finds a PANDORA-related file whose author's identity has been manually redacted, and later hears Macmillan refer to information Mullen supposedly gave the team. Together, these clues indicate that Regent's disappearance was connected to an attempt to expose the conspiracy rather than a routine reassignment.

The Montoya archive directs Victor Two Bravo toward East McFarlane Island in the Caribbean. In “Come Fly With Me,” the team steals a floatplane and approaches the island covertly before launching the final operation, “Sins of Our Fathers.” There they encounter the War Wolves, former elite operators equipped with counter-forensic implants that destroy identifying evidence when they are killed. After fighting through the island's defenses, Spook corners Edward Goldberg and Gillian Stockbridge, the principal architects behind the network he has been tracing.

Before Victor Two Bravo can arrest or kill them, Macmillan orders Spook to stand down and grant Goldberg safe passage. When Spook refuses, Bishop declares the team rogue, revealing that the betrayal is not limited to contractors or peripheral intelligence assets. Goldberg and Stockbridge escape as the campaign ends on a cliffhanger. At the same time, the coordinated assault on the American West Coast begins, linking the conspiracy uncovered by Spook to the conventional war experienced by Romeo and the infrastructure attacks resisted by Ducky. The conclusion reframes the entire game: PANDORA was not merely the opening strike of a foreign invasion, but a deliberately engineered collapse protected by people inside the institutions that were supposed to prevent it.
