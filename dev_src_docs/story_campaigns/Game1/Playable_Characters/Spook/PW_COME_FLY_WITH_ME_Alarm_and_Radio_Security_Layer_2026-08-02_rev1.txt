SECURITY STATES

STATE 0 — UNAWARE

Security personnel follow their normal guard routines. The player may bypass,
distract, restrain, incapacitate, or engage guards according to the mission's covert
acquisition rules.

STATE 1 — CCTV INVESTIGATION

A camera detects Victor Two Bravo and the manager sees the feed. The manager radios
the nearest available guard, who leaves or modifies the normal routine to investigate
the reported location. The rest of the base is not automatically placed on full
alert solely because of the initial camera sighting.

STATE 2 — COMPOUND ALARM

A fixed alarm is activated or a general radio alert is completed. All surviving
armed guards on base are alerted to Victor Two Bravo's presence. They abandon their
normal routines, attempt to locate the player and teammates, and shoot them on sight.

The full-alert state changes the encounter from covert infiltration into an active
lethal search. Exact search sectors, regrouping points, crossfire positions, radio
chatter, and alert persistence remain production work.

MANAGER LOCKDOWN BRANCH

If the player eliminates all seven armed security guards before securing the
manager, the manager locks himself inside his office and calls the police. The call
starts the external-response pressure and prevents the normal compliant access
handoff.

The player must breach the locked office, confront and secure the manager, and
obtain the Found FBA-2C keys before the responding forces arrive. The manager remains
required for aircraft access; eliminating the guards does not allow the player to
skip the office objective.

DESIGN INTENT

The security system is deliberately layered and redundant:

- cameras create an observable but initially localized investigation threat;
- fixed buttons create readable spatial alarm objectives;
- radios make every guard and the manager a mobile alert risk;
- full alarm converts all surviving guards to shoot-on-sight search behavior;
- eliminating every guard triggers a different pressure branch rather than removing
  all mission tension;
- police close the land approaches while the Coast Guard threatens the maritime exit;
- controlling one gatehouse or one administration-building alarm does not disable
  every other alert channel.

The west-gate guard's football viewing, bathroom absence, and flask break remain
valid infiltration windows, but catching him away from the west gatehouse does not
remove his radio capability or the outward-facing west-gate camera.
