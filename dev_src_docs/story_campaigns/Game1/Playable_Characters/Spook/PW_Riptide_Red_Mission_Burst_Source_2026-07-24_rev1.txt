01. MAN FROM MEDAN / OPERATION DRAGONSLAYER
===============================================================================

MISSION FUNCTION
Victor Two Bravo is sent to Indonesia to apprehend Agarwal Berkhan, a logistics/procurement intermediary whose testimony could expose the wider contractor-state architecture. The mission demonstrates that the conspiracy is already willing to destroy its own human links rather than permit interrogation.

CORE FLOW
1. approach the Lestari House / Medan-area target space under controlled covert posture;
2. identify Berkhan and penetrate the protected interior;
3. recover or copy useful procurement/route material;
4. grapple with Berkhan during his escape and steal his access credential/ID;
5. use that credential to override the lockdown he triggers behind him;
6. pursue toward the helicopter extraction point;
7. witness the local Skynex/RADS/SAM defense system "malfunction" and destroy Berkhan's helicopter;
8. break contact before Indonesian security forces close the area.

DRAMATIC RULE
Berkhan's death must read as deliberate remote sanitation disguised as technical failure. The team reaches him and still loses the interrogation because someone with deeper access can close the trail faster than they can exploit it.

COMMAND / COMMS
- BLACK KNIGHT and BLACK KING are recovered callsign directions for the command/mission-control layer; final assignment remains a production pass.
- After the loss, Macmillan/Bishop tells the team Regent has been urgently reassigned and places Victor Two Bravo under direct authority.
- That command move initiates WHITEOUT and the Miami chain.

LOCAL-FORCE RULE
Indonesian security forces are not a disposable enemy faction. Their arrival creates an exfiltration clock and sovereignty pressure. Avoid broad engagement; contact is failure pressure, not the mission's intended combat identity.

OPEN / PROVENANCE ONLY
- Gerhana/Eclipse yacht or shell-asset connections may be seeded only where compatible with later Game 4 Marietta continuity.
- Exact location geometry, command callsign assignments, and Berkhan's complete dossier remain open.

2. surveil the Desautels resort and identify Simmons/Lynch-linked activity;

04. BURNING DAYLIGHT
===============================================================================

MISSION FUNCTION
Victor Two Bravo infiltrates Montoya Farm during a gala and discovers the complete hidden conditioning facility beneath an affluent social surface. The mission converts the Miami clue into hard institutional evidence and propels the campaign toward East McFarlane Island.

CORE FLOW
1. enter or observe the Montoya Farm gala under covert constraints;
2. use a directional microphone to collect Stockbridge-linked conversation/evidence;
3. obtain the cloakroom/security keycard and manipulate or avoid CCTV;
4. locate the pool-house/pool-area hatch and descend beneath the estate;
5. move through prison, medical, dormitory, simulated-sky, and production/training spaces;
6. plant the server implant and begin the layered decrypt/exfil process;
7. escape through the service/laundry architecture rather than retracing the gala route;
8. carry forward the recovered network, procurement, personnel, and conditioning evidence.

ENVIRONMENTAL / COLLECTIBLE ANCHORS
- vent-cleaning or maintenance evidence that reveals how long the underground site has operated;
- a Ludovico-style conditioning room presented clinically, not as horror-theme scenery;
- MAWL-DA or related equipment provenance that ties legitimate procurement to the covert program;
- records that distinguish prisoners, conditioned assets, handlers, and production/logistics functions.

ANTAGONIST NAME LOCK
The relevant antagonist is JACK PRATT.
- PRATT is distinct from Dale PRUETT.
- Purge Pruett/Pruitt variants when they refer to Jack Pratt.

DESIGN RULES
- The gala and underground facility must feel like two layers of the same institution, not two unrelated levels.
- Covert observation, access theft, surveillance control, and evidence collection carry equal weight with combat.
- The facility should be operationally plausible and materially ugly, not a theatrical supervillain dungeon.
- The mission's success is evidence implantation/recovery and escape, not killing everyone present.
