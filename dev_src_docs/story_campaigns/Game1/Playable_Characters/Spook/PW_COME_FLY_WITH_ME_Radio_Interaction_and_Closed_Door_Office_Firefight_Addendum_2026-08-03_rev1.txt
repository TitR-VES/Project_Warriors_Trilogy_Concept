PURPOSE

Land two owner-developed refinements for COME FLY WITH ME:

1. The exposed radio or compact sound system carrying the preferred Frank Sinatra
   "Come Fly With Me" diegetic cue is player-operable and destructible. It ceases to
   function when disabled by direct small-arms fire or a damaging nearby explosion.

2. If the player reaches the manager's office without detection and attacks the
   unaware manager and two guards by firing through the closed destructible door, the
   occupants immediately return fire through that same door. The unlocked door does
   not require a forced breach and can become partially or fully open through damage.

This addendum extends the existing safehouse, manager-office, UI-prompt, security,
and manager-death sources. It does not replace the mission's geography, alarm logic,
police response, Coast Guard response, aircraft access, key-bundle ownership, or
manager-death fallback.

================================================================================
PART I — SINATRA / FALLBACK RADIO INTERACTION
================================================================================

CREATIVE CUE

The preferred diegetic source remains Frank Sinatra's "Come Fly With Me," subject to
commercial music clearance and the existing fallback requirement. A newly composed or
separately cleared mid-century lounge / big-band cue may replace it without changing
the interaction and destruction rules below.

SOURCE-PLACEMENT CONTINUITY

The repository currently supports a radio in the Christiansted safehouse and a radio
or compact office sound system in the manager-office sequence. Any exposed device that
actually carries the Sinatra/fallback cue inherits this addendum.

Production must explicitly decide whether the final mission uses:

- one principal occurrence of the cue;
- two deliberately separate diegetic occurrences;
- or one cue placement plus a different low-volume station track elsewhere.

Do not duplicate the same master recording in the safehouse and manager office by
accident. If both occurrences are retained, the repetition must be an intentional
mission motif rather than a source-integration mistake.

PLAYER OPERATION

When the player is close enough to reach the active radio's controls, has direct line
of sight, and no physical obstruction blocks the interaction, display:

    TURN OFF

The prompt is restrained and functional. It should not use a more theatrical Spook-
specific verb because the action is mundane and instantly legible.

The prompt may surface during or immediately after the relevant drawer-opening
sequence if that sequence leaves Spook physically close enough to the radio. The
player must be able to interrupt the music without waiting for a later mission state.

Interaction behavior:

- one press triggers a short first-person control operation appropriate to the device;
- the radio stops on the switch, button, or knob action;
- the prompt disappears once the device is off;
- TURN ON may be offered only if reversible operation serves the authored sequence;
- mission-critical dialogue or objective information must not depend on leaving the
  radio on;
- manually turning the radio off does not itself alert nearby enemies unless a later
  source deliberately establishes that someone notices the change.

DESTRUCTION

The exposed cue source is destructible.

Valid disabling causes include:

- one or more direct small-arms hits sufficient to damage the working components;
- projectile penetration through the casing;
- fragments from a nearby explosion;
- blast impulse or overpressure sufficient to damage or disconnect the device;
- impact with the floor or environment after being thrown by an explosion;
- visible cable or power separation where the device depends on external power.

Required response:

- the local music emitter stops immediately or collapses through a very brief failure
  transient;
- acceptable punctuation includes a short static burst, speaker pop, electrical crack,
  pitch collapse, or mechanically interrupted playback;
- the device loses TURN OFF / TURN ON prompts after functional destruction;
- its destroyed or disabled state persists for the encounter and through compatible
  checkpoints;
- audio follows the prop if it is moved while still functional and never remains fixed
  at the original location after the device falls or is displaced;
- a visibly destroyed radio never continues clean music playback.

COMBAT MIX

- weapon reports, impacts, barks, and essential dialogue take priority over the music;
- the radio may duck while still audibly existing in the room;
- destroying or switching off the source removes the music naturally rather than
  muting the entire combat mix;
- if another separate speaker carries the same signal, only physically connected or
  dependent emitters should stop with the source.

EXEMPTIONS

The owner's broader game-wide exception applies here and elsewhere:

- reinforced speakers;
- recessed speakers;
- concealed architectural speakers;
- protected public-address hardware;
- inaccessible audio connection hardware;
- buried cabling;
- hardened amplifier or distribution equipment.

These do not need to fail from incidental small-arms fire or nearby blast when their
working components are credibly protected. They remain vulnerable to power loss,
upstream source failure, direct access, sabotage, or mission-authored heavy damage.

The visible Sinatra/fallback radio in COME FLY WITH ME is not part of this exemption.
It is an ordinary exposed device and must be physically consistent.

================================================================================
PART II — UNAWARE CLOSED-DOOR OFFICE ATTACK
================================================================================

BRANCH NAME

    VARIANT A-F — UNAWARE CLOSED-DOOR FIREFIGHT

This branch begins when:

- the compound has not entered full alarm;
- the manager has not locked or barricaded the office;
- the manager and both office guards remain inside and initially unaware;
- the office door is closed but unlocked;
- Spook fires into the office through the door before Smoke opens it and before the
  coordinated Variant A entry begins.

The first hostile shot cancels the semi-scripted unaware entry choreography. The scene
transitions immediately into a freeform combat state without failing or softlocking the
mission.

TACTICAL INTENT

The branch turns the office approach hallway into a dangerous penetration fight.

The player has the initiative but not a free kill box. The manager and two guards are
armed, close to cover, and able to exploit the same destructible door that the player
uses. Because the approach hallway is long and relatively narrow, their return fire can
create overlapping threat lanes and make a careless frontal attack substantially more
difficult than the recommended coordinated entry.

The sequence should reward:

- controlled use of cover;
- movement off the doorway's center line;
- intelligent wall and door penetration;
- suppression and timing;
- stun-grenade employment;
- careful target discrimination when manager survival matters;
- acceptance of lethal or blast-overpressure consequences when manager survival does
  not matter to the player.

It should not become an unwinnable punishment branch or an instant scripted death.

IMMEDIATE OCCUPANT RESPONSE

On the first incoming shot or credible door penetration:

1. Both guards alert immediately.
2. The manager alerts immediately and attempts to access or present his handgun if he
   can do so safely.
3. The occupants break from idle poses and seek the nearest credible cover rather than
   remaining frozen in their unaware choreography positions.
4. Guards call the threat direction and begin suppressive or aimed return fire through
   the door.
5. The manager may also return fire through the door when armed, positioned, and not
   blocked by a guard.
6. The office radio or sound system continues, is switched off by an NPC only if
   deliberately authored, or is disabled naturally by gunfire / blast under Part I.
7. Any radio call, alarm escalation, CCTV consequence, or wider-compound response
   follows the controlling security-layer state logic.

RETURN-FIRE MODEL

The manager and guards can fire through:

- intact but penetrable door panels;
- existing bullet holes;
- widening splintered openings;
- a damaged latch-side gap;
- the open doorway after the door swings or falls;
- adjacent penetrable office construction only where material and geometry support it.

AI must not use perfect knowledge through an opaque barrier.

Initial return fire should be driven by:

- impact direction;
- muzzle report and acoustic localization;
- visible splinters and penetration origin;
- last known player position;
- teammate callouts;
- brief glimpses through holes, edge gaps, damaged panels, or the opening door.

Accuracy and confidence may improve as the barrier degrades and sightlines become real.
The guards can use disciplined suppression to deny the doorway even before they have a
clean visual. The manager should be less tactically capable than the guards but still
dangerous enough that ignoring him is a mistake.

Do not let AI continuously track Spook through solid opaque material after he changes
position. Repositioning, breaking line of fire, and using cover must matter.

HALLWAY THREAT GEOMETRY

The final level blockout should preserve a tactically meaningful approach:

- enough hallway length that the office occupants can threaten the player before a
  simple one-step rush reaches the threshold;
- limited width that makes the doorway center line dangerous;
- at least one credible piece of player cover or offset angle so the branch is hard but
  not arbitrary;
- no decorative clutter that traps the player under unavoidable penetration fire;
- office furniture that gives the guards and manager distinct cover choices rather than
  one stacked firing point;
- enough internal separation that a stun grenade can create a meaningful opening while
  a frag or blast-overpressure grenade carries a real manager-survival risk.

Exact dimensions remain a production blockout decision. The tactical relationship is
locked.

DOOR STATE AND DAMAGE PROGRESSION

The door is unlocked throughout the unaware branch unless another alert-state system
has explicitly changed it.

A recommended damage progression is:

1. CLOSED / INTACT / UNLOCKED
   - handle works;
   - Smoke can open it for planned Variant A;
   - bullets may penetrate according to material and calibre.

2. PERFORATED
   - visible bullet holes and splintering;
   - partial sight and sound leakage increases;
   - collision remains intact;
   - return fire through the barrier is active.

3. LATCH OR HINGE COMPROMISED
   - repeated impacts, buckshot, heavy projectiles, or blast can break the latch or one
     or more hinges;
   - door may swing inward or outward depending on authored hinge direction and force;
   - the opening creates a new sightline and navigation state.

4. PARTIALLY DETACHED
   - door hangs from one hinge, leans, or obstructs part of the opening;
   - collision and navigation update to match the visible object;
   - AI must not fire or move as though the original closed collision still exists.

5. OPEN OR FULLY DETACHED
   - door falls open or comes completely off its hinges;
   - no breach interaction is required;
   - player, companion, grenade, projectile, and AI navigation use the exposed doorway;
   - fallen-door physics must not create an unavoidable player trap or block key
     recovery.

Even if damage never reaches stages 3–5, the player can open the surviving door because
it is unlocked. No lock-pick, charge, shotgun breaching round, ram, or scripted breach
animation is required to defeat a lock that does not exist.

BREACH PROMPT CONTINUITY

The planned Variant A prompt remains:

    BREACH

Its meaning is:

- initiate the coordinated team entry;
- Smoke operates and throws the unlocked door;
- Spook crosses first;
- Smoke and Tripper execute their established roles.

If the player fires through the door first:

- remove BREACH immediately;
- cancel bounded threshold choreography and time dilation;
- transition companions into combat support and hallway / room-clearing behavior;
- allow ordinary OPEN / PUSH / contextual door behavior only if useful and physically
  valid after the fight begins;
- never make the player complete the abandoned semi-scripted entry before combat can
  continue.

GRENADE EMPLOYMENT

STUN GRENADES

Preferred tool when the player wants to preserve the manager alive.

- can interrupt the defenders' return-fire rhythm;
- create a short entry window;
- degrade aim, orientation, and communication according to existing flash/stun rules;
- remain subject to room geometry, cover, facing, and hearing protection;
- do not guarantee a scripted surrender.

FRAGMENTATION GRENADES

Valid but high-risk.

- can kill or incapacitate either guard;
- can kill the manager;
- can damage or destroy the radio and office props;
- can further compromise or remove the door;
- can displace the key bundle or manager body, requiring the existing recovery fail-
  safes;
- may accelerate wider alarm and response states.

BLAST-OVERPRESSURE / CONCUSSIVE GRENADES

Also valid where supported by the game's equipment set.

- can incapacitate or kill occupants in the confined office;
- can create severe manager-survival risk even without obvious fragmentation wounds;
- can break the door, windows, lightweight partitions, and exposed audio device;
- should not be treated as a consequence-free non-lethal substitute.

The game does not moralize through an intrusive warning. Risk is communicated through
weapon knowledge, room geometry, companion barks where appropriate, and the resulting
state.

MANAGER SURVIVAL AND KEY RECOVERY

Manager survival remains preferred but not mandatory for mission continuity.

If the manager survives:

- Spook can secure, disarm, interrogate, and obtain the aircraft answer / keys under the
  existing live-manager sources;
- dialogue may compress or change because the manager has just participated in a
  firefight;
- the manager should not revert to the pristine unaware apprehension animation.

If the manager is killed:

- the mission continues through the existing deceased-manager search and key-recovery
  fallback;
- Spook searches the body or immediate displaced-body area as authored;
- the N272BT key bundle remains recoverable;
- the Spook-to-Smoke handoff remains available according to the controlling fallback;
- no grenade or door physics event may permanently lose the keys beneath inaccessible
  geometry.

If the manager is wounded or incapacitated:

- weapon security is resolved before interrogation or search;
- surrender, treatment, restraint, or body-search logic follows his actual state;
- the scene does not force a binary healthy-versus-dead presentation when the combat
  system supports intermediate injury.

COMPANION BEHAVIOR

After the player initiates the closed-door firefight:

- Smoke and Tripper abandon the unaware choreography cleanly;
- they take offset positions that do not crowd Spook into the penetration lane;
- they return controlled fire only when they have a credible target, penetration line,
  or suppression requirement;
- they do not shoot through Spook or occupy the same narrow cover node;
- they may call guard movement, manager weapon access, grenade risk, door failure, or a
  newly opened sightline;
- they can support a stun-assisted entry;
- they do not automatically kill the manager unless he presents an immediate threat;
- they secure surviving weapons and occupants after room control;
- Tripper's rear-security responsibility resumes when tactically possible, but active
  threats take priority over mechanically returning to the planned pose.

ENEMY AND CIVILIAN CONTINUITY

- the office contains the manager and two armed guards, not a fourth hidden guard;
- any civilian possibility must come from a separate controlling source and cannot be
  invented by this branch;
- upper- and lower-level guard reactions remain governed by the larger facility state;
- gunfire and explosions may alert nearby personnel even if the player reached the
  office undetected;
- the freeform branch must not silently preserve stealth after an unmistakable loud
  firefight;
- suppressed fire through the door can delay broader detection only where acoustics,
  radio behavior, witness states, and security logic genuinely support it.

DIFFICULTY AND FAIRNESS

The branch should be tactically demanding because it is a poor frontal choice, not
because AI cheats.

Preserve:

- readable material penetration behavior;
- consistent player and AI ballistics through the same door;
- credible suppression rather than perfect wall-tracking;
- at least one survivable fallback position;
- grenade and repositioning solutions;
- companion support without automated victory;
- a viable careful-firearms path for players who lack grenades;
- accessibility tuning for penetration indicators, audio localization, reaction time,
  and friendly collision without removing the branch's tactical identity.

Do not preserve:

- defenders standing motionless behind the door;
- one-way player-only penetration;
- invulnerable door panels after obvious structural failure;
- a forced charge placement on an unlocked door;
- perfect enemy aim through intact opaque material;
- door debris that blocks the only route or hides the key bundle permanently;
- automatic mission failure solely because the manager dies;
- a return to the unaware scripted choreography after combat has begun.

STATE TRANSITION SUMMARY
================================================================================

UNAWARE APPROACH

- door closed, intact, unlocked;
- manager and two guards unaware;
- Sinatra/fallback cue plays from an exposed local source if this is the retained cue
  placement;
- BREACH offers coordinated dynamic entry.

PLAYER CHOOSES BREACH

- existing Variant A semi-scripted entry runs;
- Smoke opens the unlocked door;
- Spook enters first;
- adaptive guard resolution and manager apprehension proceed.

PLAYER FIRES THROUGH DOOR

- BREACH disappears;
- Variant A-F begins;
- occupants take cover and return fire through the barrier;
- door damage and combat geometry evolve physically;
- stun, fragmentation, and overpressure solutions remain valid with different risks;
- manager survival or death determines live handoff versus body-search fallback.

PLAYER OPENS DOOR WITHOUT FIRING OR BREACH PROMPT

Where mission scripting permits ordinary manual operation before the stack is complete:

- the door opens because it is unlocked;
- occupant awareness follows sight, sound, and timing;
- the scene enters an improvised alert response rather than pretending a lock was
  defeated;
- production may restrict this specific interaction during final stack commitment to
  preserve companion collision and choreography, but not by changing the canonical
  lock state.

ALERTED COMPOUND / MANAGER LOCKDOWN

- this addendum no longer controls door lock state;
- transition to Variant B and the security-layer sources;
- a genuinely locked or barricaded door may require forced entry.

================================================================================
RESULT
================================================================================

COME FLY WITH ME now treats its exposed Sinatra/fallback radio as a real world object:
the player can switch it off at close range, shoot it, or disable it through nearby
blast, and its audio responds immediately and persistently. The broader rule extends to
ordinary exposed audio devices across the game while exempting credibly reinforced,
recessed, concealed, and protected infrastructure from casual destruction.

The manager-office unaware branch now supports a dangerous player-initiated closed-door
firefight. The unlocked destructible door is a two-way ballistic barrier, not passive
cover for the player. The two guards and armed manager take cover and return fire
through it, the hallway geometry makes frontal pressure tactically meaningful, and
door damage can open or remove the barrier without requiring a forced breach. Stun
grenades support a manager-survival approach; fragmentation and blast-overpressure
solutions remain valid with lethal consequences and the existing key-recovery fallback.
