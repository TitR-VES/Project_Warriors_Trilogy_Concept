EXECUTIVE PRODUCTION DECISION
================================================================================

RECOMMENDED DEMO BOUNDARY

The first playable demo should be a Christiansted vertical slice focused on the
Ocean Blue Sky Tours seaplane-base acquisition sequence.

The playable slice should begin with a brief interactive safehouse preparation beat
and end after the Found FBA-2C N272BT successfully begins its escape from
Christiansted. The preferred first-slice endpoint is either:

- an authored takeoff followed by a short airborne beat and fade; or
- a fade immediately after the aircraft clears the harbor and the escape state is
  unambiguously achieved.

The first slice should not require a full 39-mile playable flight or a second detailed
East McFarlane level.

RECOMMENDED INCLUDED EXPERIENCE

1. Interactive safehouse preparation.
2. SUV departure or compressed transition to the western observation position.
3. Short playable surveillance and guard-routine learning.
4. Relocation to the close final staging point.
5. Night infiltration of Ocean Blue Sky Tours.
6. Multiple perimeter approaches.
7. Seven-contractor security layout.
8. Guard routines and readable vulnerability windows.
9. Four CCTV cameras monitored by the manager.
10. Four fixed alarm buttons.
11. Radios carried by all seven contractors and the manager.
12. Localized CCTV-investigation state.
13. Compound-wide alert state.
14. Recoverable stealth failure.
15. Smoke and Tripper companion behavior.
16. Manager-office Variant A — unaware / unlocked office.
17. Manager-office Variant B — alerted / locked office.
18. Deceased-manager key-recovery fallback.
19. N272BT key-bundle recovery and transfer to Smoke.
20. Movement to the north jetty.
21. Aircraft boarding, startup, mooring release, water movement, and escape.
22. Police and Coast Guard escalation where triggered, at a level appropriate to the
    vertical slice.

RECOMMENDED NON-GOALS FOR THE FIRST SLICE

- Full 39-mile playable flight to East McFarlane.
- Detailed East McFarlane environment.
- SINS OF OUR FATHERS gameplay.
- Full civilian-aircraft simulation suitable for an entire game.
- A general-purpose vehicle framework for the trilogy.
- Every Project Warriors weapon, injury, armor, inventory, squad-command, and
  customization system.
- Full anatomical damage and armor-coverage implementation.
- Broad campaign progression, save-profile, or metagame systems.
- Multiplayer or cooperative networking.
- Final marketing-quality art across every visible background area.

RATIONALE

The Christiansted slice already demonstrates the most distinctive and expensive-to-
prove Project Warriors qualities:

- observation before action;
- grounded first-person tactical handling;
- restrained contextual UI;
- stealth with readable routines;
- multiple valid force levels;
- competent companion operators;
- layered security rather than a single alarm trigger;
- authored but player-controlled first-person set pieces;
- recoverable escalation;
- environmental storytelling;
- narrative objective continuity through a physical hero prop;
- a memorable escape payoff.

Adding a second island and full flight model before these systems work would increase
scope without proving the core mission promise more effectively.

================================================================================
VERTICAL-SLICE CHARTER
================================================================================

DEMO PROMISE

A grounded first-person covert-action level in which the player observes and
infiltrates a compact Caribbean seaplane base with two AI teammates, manages a
layered security system, acquires a floatplane through a branching office
confrontation, and escapes without the mission collapsing when stealth is lost.

PLAYER EXPERIENCE PILLARS

1. OBSERVE, THEN ACT

The player should be rewarded for watching the compound, learning routines, and
identifying windows rather than treating stealth as hidden trial and error.

2. PROFESSIONAL TEAMWORK

Smoke and Tripper should read as highly competent partners with distinct roles. They
support the player, react to player action, and preserve the player's ownership of
Spook's key objectives.

3. LAYERED SECURITY

Cameras, manager-directed investigation, radios, fixed alarms, guard search, police,
and maritime pressure form a connected system. No single switch should trivialize
the whole base.

4. RECOVERABLE ESCALATION

A mistake should materially change the mission without automatically ending it.
Quiet, disrupted, alerted, and external-response states should all remain playable.

5. FIRST-PERSON CONTINUITY

Major authored actions should remain in first person. The player initiates key beats
and keeps meaningful look, aim, and situational ownership whenever safely possible.

6. RESTRAINED PRESENTATION

Prompts, music, camera assistance, time dilation, HUD information, and companion
callouts should support comprehension without converting the mission into a heavily
telegraphed arcade sequence.

VERTICAL-SLICE SUCCESS CRITERIA

The slice is successful when a representative player can:

- identify at least two useful guard opportunities through observation;
- understand that cameras create a localized investigation before a full alarm;
- recognize an in-progress radio or alarm action and interrupt it;
- enter through more than one viable perimeter approach;
- complete the mission without killing every contractor;
- recover from detection and continue toward the aircraft;
- experience the appropriate office branch based on mission state;
- recover the N272BT key bundle without a state or animation softlock;
- understand why Smoke receives the keys and pilots the aircraft;
- reach and depart from the north jetty under quiet or pressured conditions;
- distinguish Spook, Smoke, and Tripper's operational roles;
- complete the slice with readable objectives, subtitles, remappable controls, and
  no branch-dependent progression failure.

MACRO LEVEL FLOW
================================================================================

PHASE 1 — SAFEHOUSE PREPARATION

Function:

- establish first-person control;
- orient the player to Spook, Smoke, and Tripper;
- present the East McFarlane lead and need for an aircraft;
- allow a restrained equipment/readiness interaction;
- transition into the surveillance operation.

First-slice recommendation:

Keep this phase brief and interactive. Do not build a large safehouse hub system.
Use the minimum interior needed to establish tone, team relationship, objective, and
player controls.

PHASE 2 — WESTERN SURVEILLANCE

Function:

- show the compound at readable scale;
- introduce surveillance optics or observation interaction;
- expose at least two guard routines;
- identify the north service entrance, jetty, aircraft, cameras, alarms, or patrol
  vulnerabilities through player observation and companion callouts;
- compress several hours without real-time waiting.

Implementation requirement:

Observation must produce usable knowledge. The player should not merely watch a
cinematic. Learned routines may create later HUD tags, notebook entries, objective
hints, or companion reminders, but information presentation must remain restrained.

PHASE 3 — FINAL STAGING

Function:

- establish after-dark infiltration state;
- confirm equipment and companion readiness;
- provide the last calm decision point;
- move from observation to on-foot play.

PHASE 4 — PERIMETER INFILTRATION

Function:

- support at least two practical routes;
- present gatehouse cameras, guards, chain-link visibility, and open apron exposure;
- validate stealth, distraction, and low-signature takedown systems;
- allow detection to transition into investigation or alert without scripting failure.

PHASE 5 — ADMINISTRATION-BUILDING APPROACH

Function:

- coordinate west-gate bathroom movement with the interior guard;
- support north-service access;
- expose the hallway alarm;
- establish office branch preconditions;
- maintain companion navigation and stack readiness.

PHASE 6 — MANAGER'S OFFICE

Function:

- resolve through Variant A, Variant B, a hybrid/freeform alerted state, or the
  deceased-manager fallback;
- obtain the N272BT keys;
- secure or resolve remaining office threats;
- transfer flight responsibility to Smoke.

PHASE 7 — NORTH-JETTY MOVEMENT

Function:

- move past the fuel-storage landmark;
- engage or bypass remaining security;
- use the north-jetty guard routine where still valid;
- escalate pressure if external response is active;
- deliver a clear visual payoff when N272BT comes into full view.

PHASE 8 — AIRCRAFT ESCAPE

Function:

- verify key ownership;
- board the team;
- release or resolve moorings;
- start N272BT;
- react to quiet, alert, police, and maritime states;
- taxi and take off;
- close the demo cleanly without requiring East McFarlane production.

SECURITY-DIRECTOR ARCHITECTURE
================================================================================

RECOMMENDATION

Implement one authoritative mission-level Security Director rather than allowing
each guard Blueprint to independently decide compound-wide state.

The Security Director should own or reference:

- current security state;
- last confirmed player/team location;
- active investigation location;
- investigation source;
- available investigator selection;
- surviving guard roster;
- restrained/incapacitated guard roster;
- active radio calls;
- completed general alert;
- fixed-alarm states;
- camera detections;
- manager awareness;
- manager lockdown state;
- office-guard deployment state;
- police-call timer and completion;
- police response state;
- Coast Guard response state;
- N272BT key ownership;
- aircraft startup and escape state.

RECOMMENDED STATE MODEL

STATE 0 — UNOBSERVED

- normal patrol routines;
- distractions active;
- cameras monitor normal cones;
- manager watches feeds;
- office Variant A remains possible;
- guard vulnerability windows may occur.

STATE 1 — LOCAL INVESTIGATION

Trigger examples:

- manager confirms a camera sighting;
- suspicious sound or partial visual cue;
- a guard reports uncertainty without completing a general alert.

Behavior:

- nearest available guard investigates;
- other guards remain on modified or normal routines;
- manager remains attentive;
- unresolved confirmation may return to State 0;
- confirmed hostile action may escalate to State 2.

STATE 2 — COMPOUND ALERT

Trigger examples:

- completed guard or manager general-radio alert;
- fixed alarm activated;
- sufficiently confirmed hostile event under final rules.

Behavior:

- surviving guards abandon normal routines;
- shoot-on-sight search behavior;
- office guards deploy under Variant B preconditions;
- manager may lock down;
- north-jetty routine stops;
- aircraft startup becomes contested.

STATE 3 — EXTERNAL RESPONSE INBOUND

Trigger:

- manager police call completes.

Behavior:

- police timer/approach begins;
- Coast Guard approach begins under controlling escalation rules;
- audio and objective pressure communicate the change;
- securing the manager afterward does not silently erase a completed call unless a
  later owner decision explicitly permits cancellation.

STATE 4 — CONTAINMENT / ARRIVAL

Behavior:

- two police cars approach the west and east gates;
- eight armed police officers deploy in total;
- Coast Guard pressure affects the north-jetty departure route;
- mission remains technically completable but substantially harder.

STATE 5 — AIRCRAFT ESCAPE

Behavior:

- player objective transitions from acquisition to survival/departure;
- ground AI reacts to engine, taxi, and takeoff;
- maritime AI or authored pressure reacts to aircraft movement;
- mission completion occurs only after N272BT reaches the protected escape threshold.

TRANSITION SPECIFICATION REQUIREMENTS

Every transition must define:

- exact trigger;
- minimum confirmation threshold;
- time delay;
- interruptibility;
- audiovisual cue;
- subtitle/caption equivalent;
- companion bark;
- AI destination update;
- objective update;
- persistence through checkpoints;
- rollback or recovery conditions;
- failure and softlock prevention.
