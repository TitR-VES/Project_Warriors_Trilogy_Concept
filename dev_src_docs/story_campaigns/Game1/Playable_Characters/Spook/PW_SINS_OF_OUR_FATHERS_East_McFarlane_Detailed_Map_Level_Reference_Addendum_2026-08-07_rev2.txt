DETAILED MAP DEVELOPMENT ACCEPTED IN REV2

1. TEMPLE PAVILION BRANCH

The western Temple Pavilion is now developed as a navigational branch rather than a
single objective point. The outside door is locked; the player reaches Goldberg’s
secure office through a service-shed tunnel, stairs and a concealed movable-bookshelf
door. From inside, the pavilion can be opened back toward the exterior. The unnamed
M200 and a low-wall firing position provide one optional counter-sniper solution
against Rip without making that solution mandatory.

2. REGENT CACHE DISCOVERY

The western-cape flagpole cache remains the protected optional Regent dead drop. Map
notes preserve the weatherproof package, encrypted USB drive, unsigned handwritten
note, unfamiliar symbol and non-Game-1 coordinates. Rev2 additionally maps eight
benches around the island and explicitly ties them to scattered coordinate fragments
that can reveal the cache location. The puzzle is optional and must not make the cache
mandatory for completing the finale.

3. GUESTHOUSE / SECURITY ROUTE

The detailed KML places Underwood’s encounter in the northwestern guesthouse/service
side of the residence property. Victor Two Bravo may approach under foliage cover,
enter the apparently dark guesthouse, survive Underwood’s stun-grenade initiation,
and recover his access card for the concealed security-monitoring room.

The earlier phrase “north eastern corner” is corrected. The physical guesthouse marker
and encounter wording now agree on the northwestern guesthouse/service side.

4. LOCAL IFF AGAINST GRAVES

Underwood’s card may allow the player to alter local IFF threat profiles so Goldberg’s
privately sourced property defenses turn against Graves. Rev2 intentionally does not
lock an automatic remote kill. The IFF-assisted route may suppress, injure, displace,
or defeat Graves depending on final encounter implementation. Direct combat remains a
fully valid solution.
