DETAILED MAP DEVELOPMENT — CURRENT LOCKS

1. TEMPLE PAVILION BRANCH

The Temple Pavilion remains an optional navigational branch. Exterior access is locked;

The unnamed non-thermal M200 and low-wall firing position provide one practical
counter-sniper solution against Mansell.

2. CRIMSON TWO / I-DOME BERM RELOCATION

Mansell’s mobile observation post has moved from the earlier separate high-ground marker
into the fortified bermed I-Dome autonomous air-defense site. This site occupies the
highest point of the island in current pre-transform reference geometry.

The I-Dome site repurposes the real-reference south-tennis-court area. A several-foot
berm surrounds the site on all sides except a narrow southern entry gap. The berm,
I-Dome battery and Mansell observation post now share one fortified high-point space.

Geometry review result: ACCEPTED. The relocation does not break the mission spine.
Approximate current reference-space relationships remain viable:
- staging -> Mansell: ~339 m;
- Temple firing position -> Mansell: ~240-260 m;
- southern-flagpole vantage -> Mansell: ~500 m;
- Mansell remains meaningfully separated from the guesthouse / residence finale route.

The old concept of using the I-Dome berm itself as a firing platform against a farther
uphill Mansell position is superseded. The berm is now the fortified CRIMSON TWO
objective space.

3. SOUTHERN-FLAGPOLE LONG-RANGE VANTAGE

A new vantage point near the southern flagpole provides another optional solution to
CRIMSON TWO. It is substantially farther from Mansell than the Temple Pavilion lane and
should read as a difficult, rewarding long-range shot rather than the default solution.

Current design intent:

7. THREEHALF HEAVY Q-UGV — OPTIONAL ESCALATION

ThreeHalf is now a source-backed heavy autonomous UGV security threat.

Protected mission behavior:
- ThreeHalves normally remain docked and dormant;
- once active, they are extremely tough to disable;
- the player can avoid them entirely;
- reckless repeated aggressive engagement of armed enforcer UAVs can escalate the local
  defense state enough to activate ThreeHalves into combat mode;
- direct attack on a dormant ThreeHalf can also trigger activation;
- if Victor Two Bravo reaches a docking station stealthily while the unit remains docked
  and on standby, Tripper can sabotage its power-management circuitry;
- successful sabotage shuts the ThreeHalf down without waking it;
- sabotage is optional and rewards stealth / restraint rather than becoming a mandatory
  objective.
