DETAILED MAP / ENCOUNTER LOCKS

1. TEMPLE PAVILION BRANCH

The Temple Pavilion remains an optional navigational branch. Exterior access is locked;
the player can reach Goldberg's office through the service-shed tunnel, stairs and
concealed movable-bookcase route, then open the pavilion back toward the exterior.

The unnamed non-thermal M200 and low-wall firing position provide one practical
counter-sniper solution against Mansell.

2. CRIMSON TWO / I-DOME BERM

Mansell's mobile observation post shares the fortified bermed I-Dome autonomous
air-defense site at the island high point in current reference geometry.

The site repurposes the real-reference south-tennis-court area. A several-foot berm
surrounds the site except for the narrow southern entry gap.

Approximate viable reference-space relationships:
- staging -> Mansell: ~339 m;
- Temple firing position -> Mansell: ~240-260 m;
- southern-flagpole vantage -> Mansell: ~500 m.

The older concept of using the berm itself as a firing platform against a separate
farther-uphill Mansell position is superseded. The berm is now CRIMSON TWO's fortified
objective space.

3. SOUTHERN-FLAGPOLE LONG-RANGE VANTAGE

The southern flagpole provides an additional optional long-range solution against
Mansell. It is substantially harder than the Temple lane. The Temple M200 makes the shot
more manageable; a GRAY GHOST attempt may remain possible at much higher difficulty.
Exact ballistics, first-shot thresholds and acoustic-location timing remain production
tuning unless separately locked later.

4. REGENT CACHE COORDINATE FRAGMENTS

The Regent flagpole-base cache remains optional and preserves its established payload.
Eight bench-based coordinate fragments can reveal its location. The southern-flagpole
bench carries W64D, deliberately reducing central clustering.

5. GUESTHOUSE / LOCAL IFF

The reference-space Underwood approach remains the northwestern guesthouse/service side.
Final cardinal terminology must be regenerated after the east-west production mirror.

Underwood's access card may allow local IFF manipulation against Graves. The exact
IFF-assisted outcome remains implementation-open: suppression, injury, displacement or
defeat are all possible; direct combat remains valid.

7. THREEHALF HEAVY Q-UGV — OPTIONAL ESCALATION

ThreeHalf is a source-backed heavy autonomous UGV security threat.

Protected behavior:

- extremely difficult to disable once active;
- entirely avoidable in SINS OF OUR FATHERS;
- reckless repeated aggressive engagement of armed enforcer UAVs can escalate local
  security enough to activate ThreeHalves into combat mode;
