2. It distinguishes finding a usable aircraft from obtaining physical access to it.
3. It gives Smoke a pilot-specific reason to foreground fuel and immediate readiness.

5. It lets Spook make the manager-office solution feel planned rather than improvised.

PROTECTED LINE

The canonical Spook line is:
