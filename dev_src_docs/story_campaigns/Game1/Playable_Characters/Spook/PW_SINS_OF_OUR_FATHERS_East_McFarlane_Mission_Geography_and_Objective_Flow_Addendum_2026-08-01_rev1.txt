infiltration spine with an optional western intelligence loop:

engine-off floatplane landing
-> concealed staging and security assessment
-> direct high-ground route or western pavilion route
-> CRIMSON TWO / Rip overwatch encounter
-> optional Regent cache
-> eastern-beach foliage approach
-> CRIMSON THREE / Underwood guesthouse ambush

-> CRIMSON ONE / Graves main-residence encounter
-> residence intelligence search
-> courtyard and driveway crossing

The map should be treated as the objective skeleton, not a production-ready level.
The eight points are sufficient to lock relative placement and cause-and-effect
order, but they do not yet define traversal lines, patrol volumes, encounter
boundaries, checkpoint logic, interiors or cinematic trigger spaces.

CORRECTED PLACEMARK INTERPRETATION

1. COVERT FLOATPLANE LANDING AND ENTRY

Smoke coasts the BFA-2 floatplane to an engine-off landing. Victor Two Bravo enters
through an unlit, disused boat launch without using an official CIA route.

This is a short, controlled infiltration opening. The team must have enough space
to unload, establish security and leave the aircraft before the player reaches the
staging node. Exact water approach, landing animation, aircraft concealment and
failure-state presentation remain open.

2. CONCEALED STAGING AREA

Tripper assesses physical security and the island’s closed local network while the
team finalizes the approach. This is a briefing-in-motion and route-choice node, not
a long static command scene.

The player should understand:

- the main residence approach is heavily surveilled;
- drones and automated weapons protect open routes;
- the western pavilion may contain intelligence and useful equipment;
- Rip controls high ground;
- foliage and service architecture provide the safer final approach.

3. CRIMSON TWO — RIP OVERWATCH POSITION

Lawrence “Rip” Mansell occupies a mobile observation tower near the sundial at the
island’s highest elevation. His encounter preserves the locked CRIMSON TWO
precision role.

EXTREME PREJUDICE, Rip’s personal CheyTac M200 Intervention, remains at the
observation position after his defeat and is recoverable by the player under the
named-weapon rules.

Presentation must preserve Game 1 anonymity. The authoring source knows Rip’s legal
identity; Spook does not receive a clean legal identification from the body because
CFIM destroys identifying facial evidence on death.

4. WESTERN TEMPLE PAVILION / GOLDBERG OFFICE

The western pavilion contains:

- a secure office used by Goldberg;
- optional mission intelligence and environmental evidence;
- a separate unnamed CheyTac M200 Intervention.

The pavilion rifle is not EXTREME PREJUDICE. It is a separately serialized island
security weapon. The player may use it to engage Rip from the pavilion sightline,
then later recover EXTREME PREJUDICE from his observation post.

The pavilion is an optional advantage and investigation space, not a mandatory
second main residence. Exact office contents, access method, enemy presence and
interior footprint remain open.

5. WESTERN CAPE POOL-HOUSE FLAGPOLE CACHE

The source-map phrase “eastern cape” is geographically incorrect for the marked
point. The repository artifact normalizes it to the western cape.

A loose stone block in the flagpole base conceals a weatherproof package containing:

- one encrypted USB drive;
- one unsigned handwritten note;
- one specific symbol;
- one set of coordinates that does not correspond to any Game 1 objective.

Finding the package sets the protected cross-game hidden-quest state associated
with Regent’s unredacted Riptide Red report. The symbol remains seeded at Montoya
Farm and reappears as an environmental access key in Game 2.

The cache is optional, easy to miss and off the main objective path. It must not be
converted into an automatically highlighted mandatory pickup.

6. EASTERN-BEACH APPROACH / CRIMSON THREE GUESTHOUSE ENCOUNTER

Victor Two Bravo uses foliage along the eastern beach because the principal approach
is heavily surveilled and covered by automated weapons.

The team enters the northeastern service side of the residence property through an
apparently dark guesthouse. Dermot “Lucky” Underwood / CRIMSON THREE initiates the
encounter with a stun grenade, creating a disorienting close-quarters fight.

After defeating Underwood, the player may recover his access card and open a
concealed security-monitoring room.

Underwood remains the breacher / support War Wolf. His established suppressed
compact 5.56 package remains the default loadout unless a later dedicated source
revises it.

7. SECURITY ROOM AND LOCAL IFF MANIPULATION

The guesthouse security room exposes the island’s closed property-security system:

- video feeds;
- drone and automated-weapon coverage;
- access-control state;
- local IFF threat profiles protecting the main residence and courtyard.

This is Goldberg’s isolated private security architecture. It is not a casual
connection into PANDORA, AEGIS, U.S. military systems or a wider public network.

Using Underwood’s access card, the player may alter the local IFF profile and turn
part of the property defense against Graves. Direct combat must remain possible.
The exact degree of remote lethality, player interaction, UI, timing and encounter
balance remain open.

8. CRIMSON ONE — GRAVES MAIN-RESIDENCE ENCOUNTER

Richard “Digger” Graves waits in prepared cover on the first-floor mezzanine of the
main residence entry hall.

The player has two authored solution families:

- direct close-quarters assault against a prepared senior operator;
- security-room exploitation that degrades, distracts, wounds or potentially
  defeats him through the local automated-defense system.

The IFF route should feel like earned intelligence exploitation, not a universal
“hack to win” button. Final tuning must preserve a rewarding direct fight while
making prior reconnaissance materially useful.

After the encounter, the player may search the residence for intelligence before
crossing the courtyard to the helipad.

9. FINAL HELIPAD CONFRONTATION

Spook reaches Edward Goldberg and Gillian Stockbridge as they prepare to leave by
helicopter. The confrontation confirms that the shipment chain, Montoya Farm,
KINGMAKER, the Shadow Underground and PANDORA belong to the same protected
architecture.

Macmillan / Bishop orders Spook to stand down and grant Goldberg safe passage. The
protected slip remains:

“Stand down, Spook. This is bigger than Mullen told you.”

Spook refuses the order. Bishop declares Victor Two Bravo rogue. The intervention,
compromised command state and helicopter departure prevent the team from arresting
or killing Goldberg and Stockbridge before they escape.

Do not rewrite this as willing compliance by Spook. The mission ends on betrayal,
loss of institutional protection and the widening West Coast assault.

LOCKED PLAYABLE FLOW

PHASE 1 — ENGINE-OFF ARRIVAL

- Smoke lands the floatplane without engine power at the disused boat launch.
- Victor Two Bravo disembarks, secures the immediate area and moves inland.
- The opening establishes isolation, heat, darkness and deliberate lack of formal
  support.

PHASE 2 — STAGING AND ROUTE CHOICE

- Tripper assesses local physical and network security.
- The team identifies the high-ground threat and heavily surveilled main approach.
- The player may move directly toward Rip or take the longer western pavilion route.
- The route choice changes information, equipment and engagement geometry, not the
  finale outcome.

PHASE 3A — DIRECT HIGH-GROUND ROUTE

- Victor Two Bravo closes on Rip’s observation sector using terrain and cover.
- The player defeats CRIMSON TWO in a precision / counter-overwatch encounter.
- EXTREME PREJUDICE becomes recoverable at the observation post.
- The western pavilion and Regent cache remain optional detours.

PHASE 3B — WESTERN INTELLIGENCE ROUTE

- The player infiltrates the temple pavilion.
- Optional intelligence clarifies Goldberg’s activity and island security.
- The player may take the separate unnamed M200 and use the pavilion-to-tower
  sightline against Rip.
- The player may continue to the western pool-house flagpole and discover the
  Regent cache.
- The branch rejoins the residence approach without changing the protected ending.

PHASE 4 — EASTERN-BEACH INFILTRATION

- The team avoids the exposed main approach.
- Foliage, shoreline terrain and service access conceal movement toward the
  guesthouse.
- Drones and local automated defenses create surveillance pressure rather than an
  unavoidable open firefight.

PHASE 5 — UNDERWOOD AMBUSH

- The apparently deserted guesthouse becomes the CRIMSON THREE encounter space.
- Underwood’s stun-grenade initiation disrupts vision, hearing and team formation.
- The player defeats him and may recover the access card.
- The encounter must preserve Underwood’s breacher / support identity.

PHASE 6 — SECURITY EXPLOITATION

- The player finds the concealed monitoring room.
- Video feeds reveal the main residence and Graves’s prepared position.
- The access card permits optional IFF manipulation.
- The choice changes the Graves encounter but does not bypass the residence as a
  narrative space.

PHASE 7 — GRAVES AND RESIDENCE SEARCH

- Victor Two Bravo enters the main residence.
- CRIMSON ONE confronts the team from the mezzanine.
- Direct combat and security exploitation remain valid.
- Optional intelligence search occurs after or around the encounter without
  collapsing finale momentum.

PHASE 8 — HELIPAD BETRAYAL

- The team crosses the courtyard and driveway.
- Goldberg and Stockbridge are confronted at the helicopter.
- Bishop issues the stand-down order and makes the Mullen slip.
- Spook refuses.
- Victor Two Bravo is declared rogue.
- Goldberg and Stockbridge escape.
- The West Coast assault widens the ending beyond the island.

OPEN MAP AND LEVEL-DEVELOPMENT REQUIREMENTS

The following requirements are deliberately recorded as open. They must be resolved
or explicitly deferred before the mission is treated as production-ready.

A. GEOMETRY AND TRAVERSAL

- Draw the primary direct route, western optional loop, eastern-beach approach,
  residence route and any permitted backtracking paths.
- Define terrain grades, climbable surfaces, shoreline limits, vegetation density,
  concealment reliability and readable traversal landmarks.
- Define route blockers, soft gates, one-way drops and the point after which the
  western cache can no longer be reached.
- Establish whether the player can revisit Rip’s tower after using the pavilion
  sightline and whether EXTREME PREJUDICE can always be recovered.
- Define the final no-return boundary before the helipad.
- Fictionalize paths, structures and security sufficiently that East McFarlane is
  not a one-to-one recreation of the real reference property.

B. ENCOUNTER AND AI SPACES

- Draw patrol areas, combat polygons, reinforcement routes, fallback positions and
  alert-state propagation.
- Define ordinary island-security personnel and their relationship to the three
  War Wolf encounters.
- Define Victor Two Bravo companion formation, separation and reunion behavior,
  especially after Underwood’s stun grenade.
- Define failure, recovery and checkpoint logic for stealth breaks and route
  transitions.
- Prevent optional-route completion order from creating softlocks or duplicate
  boss states.

C. DRONES AND AUTOMATED DEFENSES

- Select drone types, sensors, patrol paths, detection cones and alert escalation.

- Select automated-weapon types, firing arcs, ammunition limits and safety zones.
- Define the local network topology and exactly what Tripper can observe or alter.
- Define the IFF interaction, feedback, permanence and consequence if used against
  Graves.
- Confirm that the property-security system cannot imply access to PANDORA or other

D. WAR WOLF ENCOUNTERS

RESULT

The normalized KML and this source provide a strong, canon-compatible foundation for
the detailed SINS OF OUR FATHERS finale pass. The macro route, route-choice concept,
three differentiated War Wolf encounters, optional Regent cache, residence-security
exploitation and protected helipad ending are now landed. The mission remains
explicitly not production-ready until the open geometry, encounter, systems,
interior, cinematic and accessibility requirements above are developed.
