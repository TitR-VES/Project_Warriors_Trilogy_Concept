1. COVERT FLOATPLANE LANDING

Smoke coasts the BFA-2 floatplane to an engine-off landing at an unlit, disused boat

space and failure presentation remain open.

2. CONCEALED STAGING AREA

Tripper assesses physical security and Goldberg’s isolated local network while the
team finalizes the approach. This is a briefing-in-motion and route-choice node.

3. CRIMSON TWO — RIP

Rip occupies a mobile observation tower near the sundial at the island’s highest
elevation. The encounter preserves his overwatch / precision role. EXTREME
PREJUDICE remains recoverable at his observation post after his defeat.

4. TEMPLE PAVILION / GOLDBERG OFFICE

The pavilion contains Goldberg’s secure office, optional intelligence and one
separate unnamed CheyTac M200 Intervention. The rifle is not EXTREME PREJUDICE.
It may be used against Rip from the pavilion sightline; EXTREME PREJUDICE remains a
distinct later recovery at Rip’s position.

5. WESTERN-CAPE REGENT CACHE

A loose stone in the pool-house flagpole base conceals a weatherproof package with:

- one encrypted USB drive;
- one unsigned handwritten note;
- one unfamiliar symbol;
- coordinates that do not correspond to a Game 1 objective.

The cache is optional, easy to miss and tied to the protected cross-game quest state.

6. CRIMSON THREE — UNDERWOOD

Victor Two Bravo approaches along the eastern beach under foliage cover because the
main access route is heavily surveilled and protected by automated defenses.
Underwood attacks in the dark guesthouse with a stun grenade, initiating a
disorienting close-quarters fight. His access card opens the concealed monitoring
room after his defeat.

7. GOLDBERG’S PRIVATE SECURITY ARCHITECTURE

The guesthouse room controls Goldberg’s closed property-security architecture:

- cameras and video feeds;
- drones and automated weapons;
- access control;
- local IFF profiles covering the residence and courtyard.

The architecture is privately sourced, protected from PANDORA, fully controlled by
Goldberg and isolated from PANDORA, AEGIS, U.S. military systems and wider strategic
networks. Underwood’s card may permit optional IFF manipulation against Graves.
Direct combat remains valid. Exact lethality, UI and balance remain open.

8. CRIMSON ONE — GRAVES

Graves waits in prepared cover on the main-residence entry-hall mezzanine. The
player may assault him directly or exploit the local IFF system. The intelligence
route must feel earned rather than functioning as a universal bypass.

9. FINAL HELIPAD CONFRONTATION

Macmillan / Bishop orders Spook to stand down and grant Goldberg safe passage.
Spook refuses. Bishop declares Victor Two Bravo rogue. The intervention,
compromised command situation and helicopter departure prevent the team from
stopping Goldberg and Stockbridge. Do not describe Spook as willingly complying.
