PURPOSE

Define the valid but non-recommended fallback path when the manager is killed inside
his locked office during the alerted Variant B approach or breach.

This contingency preserves player freedom and mission continuity when the manager is
fatally struck before Victor Two Bravo can complete the intended live apprehension and
interrogation. It does not make killing the manager the preferred, rewarded, or most
informative solution.

================================================================================
VALID FALLBACK TRIGGERS
================================================================================

The deceased-manager fallback may activate if the manager is killed inside the locked
office through player or team action during the alerted approach, including:

- rounds penetrating the destructible wooden office door before the coordinated
  breach;
- return fire and crossfire through a heavily damaged doorway;
- the player destroying enough of the door or surrounding opening to throw an
  explosive device into the office;
- blast, fragmentation, or other lethal damage during a freeform premature entry;
- another supported combat-system interaction that kills the manager before Spook can
  disarm and secure him alive.

The exact lethal mechanism does not alter the recovery objective. Once the manager is
dead, the mission must transition cleanly into body search and key recovery rather
than fail solely because the authored interrogation can no longer occur.

This route is valid player expression but remains tactically and narratively inferior
to capturing the manager alive.

================================================================================
CONSEQUENCE AND NON-REWARD RULE
================================================================================

Manager death must not be presented as the optimal shortcut.

Consequences include:

- the live interrogation and its performance are lost;
- the manager cannot provide the spoken aircraft-location confirmation;
- the room may be more damaged and dangerous;
- the police-call consequence remains governed by whether the call completed before
  death;
- any already-dispatched police cars and Coast Guard patrol boat remain active;
- no bonus, special reward, cleaner objective state, or faster automatic key grant is
  created by killing him;
- the player must still enter, clear, approach, and physically search the body.

The fallback exists to prevent an artificial mission failure or continuity break when
the game's destructible environment and player freedom produce a lethal outcome.

================================================================================
ROOM ENTRY AND BODY CONFIRMATION
================================================================================

After the manager is killed, Victor Two Bravo must still gain entry to the office.

Depending on door state, the team may:

- complete the authored Tripper / Smoke breach if the door remains mechanically valid;
- transition through a player-created opening if the door has been sufficiently
  destroyed;
- use the existing freeform-entry recovery path if the authored BREACH prompt has
  become invalid.

The team does not assume the keys have been destroyed or automatically transferred.
The office must be made safe enough for interaction.

Before SEARCH becomes available:

- immediate armed threats in the office or doorway must be resolved;
- the manager's death state must be confirmed;
- the body must remain reachable;
- the key bundle must remain associated with the manager's left jacket pocket even if
  body physics have shifted his final pose.

The body is not removed, replaced, or silently converted into a generic loot marker.

================================================================================
SEARCH PROMPT AND KEY RECOVERY
================================================================================

Once Spook is close enough to the deceased manager and the immediate room state is
safe, the player receives:

    SEARCH

The interaction targets the manager's body and specifically resolves the left jacket
pocket that contains the N272BT key bundle.

Activating SEARCH triggers a short first-person animation in which Spook:

- crouches or leans toward the body as required by its final position;
- checks the manager's left jacket pocket;
- retrieves the complete Ocean Blue Sky Tours key bundle;
- visibly confirms possession before standing or returning to ready posture.

The recovered object remains the same tracked prop used in the live paths:

- up to four differently shaped keys;
- scratched metal split ring;
- compact float-compartment drain / inspection-fitting mini wrench;
- worn white plastic tag marked `FBA-2C / N272BT`.

No individual component may detach, disappear, or be replaced by a generic key icon.

KEY-RECOVERY FAIL-SAFES

- The SEARCH volume follows the body if physics move it within the valid office area.
- If the body clips against the desk, wall, or damaged door, an enlarged but visually
  credible interaction volume preserves access.
- The keys cannot be destroyed by ordinary authored door splintering, grenade effects,
  or body movement.
- A checkpoint cannot restore a dead manager without a searchable key state.
- SEARCH cannot complete without transferring the full bundle to Spook.
- The mission cannot advance to aircraft startup while key ownership is undefined.

================================================================================
VARIANT A-STYLE HANDOVER TO SMOKE
================================================================================

After SEARCH completes, this fallback uses the established Variant A physical
handover rather than Variant B's in-room toss.

POSITIONING

- Smoke moves to or remains at the safe covered position just inside the damaged
  office doorway.
- Smoke covers the first-floor corridor and immediate exit lane.
- Tripper confirms the room, body, and any surviving or incapacitated threats are
  secure enough for departure.
- Because the manager is dead, Tripper performs no manager-restraint animation.
- Spook approaches Smoke under normal first-person locomotion with the recovered key
  bundle in his possession.

HANDOVER BEAT

When Spook reaches the valid short-range handover position:

- the camera gives the same restrained first-person glance used by Variant A;
- Spook extends the complete key bundle toward Smoke;
- Spook says:

    SPOOK
    You might wanna take these, Smoke.

- Smoke accepts the bundle physically and secures it on his person;
- the internal keyholder state transfers from Spook to Smoke;
- control returns immediately and the team continues toward N272BT.

This fallback does not use:

    Smoke! Catch!

and does not use the Variant B toss animation. The calmer direct handover is more
credible after the team has entered, confirmed the manager dead, and completed the
body search.
