AUTHORITY RULE

Draft F controls the interior room arrangement, circulation, ready-room passages,
service-core relationships, kitchen counter direction, and window program. The exterior
reference package controls broad building massing, facade rhythm, arcade, gallery,
materials, roof character, weathering, and surrounding street language. The KML controls
where the apartment sits inside the exterior shell. The cutaway concept is a visual test
of those sources, not a replacement for them.

CUTAWAY COMPOSITION

- The building is shown from an elevated front-corner isometric view.
- The left/non-safehouse second-floor portion retains an intact roof section.
- The right-hand second-floor safehouse occupancy is exposed as a roof-off cutaway.
- The ground-floor arcade, pale teal/blue walls, white trim, upper timber character,
  neighboring pink building, street edge, parked vehicles, and utility context derive
  from the accepted exterior visual language.
- The exposed apartment is represented as a gameplay-authored interior inside that
  exterior shell.
