LOCK 01 — KYSC WRIST MARKING
================================================================================

The five KYSC subjects encountered in PANDORA'S BOX carry the supplied three-glyph
pattern on their wrists.

This is a deliberate recurring program mark, not incidental body art.

Production intent:

- the marking should be visually readable at close investigative distance;
- the player does not need a forced cinematic close-up for the motif to exist;
- observation, body inspection, environmental photography, medical documentation,

- the marking functions as an early visual clue that the KYSC subjects belong to a

LOCK 03 — KINGMAKER SYMBOL
================================================================================

KINGMAKER has its own separate recurring symbol inside the Montoya Farm underground
complex.

The KINGMAKER mark is a stylized minimalist representation derived from the upper-wing
pattern / markings of a monarch butterfly.

The design has two intended layers:

- a direct visual / verbal play on the KING element of KINGMAKER through the monarch
  butterfly; and
- a deliberate allusion to the alleged real-world "MONARCH" mind-control program as it
  is described in conspiracy literature, where it is often claimed to be a later
  evolution or continuation of MKULTRA.

REAL-WORLD REFERENCE BOUNDARY

The Project Warriors use of MONARCH is an allusive fictional / cultural reference. This
source does not assert that the alleged real-world MONARCH program has been verified as
historical fact. The in-universe Montoya / KINGMAKER architecture remains fictional and
must not depend on presenting contested conspiracy claims as established real history.

The monarch-butterfly derivation should remain visually restrained and minimalist. It
should not become a literal butterfly illustration unless a later art-direction source
chooses that treatment. The mark needs to read first as a covert institutional symbol
and only secondarily reward players who recognize the monarch reference.

LOCK 04 — RELATIONSHIP BETWEEN THE TWO MOTIFS
================================================================================

The KYSC wrist motif and the KINGMAKER monarch-derived symbol are distinct marks.

They may appear in proximity within Montoya Farm because both belong to the hidden
program's visual ecosystem, but production must preserve their separate identities.

Do not:

- redraw the KYSC wrist pattern into a butterfly;
- treat the wrist mark as merely a simplified KINGMAKER logo;
- place the KINGMAKER mark on the KYSC wrists unless separately established later;
- infer that every person or object carrying one motif must carry the other;
- invent a hierarchy between the two marks without source support.

Their co-presence is useful precisely because it suggests multiple layers inside the
same institution: one mark can follow conditioned human subjects while the other can
identify the broader KINGMAKER architecture.
