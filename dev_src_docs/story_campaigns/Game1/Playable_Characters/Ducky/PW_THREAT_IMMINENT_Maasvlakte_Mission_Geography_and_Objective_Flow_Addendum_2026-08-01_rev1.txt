PLACEMARK INTERPRETATION

1. MAASVLAKTE ROTTERDAM
General setting anchor for the finale.

2. CONTESTED NMESIS POSITION
The player must secure the camouflaged launcher and mobile command post in a small
employee parking lot. Ducky manually prepares and launches the Naval Strike Missile.

3–6. RETREAT, HELL HANDOFF
The Zubr landing, Dutch Marine observer rendezvous, RHIB embarkation and RHIB landing
form the transition from the pre-finale into THREAT IMMINENT. Preserve the mission
title punctuation: RETREAT, HELL.

7. PORT-DEFENSE FORWARD COMMAND
Dutch Navy and Marine Corps personnel brief Ducky on the endangered NMESIS battery
and the wider terminal-defense situation. This is the finale's explicit opening
briefing and objective assignment.

8–11. ECT DELTA ADVANCE
AEGIS supports Dutch Marines across a terminal service yard, advances under smoke,
secures ECT Delta and exits through the northern container-yard gate. Containers,
service vehicles, railcars and terminal equipment define the cover language; do not
reintroduce airport, runway or aircraft-apron geography.

10, 14 AND 16. FROST REPOSITIONING
Frost's three mapped positions describe his movement through the battle. The first
two are NPC overwatch states. The third position on the power-plant roof is the
transition point for his playable sequence.

12–13 AND 23. NAVAL-FIRE DIVERSION
Russian naval gunfire strikes the transformer station and later the eastern half of
a quay-adjacent building. The first strike forces the team east toward Coloradoweg
fire station. The second demonstrates improving correction and supports the need to
eliminate the enemy observation team.

15 AND 21. SCOUTS AND FORWARD OBSERVERS
A UAV-equipped scout team near the power-plant cargo bay reveals Russian reconnaissance
activity. A separate team farther north occupies a pipeline and storage-tank position
and corrects naval gunfire. Frost's playable section requires deliberate target
identification and elimination of this observation team.

17–20. NMESIS CLIMAX
Lupin occupies an elevated storage-tank position before Ducky and Switchblade assault
NMESIS. Ducky launches at Severomorsk. An initial Spetsnaz RPG shock element disables
and overturns the command post. Lupin's playable section begins after launch and
covers Ducky's escape. The player then returns to Ducky and fights Kapitan's main
Spetsnaz team while moving to the Dutch regroup point.

19. SEVEROMORSK
The marked offshore position is the Naval Strike Missile impact reference. The strike
forces Severomorsk to withdraw in flames; it is not a confirmed sinking.

24. APM / AEGIS INFRASTRUCTURE
The APM terminal complex supplies telecommunications, computing, relay, logistics and
vessel-tasking infrastructure integrated into AEGIS. Critical hardware is below the
surface, but the surface complex, connectivity and power remain dependencies. Holding
territory is insufficient if the node becomes inoperable. This protected asset ties
the finale directly to Ducky's infrastructure-defense campaign.
