================================================================================
PURPOSE
================================================================================

This source locks the maritime interception that forms the Danish-waters arm of
PERSISTENT THREAT's AEGIS FALLS finale as a playable mainline mission in the SEA
SPEAR DLC.

The purpose is to let the player experience first-hand the canon operation that
Ducky's campaign presents from the parallel Aix-en-Provence arm and through the
ORACLE-routed live helmet-camera feed.

This is a perspective expansion of one shared event. It is not a second version of
the operation, an alternate outcome, or an additional mission in Ducky's campaign.

================================================================================
CONTROLLING CROSS-CAMPAIGN RELATIONSHIP
================================================================================

In PERSISTENT THREAT:

- AEGIS FALLS remains Mission 13 and the campaign finale;
- the Aix-en-Provence estate operation and Danish maritime interception remain two
  simultaneous arms of one synchronized compound mission;
- AEGIS squad remains at Tremblay's estate;
- Ducky experiences the maritime result through ORACLE, TRU/SIGHT, and the secure
  live helmet-camera feed used during the Tremblay confrontation.

In SEA SPEAR:

- the player directly experiences the Danish maritime arm;
- Grim remains the sole primary playable protagonist;
- Rime, Undertow, and Lantern remain Grim's Nereid Black squadmates;
- SHIELD operates with or immediately behind Nereid Black as the exploitation,
  render-safe, recovery, and evidence-preservation element;
- the Royal Danish Navy's support-vessel interception remains a parallel allied
  action communicated through the shared command picture rather than replacing the
  deployment-vessel mission space.

Both campaigns depict the same timestamps, intervention trigger, operational result,
and evidence package.

================================================================================
SEA SPEAR MISSION LOCK
================================================================================

SEA SPEAR must include a mandatory playable mission covering the maritime
interception in Danish territorial waters against the Russian-backed shadow-fleet
package staging an attack on a strategically important undersea cable terminal.

The exact mission title, ordinal position, and surrounding SEA SPEAR ladder remain
open. The mission is chronologically fixed to the AEGIS FALLS execution window and
must occur when SEA SPEAR's campaign chronology reaches that Game 2 event.

The playable perspective is Grim as Nereid Black team lead / primary operator. The
mission should preserve Nereid Black's maritime identity and should not reduce the
operation to a generic surface boarding action.

================================================================================
MINIMUM PLAYABLE STRUCTURE
================================================================================

The final production design may use boarding, diving, remote systems, or a mixed
approach, but the playable mission must contain the following dramatic and causal
beats:

1. PRE-INTERVENTION HOLD
   Nereid Black and SHIELD are already in an assured interception position while
   ORACLE and TRU/SIGHT preserve the covert track. The player understands that the
   target vessels have disabled their transponders but remain under non-cooperative
   observation.

2. INTERVENTION RED LINE
   The hostile staging is complete and the sabotage device begins preparation for
   deployment. ORACLE gives the synchronized execution signal. The operation does
   not wait for the device to be placed or the cable terminal to be endangered.

3. APPROACH AND ENTRY
   Grim leads Nereid Black onto or into the covert deployment vessel. The approach
   must exploit the team's maritime specialization and distinguish the mission from
   WIRECUTTER and from a conventional naval infantry boarding.

4. SHIPBOARD FIGHT
   The Russian covert sabotage team resists. Nereid Black clears the vessel through
   confined maritime spaces while maintaining target discrimination around any
   legitimate, coerced, or noncombatant crew.

5. DEVICE SECURITY
   The sabotage device is secured before deployment or detonation. SHIELD moves into
   the technical and exploitation space to render it safe, preserve control systems,
   and capture targeting, navigation, timing, communications, and deployment data.

6. SUPPORT-VESSEL CONFIRMATION
   The shared command picture confirms that the Royal Danish Navy has intercepted
   the support vessel and established control. This remains a parallel operation,
   not a second player-controlled target by default.

7. LIVE-FEED BRIDGE
   ORACLE routes authenticated Nereid Black and SHIELD helmet-camera imagery into
   the Aix arm. The SEA SPEAR player sees the originating side of the feed later
   shown by Ducky to Tremblay. The scene should remain operational and understated;
   no character needs to explain the entire estate confrontation.

8. EVIDENCE-PRESERVATION CLOSE
   The mission ends only after the vessel, device, surviving personnel, and relevant
   technical spaces are under allied control and the evidence stream is stable.

NEREID BLACK ROLE EMPHASIS
================================================================================

GRIM
- Sole playable character.
- Leads movement, target discrimination, and the decisive assault path.
- Carries the player's first-hand responsibility for stopping the device before it
  reaches the cable infrastructure.

RIME
- Maritime breacher and technically fluent entry specialist.
- Provides a natural role in hull, hatch, compartment, or flooded-access problems.

UNDERTOW
- Assault medic and maritime operator.
- Supports casualty control and movement through spaces where both hands, flotation,
  and medical access matter.

LANTERN
- Maritime reconnaissance and overwatch specialist.
- Helps identify behavioral wrongness, vessel activity, and threat patterns before
  labels are available.

SHIELD
- Owns render-safe, exploitation, sensitive-site recovery, and evidence preservation
  after Nereid Black creates access and control.
- Should feel colder and more procedural than Nereid Black without displacing Grim's
  playable leadership.

================================================================================
CONTINUITY AND PORTRAYAL RULES
================================================================================

Protect the following:

- The deployment vessel is the player's primary mission space.
- Nereid Black leads the physical assault.
- SHIELD secures the device and evidence.
- The Royal Danish Navy controls the support vessel.
- ORACLE is the command bridge.
- TRU/SIGHT is the real-time intelligence and evidentiary bridge.
- Tremblay's ZEUS-level override suppresses warning propagation but does not erase
  raw tracks or make the vessels invisible.
- The operation launches at staging complete / device preparation beginning.
- The sabotage device is stopped before deployment.
- The same helmet-camera feed becomes Ducky's live proof during the Aix confrontation.
- Tremblay remains a coerced but culpable accessory, not the sabotage mastermind.
- Support-vessel contractor nationality is not evidence of criminality. Distinguish
  legitimate or coerced maritime crew, falsely registered contractor security, and
  the small Russian or Russian-aligned coordination element.

Do not:

- create a different outcome for SEA SPEAR;
- let the player fail into a canon branch where the device is deployed;
- move AEGIS squad onto the maritime target;
- turn the Danish interception into a separate Ducky ladder mission;
- replace Grim with a SHIELD playable perspective;
- make the live-feed bridge theatrical or expositional;
- collapse this mission into a replay of WIRECUTTER's exact structure.
