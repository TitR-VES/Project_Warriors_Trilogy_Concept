================================================================================
DECISION
================================================================================

The current working title for the mandatory SEA SPEAR mission depicting the
Danish-waters maritime arm of PERSISTENT THREAT's AEGIS FALLS finale is:

DEEP CUT

The title applies to the playable Grim / Nereid Black interception of the covert
deployment vessel before its sabotage device can be deployed against the strategic
undersea cable terminal.

================================================================================
TITLE STATUS
================================================================================

DEEP CUT is a working title, not yet a final canon title lock.

Use DEEP CUT in current SEA SPEAR development, mission-ladder drafts, synopsis work,
and cross-campaign references unless a later dedicated naming pass replaces or
formally locks it.

The title does not resolve the mission's exact ordinal position inside the still-
unfinalized SEA SPEAR ladder.

================================================================================
TITLE FIT
================================================================================

DEEP CUT fits the mission through several overlapping meanings:

- a hostile attempt to sever strategically important undersea infrastructure;
- Nereid Black cutting into a concealed shadow-fleet operation at the decisive
  deployment moment;
- a maritime assault that penetrates beyond the vessels' commercial cover and
  exposes the deeper evidentiary architecture behind the attack;
- a concise mission-title register consistent with the operation's controlled,
  technical, and physically confined character.

The title should not imply that the cable is successfully cut. The canon outcome
remains that Nereid Black and SHIELD secure the vessel and sabotage device before
deployment.

================================================================================
CONTINUITY RULE
================================================================================

The working-title decision changes no event causality, chronology, role ownership,
or outcome.

DEEP CUT remains the SEA SPEAR playable perspective on the Danish maritime arm of
AEGIS FALLS. AEGIS squad remains at Aix-en-Provence; Grim remains the sole playable
protagonist; Nereid Black leads the deployment-vessel assault; SHIELD owns render-
safe and technical exploitation; the Royal Danish Navy controls the support vessel;
and the authenticated helmet-camera feed is routed to Ducky during the Tremblay
confrontation.
