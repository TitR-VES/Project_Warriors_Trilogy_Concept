3. OPERATION HOLE PUNCH — CHRONOLOGICAL PLACEMENT

Operation HOLE PUNCH occurs after Spook's Game 1 campaign and before the opening phase of Spook's Game 2 GODKILLER campaign.

Goldberg has surrendered to US authorities, accepted a deliberately reduced prosecution, and begun serving his sentence at Coleman. Los Sietes then execute a dual-purpose prison operation:

- a visible assault and extraction centered on El Furtivo creates disorder and draws the institutional response;
- a quieter second track, enabled by compromised staff, removes Goldberg, substitutes a destroyed body, and establishes his false death.

The public outcome remains unchanged: Goldberg is declared dead, disappears underground, and begins rebuilding his network for an intelligence counter-offensive against Crodyn and his circle.

4. LA AVISPA'S ROLE

La Avispa is not required to be physically present with the breakout crew. Her strongest function is upstream intelligence preparation.

Using the surveillance, pattern-analysis, source-handling, and identity-tracing skills developed during her former FSB career and later refined inside Los Sietes, she supports Bisutrí's planning by:

- determining Goldberg's assigned cell and exact position within the relevant prison block;
- reconstructing Goldberg's daily movement pattern, medical access, guard rotations, and predictable institutional routines through prison-gang and correctional-system contacts;
- identifying the narrow window in which compromised staff can move Goldberg from his assigned cell to the medical wing under a routine pretext;
- locating a physically suitable decedent whose remains can be rendered unidentifiable and prepared as the replacement “Goldberg” body;
- validating the handoff chain and feeding the resulting intelligence package to Bisutrí so he can design the physical operation around reliable timing rather than improvisation.

La Avispa's contribution should be decisive but bounded. She does not command the assault, control every compromised official, or know every part of El Tiburón's arrangement. Bisutrí retains responsibility for the physical operation, paid-off staff, assault teams, vehicle staging, and extraction execution.

6. PREFERRED PLAYABLE TREATMENT

The HOLE PUNCH strand should be treated through several compact Wasp-campaign sequences rather than by turning La Avispa into an additional gun on the prison-break team.

A. Senior Los Sietes access
La Avispa is brought into direct contact with El Tiburón and Bisutrí. The scene establishes hierarchy, mutual assessment, and the fact that her access is earned but conditional. El Tiburón frames the assignment as a test of precision and discretion, not loyalty theater.

B. Prison-pattern reconstruction
The player gathers and cross-checks incomplete human-source information, institutional records, overheard routines, and surveillance fragments. The gameplay identity should be intelligence synthesis under uncertainty, with La Avispa distinguishing reliable patterns from deliberate noise.

C. Contact handling
La Avispa uses Mexican prison-gang contacts and intermediaries tied to Coleman to learn Goldberg's real schedule and identify which staff relationships can be trusted long enough to support one operation. These contacts should have their own interests and risks; they are not effortless information dispensers.

D. Replacement-body search
La Avispa locates a suitable decedent through criminal, medical, mortuary, or trafficking-adjacent channels. The sequence should be morally cold and uncomfortable rather than sensational. Its narrative purpose is to show the human cost hidden beneath Goldberg's administrative disappearance.

E. Handoff to Bisutrí
La Avispa delivers a concise operational intelligence package. Bisutrí tests the work, asks targeted questions, and accepts the plan only when satisfied that its timing and contingencies are sound. Their exchange should establish professional respect without friendship.

F. Consequence
La Avispa later sees the public report of Goldberg's death and recognizes which parts of her work have been used. The operation's success should not play as triumph. She helped erase a man from accountability and understands that Los Sietes have just returned a uniquely dangerous actor to the shadows.

For La Avispa:
- demonstrates that her highest-value skill is finding people and understanding systems, not merely close-quarters violence;
- forces her to apply old-state surveillance training in service of a criminal network;
- deepens her debt and working relationship with El Tiburón;

For Bisutrí:

- gives him a reason to respect La Avispa's competence before any later direct confrontation or alliance-of-necessity;

For El Tiburón:
- gives the player direct exposure to his authority and strategic reach;
- demonstrates that his power lies in assembling capabilities across criminal, institutional, and intelligence systems;

For Goldberg:

- shows how his survival depends on criminal specialists, compromised institutions, and people whose individual contributions remain compartmented;
- strengthens the GODKILLER premise that Spook is hunting a man who has converted official death into operational freedom.
