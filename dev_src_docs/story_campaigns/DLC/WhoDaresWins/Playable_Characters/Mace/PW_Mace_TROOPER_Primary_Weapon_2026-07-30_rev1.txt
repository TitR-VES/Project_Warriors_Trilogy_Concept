3. PRESENTATION / HANDLING IDENTITY
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
TROOPER should read as a compact full-power rifle chosen by an experienced SAS
operator who values decisive barrier performance and reach in a weapon short enough
for vehicles, maritime spaces, industrial interiors and urban assault routes.

The rifle must not handle like a 5.56mm carbine. Its 7.62x51mm identity should be
felt through stronger recoil, greater muzzle disturbance and more demanding automatic
fire control. Short controlled bursts are credible; undisciplined sustained fire
should rapidly degrade precision and control.

The 1-4x LPVO supports the campaign's movement between confined close work and
longer North Atlantic / coastal sightlines. The white light's 3 o'clock forward
mount is a fixed visual and functional part of the build.

The suppressor is an available configuration, not a universal no-cost upgrade.
Suppressed use should reduce signature while adding forward mass and preserving the
fact that a short 7.62x51mm rifle remains a forceful, mechanically loud weapon.
