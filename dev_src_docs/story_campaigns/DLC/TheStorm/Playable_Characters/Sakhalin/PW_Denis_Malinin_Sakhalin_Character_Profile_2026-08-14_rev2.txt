CORE CONCEPT
A Russian Far East littoral field operator whose background combines genuine
maritime reconnaissance/combat-diver competence with years inside the region's
failing technical and logistical skeleton. He can move people along freezing
shorelines, revive a dead relay for one more transmission window, identify
falsified weather systems, and fight when the route collapses.

BACKGROUND SHAPE
Denis is formed by distance, severe weather, maritime dependence, neglected
infrastructure, and the habit of solving practical problems without expecting
institutional rescue.

His former service gives him coastal insertion/extraction, combat-diver and
small-team reconnaissance discipline, cold-water movement, observation,
navigation, and demolition familiarity. SHADOW OF WAR establishes that by the
night of September 6, 2028 he holds the rank of Senior Sergeant and serves as a
highly competent Russian combat-diver / maritime-special-operations professional
inside a seven-man military-intelligence sabotage team tasked under GRU/GU
command. The 8th Directorate is the intended mission-tasking lane; his exact
permanent parent-unit designation remains open pending final Russian
organizational/nomenclature review.

His later work in remote facilities, salvage, weather/navigation support,
generators, batteries, relays, and shipping support gives him the systems
literacy that distinguishes him from a generic operator. Do not backfill all of
his later infrastructure competence into his military career.

BREAK WITH THE REGIME — REVISED CONTROL
Denis does NOT break with the Russian system during SHADOW OF WAR.

The break is cumulative and substantially post-WWIII. Denis has spent many years
serving the system and was very good at serving it. The fracture comes when the
promised prosperity, national renewal, security, and rewards of victory fail to
materialize after the war, while ordinary Russians instead live with deprivation,
coercion, and increasingly naked totalitarian repression by the same power he
served.

His eventual conclusion is therefore not a sudden battlefield conversion and not
a secret lifelong dissident reveal. It is the long-term recognition that helping
the system function means choosing what that system does to his own people.

The exact incident or final personal threshold that turns cumulative fracture into
active break/Procella alignment remains open. Later writing should preserve the
shape above even if a specific incident is selected.

SHADOW OF WAR CHARACTER RULE
At Kings Bay Denis is professionally committed, restrained, and focused. He does
not deliver anti-regime commentary, sympathize theatrically with the Americans,
or foreshadow his eventual break through obvious moral speeches. Showing that he
was once an effective servant of the system is necessary to give his later break
weight.

PROCELLA FUNCTION
- validate coastal routes
- prepare pickup and landing sites
- escort defectors/refugees from inland handoff to shoreline extraction
- restore temporary power and communications
- recover batteries, fuel, and parts
- corrupt or spoof weather/navigation data
- sabotage logistics/surveillance precisely
- decide when an extraction is still viable and when it has become a kill box

PERSONALITY
- quiet, concrete, restrained, observant
- difficult to impress
- protective through competence
- not emotionally blank; contained
- intolerant of preventable waste
- not an ideological speech engine, glamorous assassin, or generic edgelord

Voice rule:
A Denis line should identify what failed, identify what still works, cut away
illusion, or state the next move.

Representative register:
- “That station was killed by a liar, not by weather.”
- “Route's still usable. Window isn't.”
- “Take the battery. Leave the casing.”
- “No heroics. Boats sink the same either way.”
- “We don't need a miracle. We need ten quiet minutes.”

SHADOW OF WAR register:
After the mission-kill charges are initiated, Denis does not celebrate. His
English intent line is:
“We've done our work. Time is short. Let's move.”

Working Russian line, pending native-language review:
“Мы своё сделали. Времени мало. Уходим.”

LANGUAGE RULE
SHADOW OF WAR team dialogue is canonically performed in Russian with subtitles
for localized releases. The Russian performance must be written/finalized with
native-language and, where practical, military-language consultation so Russian
operators receive the same professional depth and internal cultural weight as
American and allied elite military characters elsewhere in the series.
