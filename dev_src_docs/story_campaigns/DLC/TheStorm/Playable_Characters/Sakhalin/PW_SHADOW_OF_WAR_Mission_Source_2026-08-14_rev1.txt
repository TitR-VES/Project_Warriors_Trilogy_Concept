02. CHARACTER / HISTORICAL FUNCTION
================================================================================
SHADOW OF WAR is NOT Denis's break with the Russian system.

At this point Denis has spent years serving that system, is highly competent at
his work, and still understands the mission through professional duty. The story
must not retroactively imply that he was secretly a dissident or already waiting
to defect.

His later fracture is cumulative and post-WWIII: the promised prosperity,
national renewal, and rewards of victory fail to materialize while deprivation,
state coercion, and totalitarian repression of Russian citizens become the lived
result of the system he served. His eventual break therefore comes from the
long-term inability to reconcile prior loyalty with what that system actually
produces and does to its own people.

SHADOW OF WAR should make that later fracture stronger by first showing that
Denis really was good at serving the machine before he decided he could no longer
help keep it running.

Working service/tasking lock for this mission:
- historical rank: Senior Sergeant;
- role: Russian combat diver / maritime reconnaissance / amphibious sabotage
  specialist;
- seven-man specialist team;
- mission-specific tasking under GRU/GU military-intelligence command, with the
  8th Directorate as the intended tasking lane;
- exact permanent parent-unit designation remains open pending final Russian
  organizational/nomenclature review.

04. THREE-HOUR WINDOW / PANDORA FORESHADOWING
================================================================================
Command gives the team no more than three hours to complete the work and get out.
From the 2243 start this creates an operational clear-by time of 0143 EDT on
September 7, 2028.

This deadline deliberately leaves a buffer before the 0230 EDT Palisade/PANDORA
final enabling-update event controlled by the companion timing clarification.
The team is not told why the deadline exists.

Opening-dialogue beat, intent lock:

OPERATOR: What happens in three hours?
TEAM LEAD: They didn't say.

Russian-language working draft, subject to native military-language review:

that knowledge from downstream operators. This does NOT mean ordinary Russian
servicemen, the whole GRU, or the wider military know the full PANDORA architecture.

================================================================================
Canonical team dialogue is Russian throughout SHADOW OF WAR.

There is no default English-language dub replacing the Russian performances.
Localized releases use subtitles/translated text while preserving the Russian
voice performance unless a later accessibility/localization decision explicitly
creates an optional alternate track.

Dialogue must be authored or finalized in Russian with native-Russian and,
where practical, military-language consultation. Do not write American tactical

Performance goals:

- utilitarian;
- professional;
- Russian cadence, terminology, shorthand, acknowledgments, and omissions;

06. CORE OBJECTIVE / STRATEGIC PURPOSE
================================================================================
The team's purpose is a covert mission kill against USS Wyoming and selected
support capabilities before the wider Russian amphibious operation approaches
the Georgia coast.

The objective is NOT to produce spectacular catastrophic destruction of a
nuclear submarine at berth. The desired strategic outcome is denial: cripple the
boat sufficiently that it cannot sortie and contribute to the U.S. response when
the invasion begins.

Secondary sabotage may affect selected support, communications, or logistics
needed to reinforce that denial. Production design must keep these targets at a
fictionalized/high-level narrative layer rather than reproduce real exploitable
security vulnerabilities, exact charge locations, or real-world sabotage
instructions for the operating installation.

================================================================================
07. STEALTH / DETECTION RULE
================================================================================
Avoiding detection is non-negotiable for most of the mission.

Before the charges are initiated, a successful base-wide alarm is effectively a
mission-failure state. Momentary observation may create a short recovery window
if the observer has not successfully raised the alarm, but the team cannot turn
the mission into an open firefight and still preserve the intended operation.

Stealth kills are permitted when tactically necessary, but must not be glamorized:
- no celebratory score/reward language;
- no execution-showcase framing;
- no exaggerated kill animations;
- no triumphant team chatter;
- ideal play allows very few people to know the team was physically present.

American personnel are ordinary sailors, security staff, technicians,
maintenance workers, and night-shift personnel doing their jobs. They are not
visually or narratively dehumanized because the player is on the opposing side.

08. AMERICAN-BASE PERSPECTIVE INVERSION
================================================================================
Kings Bay must use the same ordinary U.S./allied installation visual language
seen elsewhere in Project Warriors.

Do not visually code it as a sinister "enemy base." Use the same functional
asset grammar where appropriate:
- utilitarian buildings and corridors;
- normal security and safety signage;
- road/parking/service markings;
- maintenance spaces and utility equipment;
- government/institutional interiors;
- parked service vehicles and carts;
- break-room/office details;
- ordinary U.S. flags/unit markings only where they would naturally appear.

The inversion comes entirely from perspective. Architecture and personnel that
would normally read as friendly or mundane are now the environment Malinin must
hide inside and move through.

Desired player feeling:
"I know what this kind of place normally means in this series, but this time I am
the intruder. I absolutely should not be here."

No artificial villain lighting, red enemy-base color language, or excessive
patriotic staging purely to announce that the mission takes place in America.

09. DETONATION / CONSEQUENCE BEAT
================================================================================
After the objective charges are set, the team withdraws from the immediate target
area before initiation.

Sequence lock:
CHARGES COMPLETE -> WITHDRAWAL -> COVERED OBSERVATION POSITION -> READINESS
CONFIRMATION OVER SECURE COMMS -> DETONATION -> IMMEDIATE EXFILTRATION.

The team reaches a protected position such as a berm, low wall, or comparable
fictionalized cover point with the main submarine-support area visible at some
distance. One operator has the firing device ready. The team lead calls the
charges ready over secure comms. The charges are then initiated.

Presentation:
- separated distant explosions rather than one giant spectacle;
- delayed sound/pressure impression appropriate to distance;
- lights changing or failing;
- emergency lighting, alarms, searchlights, vehicles, and confused activity;
- smoke obscuring parts of the facility;
- no casualty montage;
- no graphic destruction foreground;
- no heroic music cue celebrating the attack;
- no Russian cheering or triumphant one-liners;
- no lingering camera that asks the player to admire the damage.

Malinin's restrained exit line, English intent lock:
"We've done our work. Time is short. Let's move."

Working Russian performance line, subject to native-language review:
"Мы своё сделали. Времени мало. Уходим."

The emotional register is consequence and secrecy, followed immediately by the
problem of escape.

================================================================================

AFTER DETONATION, YOU ARE A FUGITIVE.
