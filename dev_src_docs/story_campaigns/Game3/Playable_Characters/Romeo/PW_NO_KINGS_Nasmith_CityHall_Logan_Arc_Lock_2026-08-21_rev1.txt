01. PREWAR ROLE — INHERITED CANON
Douglas Paul Nasmith is the U.S. cover identity of Grigoryi Pavlovich Lebedev, a long-duration Russian illegal embedded in the Boston area for roughly thirty-three years by 2028.

His City Hall position gives him access to the administrative intersection between budgets, municipal contracts, maintenance, service access, infrastructure projects, structural documentation, route planning, access control, and city departments that normally hold separate pieces of the urban picture.

Before PANDORA he is tasked to collect and deliver precisely the kind of information required to facilitate rapid Russian seizure and occupation of Boston:
- service-access route maps;
- structural vulnerabilities;
- municipal / contractor access information;
- strategically useful chokepoints;
- infrastructure documentation;
- route and ingress information;
- related current administrative knowledge.

This is patient administrative intelligence preparation, not field reconnaissance.

02. EYES OF THE CITY — FIRST PLAYER-FACING SUSPICION
After the MIT / Senseable City Lab system is restored in Mission 07, researchers and AFC personnel begin correlating surviving sensor data, local route state, municipal mapping, HUMINT, and occupation movement.

They discover that Russian forces have repeatedly exploited obscure routes and access seams that ordinary maps alone do not adequately explain.

CRITICAL DEAD-GIVEAWAY — LOCKED
The strongest clue is NOT merely that the Russians knew hidden routes existed.

The occupation force consistently knew which hidden routes were CURRENTLY USABLE.

They avoid recently closed, obstructed, decommissioned, damaged, construction-blocked, or otherwise unusable ingress routes and repeatedly choose viable obscure alternatives without an observable trial-and-error phase.

There is no convincing pattern of:
- units running into stale access points;
- reconnaissance teams repeatedly testing dead ends;
- route failures followed by later correction;
- broad exploratory use of every mapped municipal access.

The absence of this margin of error is the key analytic anomaly.

A static city map could tell an invader where a service route was supposed to be.
It would not reliably tell them which obscure routes remained usable after recent closures, construction changes, maintenance restrictions, gate changes, and other city-specific updates.

This supports the first serious AFC / researcher hypothesis that the invasion force received current curated information from somebody with ongoing access inside Boston's administrative system before the attack.

03. INITIAL HYPOTHESIS — DO NOT JUMP STRAIGHT TO NASMITH
The early conclusion is deliberately institutional, not personal:

The AFC must earn the move from that hypothesis to a named suspect.

Do not have the MIT team instantly identify Nasmith as an FSB illegal.
Do not turn one suspicious route into proof.
Do not treat every official who remained in occupied Boston as a collaborator.

The investigation should accumulate through correlation.

04. CONTRACTING / DOCUMENT-CHAIN REVEAL — LOCKED
Later analysis of municipal contracting and infrastructure records reveals that projects documenting, reviewing, updating, or validating many of the hidden routes repeatedly passed through Douglas P. Nasmith's official administrative chain.

The key recurring pattern is that Nasmith, in his City Hall capacity, reviewed and/or approved the contracting work that documented these access routes and their current operational state.

This does not mean every individual contract is criminal on its face.
Most should look bureaucratically normal.

The incriminating weight comes from aggregation:
- multiple relevant projects;
- multiple departments / contractors;
- multiple strategically useful access categories;
- repeated Nasmith review / approval presence;
- route-state knowledge later mirrored by Russian operational behavior.

That pattern turns Nasmith from a name in City Hall paperwork into a legitimate person of interest.

05. NASMITH'S PUBLIC / OCCUPATION-FACING POSITION
Nasmith should initially remain plausible as a senior Boston official operating under occupation conditions.

He is not introduced as a moustache-twirling collaborator.
His normality is part of the threat.

Plausible innocent explanations should exist for much of the paperwork:
- finance / contracting oversight;
- emergency planning;
- interdepartmental approval;
- budget authority;
- infrastructure-program review.

The AFC therefore needs evidence rather than suspicion alone.

This is thematically important to NO KINGS: the resistance cannot credibly claim to be restoring legitimate civic rule if it treats accusation as conviction.

07. LOGAN INTERNATIONAL AIRPORT OBSERVATION SCENE — OWNER-RECONFIRMED CORE LOCK
Later in the NO KINGS campaign, Romeo and an AFC reconnaissance element covertly go to Boston Logan International Airport to observe Nasmith.

The operation is surveillance / confirmation, not an assassination or assault mission.

Nasmith is observed meeting senior arriving Russian intelligence / Kremlin-connected officials.

The officials arrive aboard a Russian Tupolev Tu-154 aircraft.

The scene's core dramatic function is to push the AFC's assessment beyond the hypothesis that Nasmith is merely a coerced collaborator, corrupt municipal official, or opportunistic intermediary.

A direct, privileged meeting with senior Russian intelligence figures arriving from the Kremlin / central Russian power structure establishes that Nasmith is connected to the architecture behind the occupation at a much higher level.
