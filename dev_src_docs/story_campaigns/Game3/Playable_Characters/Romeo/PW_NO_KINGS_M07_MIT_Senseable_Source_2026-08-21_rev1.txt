PRIMARY STRATEGIC OBJECTIVE
Secure reliable contact with surviving personnel at the MIT Senseable City Lab and create enough local security for them to restore a preserved prewar urban sensing / monitoring research system.

PREWAR RESEARCH SYSTEM — LOCKED FICTIONAL EXTENSION
The Senseable team had been developing an autonomous AI-assisted urban environmental data collection, monitoring, and analysis architecture using a citywide research mesh of IoT cameras, environmental probes, monitoring nodes, and related sensor devices.

The system is a fictional Project Warriors extension of the lab's urban-sensing research direction. It is not a hidden military intelligence network and should not be written as a turnkey classified SIGINT system.

PANDORA SURVIVAL MECHANISM — LOCKED TECHNICAL GUARDRAIL
At the time of the PANDORA attacks, the research system was undergoing an AI-model / orchestration-stack upgrade in an isolated validation environment.

When researchers realized trusted OTA/update infrastructure elsewhere was being weaponized, they carried out a risky emergency isolation:
- sever external administrative/update connectivity;
- halt outgoing automated configuration changes;
- shut down central inference/orchestration compute;
- preserve signing credentials, topology/device registries, and critical control material offline;
- leave compatible field endpoints in local/autonomous/buffered states where possible.

The result was not a perfectly preserved live network. The central control plane survived dormant while many endpoints degraded independently.

CURRENT SYSTEM CONDITION
- preserved but damaged and fragmented;
- many sensors destroyed, removed, powerless, disconnected, or isolated behind damaged infrastructure;
- some endpoint connectivity and historical local-buffer data remain recoverable;
- surviving imaging, traffic, environmental, RF-adjacent, and monitoring nodes provide uneven coverage rather than omniscient surveillance;
- restoration requires people, infrastructure, power, network access, and analysis, not simply pressing a switch.

RUSSIAN KNOWLEDGE / MISCONCEPTION — LOCKED
Russian occupation technical personnel know the Senseable City Lab and its research existed.

Their post-PANDORA assessment is wrong: they assume the relevant system was successfully compromised, destroyed, or rendered unusable during the attacks.

Because the surviving core was deliberately shut down and stopped advertising a meaningful operational surface, Russian EW specialists do not recognize that the citywide research architecture remains preserved but dormant.

The Russians are therefore not ignorant of Senseable. They are unaware that Senseable survived.

MIT OCCUPATION STATE
- students have been expelled / vacated from the campus;
- selected staff, officials, facilities personnel, researchers, and technical personnel remain under occupation restriction or scrutiny;
- Russian military / technical-exploitation personnel are interrogating MIT officials and systematically searching campus spaces for militarily valuable technology, research, prototypes, computing, communications, robotics, autonomy, cyber, materials, aerospace, and other exploitable knowledge;
- the campus should read as an occupation exploitation zone rather than a simple fortress;
- the Senseable personnel conceal both the preserved system and the fact that useful citywide access may still exist.

MISSION HINGE
The initial AFC task is to reach the Senseable personnel and assess/extract the value of what survived.

The technical team establishes that merely extracting people or portable hardware is insufficient. The useful system depends on a protected combination of campus infrastructure, power, networking, compute, physical access, and specialist personnel.

Therefore the operation escalates from contact/recovery into securing enough of MIT and adjacent Cambridge territory to keep the system alive.

END-STATE — RESISTANCE CAPABILITY
Before Mission 07, AFC Boston intelligence is dominated by HUMINT, couriers, physical reconnaissance, rooftop/street observation, captured documents, and local reporting.

After Mission 07, those sources can be fused with a persistent but incomplete technical picture from surviving urban sensors.

Valid campaign outputs include:
- probable patrol routes;
- vehicle and convoy movement alerts;
- checkpoint staffing / activity trends;
- unusual concentrations of occupation forces;
- traffic and movement changes;
- areas going unexpectedly quiet or dark;
- partial imaging or environmental indications;
- route viability / obstruction changes;
- later correlation with intercepted communications, captured records, and HUMINT.

GAMEPLAY / STORY LIMIT

Coverage is partial, delayed, degradable, spoofable, and dependent on analysts interpreting noisy data.

FIRST MAJOR INTELLIGENCE PAYOFFS — LOCKED
A) EASTERN EXPANSION LANE
The restored picture exposes a strategically useful corridor east / northeast from the newly linked THE POCKET–MIT resistance network toward the adjacent occupation sector containing Van Hult Bodyworks and associated skilled-labor / fabrication potential.

This intelligence is intended to motivate the next phase of Act II expansion.

B) ADMINISTRATIVE / INSIDE-HELP ANOMALY
The researchers identify a pattern in how Russian forces have exploited obscure municipal ingress routes, service roads, utility approaches, restricted access points, and other non-obvious urban seams.

The decisive clue is not merely that Russian maps are detailed.

Russian units repeatedly use hidden routes that are still viable while avoiding routes that were recently closed, obstructed, decommissioned, under repair, or otherwise unusable.

There is no visible trial-and-error pattern: no repeated dead ends, no systematic testing of stale accesses, and no corresponding sequence of reconnaissance mistakes.

This absence of margin for error is the first strong evidence that the occupation force was working from current, curated local information rather than ordinary military mapping alone.
