01. FINALE LOCATION — LOCKED
The decisive military finale of NO KINGS takes place at Boston Logan International Airport.

By this point in the campaign:
- major Boston harbor control has already shifted away from the occupation;
- the occupation's maritime sustainment system has been degraded or broken;
- resistance-controlled neighborhoods form a connected urban network;
- British support has widened from covert SAS/SBS operations into visible Royal Marine support under Vickers;
- the Battle of Boston is in its final phase;
- organized Russian occupation forces are contracting toward remaining defensible strategic infrastructure, with Logan one of the last load-bearing positions.

The liberation of Logan is the final decisive event that breaks organized occupation control of Boston.

The airport's capture is strategically meaningful because it:
- removes a final major occupation transportation / logistics node;
- prevents an orderly enemy air evacuation / reinforcement corridor from remaining under Russian control;
- gives the newly liberated city a future high-capacity air logistics / CASEVAC / reinforcement gateway;
- symbolizes Boston transitioning from isolated resistance territory into a city capable of reconnecting to the wider Allied war effort.

02. FRIENDLY FORCE COMPOSITION — LOCKED STRUCTURAL ROLE
The Logan assault is a joint operation.

Primary friendly layers:
- Boston AFC / local resistance elements, with Romeo as the player-facing assault lead;
- surviving / available U.S. military elements operating in and around Boston;
- British Royal Marines led by Lt. Dorothy Anne Vickers;
- other British support elements may appear where already justified by the campaign, but the finale should not erase the local Boston / AFC ownership of the fight.

Narrative rule:
The British are indispensable coalition partners, not the force that arrives to 'save' Boston after locals have done the work.

03. NASMITH / LEBEDEV FINALE ROLE — LOCKED
Douglas Paul Nasmith / Grigoryi Pavlovich Lebedev survives the earlier liberation of City Hall / central Boston and evades capture.

As occupation control collapses, he retreats toward Logan seeking passage out of Boston and ultimately out of the United States.

Romeo's team enters the Logan battle already aware that Lebedev is likely somewhere inside the airport complex.

His capture is a high-priority secondary strategic objective because he is:
- a deeply embedded FSB illegal;
- a direct source of pre-invasion municipal intelligence;
- a living link to the Russian clandestine preparation architecture;
- potentially valuable for intelligence on the wider illegal / sleeper network, handlers, tasking, and occupation preparation.

The player therefore has a personal and strategic reason to keep him alive.

04. FINALE GAMEPLAY / PURSUIT IDENTITY — LOCKED CORE
The airport battle should contain two interlocking tracks:

A. LARGE-SCALE LIBERATION BATTLE
Romeo and allied forces fight through airport approaches, terminals, service infrastructure, and airside positions as the occupation collapses.

B. LEBEDEV PURSUIT
During or after the main occupation defense begins breaking, Romeo learns that Lebedev is moving toward an aircraft / evacuation point.

The pursuit escalates through:
- terminal / concourse spaces;
- secure or staff-only corridors;
- baggage / logistics / service areas where appropriate;
- airside service routes;
- finally the tarmac / apron environment.

The dramatic purpose is not a stylized foot-race divorced from the battle.
Lebedev is using the collapsing airport geometry, remaining occupation personnel, service access, and evacuation chaos to reach an outbound aircraft while Romeo's team closes on him.

The chase should feel like the final compression of the whole Nasmith investigation into one physical objective:
for most of the campaign Romeo has been trying to understand who this man is; now he is trying to stop him from disappearing.

06. THE FEDERAL ORDER — LOCKED NARRATIVE FUNCTION
Immediately after Lebedev is secured, higher command communicates an order affecting his custody.

Russia has demanded that Nasmith/Lebedev be returned / remanded to Russian control under an asserted official or diplomatic-protection claim connected to the negotiated end-state / withdrawal arrangements.

The restored U.S. federal administration chooses to recognize or operationally honor that demand and instructs forces in Boston to transfer Lebedev for repatriation rather than retain him as an intelligence detainee.

REALISM / LEGAL GUARDRAIL
Do NOT treat Lebedev's deep-cover identity as automatically giving him ordinary accredited diplomatic immunity.

He is a long-duration illegal operating under a U.S. cover identity, not an openly accredited embassy diplomat in the established source.

Therefore the exact federal mechanism should remain open for a later legal / diplomatic pass. Plausible forms include:
- recognition of a Russian asserted official status as part of a negotiated withdrawal / exchange arrangement;
- executive agreement governing repatriation of designated officials;
- a politically negotiated protected-transfer list;
- a deliberately contestable federal legal interpretation.

The important dramatic truth is that Washington CHOOSES to order his transfer despite his intelligence value and despite the evidence against him.

07. WHY THE ORDER HITS ROMEO SO HARD — LOCKED CONTEXT
Romeo's anger is not simply anti-government defiance.

His perspective is shaped by the preceding war:
- PANDORA and occupation severely degraded central command and federal capacity;
- prewar Department of War corruption, bad procurement, weakened institutions, and poor leadership worsened the conditions faced by scattered U.S. forces;
- many surviving U.S. formations were forced to operate with fragmented support and local/theater initiative;
- Romeo's movement toward Boston was primarily enabled through surviving theater / CENTCOM-linked leadership and coalition relationships rather than meaningful day-to-day federal political support;
- in Game 2, President Barry Crodyn and War Secretary Hogworth publicly designated the AFC as a domestic terrorist organization;
- the Boston resistance, AFC, local forces, and British partners carried the practical burden of the city campaign.

The federal administration's reappearance at the moment of victory therefore has emotional and political weight.

08. IMPORTANT THEMATIC GUARDRAIL — CIVILIAN COMMAND ITSELF IS NOT THE VILLAIN
NO KINGS must not imply that federal civilian authority over U.S. armed forces is inherently illegitimate.

The problem is the timing, credibility, history, and content of the specific orders.

Some federal reassertion after communications and territorial restoration is normal and necessary.
The dramatic conflict comes from the impression that a government which did little to produce the local victory immediately prioritizes central control over accountability, local trust, and the institutions that actually kept the city alive.

09. POST-BOSTON FEDERAL ORDERS — LOCKED STRUCTURAL PACKAGE
After Logan falls and Boston is effectively liberated, federal authority issues a wider transition package.

A. LEBEDEV TRANSFER
- transfer / repatriate Lebedev under the federal government's accepted Russian demand / negotiated arrangement.

B. AFC DEMOBILIZATION / DISARMAMENT
- all non-military armed AFC personnel in Boston are ordered to stand down;
- their weapons and ammunition are to be surrendered to recognized military authority.

C. U.S. FORCE SPLIT
- one large U.S. element remains in Boston to hold the city, supervise the final enemy withdrawal / surrender process, maintain security, and support transition logistics;
- another large U.S. element is ordered south toward Washington, D.C. to support restoration / logistics in and around the federal capital after the maritime blockade has been effectively broken.

D. BRITISH MOVEMENT / LIMIT
- part of the British force remains in Boston to support logistics and city stabilization;
- another British element accompanies / supports the southbound movement to a defined handoff / limit north of Washington, D.C.;
- British forces are ordered not to enter the capital itself and to return toward Boston after the handoff.

Exact road / rail / maritime route, staging points, and geographic handoff line remain OPEN pending wider East Coast theater mapping.

10. AFC DISARMAMENT — THEMATIC INTERPRETATION
The disarmament order should remain morally and politically ambiguous rather than automatically framed as evil.

A post-liberation government has legitimate reasons to seek demobilization of irregular armed formations.

What makes the order alarming to Romeo and Boston allies is:
- its immediacy;
- its blanket character;
- the absence of a clearly trusted local civilian transition process;
- the federal government's prior branding of the AFC as terrorists;
- the fact that the local resistance and AFC have just proven indispensable to the city's survival;
- the simultaneous transfer of other sensitive powers upward / outward.

This supports NO KINGS' central question rather than answering it cheaply.

12. DO NOT FLATTEN THE FEDERAL RESTORATION INTO A CARTOON COUP
The post-Boston orders should contain genuinely reasonable state functions alongside disturbing choices.

Reasonable / necessary elements can include:
- restoring logistics to Washington;
- establishing secure military responsibility for a newly liberated major city;
- coordinating final occupation withdrawal / surrender;
- restarting humanitarian aid pipelines;
- demobilizing irregular forces over time;
- restoring aviation / port / municipal services.

The disturbing elements are the choices about HOW these goals are pursued:
- Lebedev's politically convenient transfer;
- blanket / immediate AFC disarmament without earned trust;
- corporate control of Senseable-derived citywide observation infrastructure;
- reassertion of authority without meaningful acknowledgment of the networks that actually sustained Boston.

This mixture is intentional.

13. ACT IV / WHAT VICTORY IS FOR — THEMATIC FUNCTION
The Logan battle answers the military question:
Can Boston be liberated?

The post-battle orders create the real NO KINGS ending question:
Who gets to define liberation after the shooting stops?

Romeo must confront the possibility that:
- defeating an occupier does not automatically restore legitimate trust;
- an irregular resistance can itself become dangerous if it refuses all demobilization;
- a nominally lawful government can still use legality, contracting, surveillance, and command authority to consolidate power in ways that betray the people who carried the war;
- victory is not ownership.

The exact final Romeo choice / statement / action remains OPEN and requires a dedicated ending pass.

14. RECOMMENDED RESOLUTION PRINCIPLE — NOT YET HARD-LOCKED
The strongest eventual ending should avoid either extreme:
- Romeo does not crown himself or the AFC as a permanent parallel government;
- Romeo also does not simply accept every federal order as morally self-validating because it came through a restored chain of command.

The ending should force him to separate:
- legitimate civil restoration;
from
- political convenience, cover-up, coercive centralization, or private capture of public infrastructure.

This is the cleanest expression of the title NO KINGS.
