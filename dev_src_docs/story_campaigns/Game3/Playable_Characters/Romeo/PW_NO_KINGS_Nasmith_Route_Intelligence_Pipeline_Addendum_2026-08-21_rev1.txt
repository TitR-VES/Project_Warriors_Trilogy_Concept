PURPOSE
Clarify the exact mechanism behind the occupation's suspiciously accurate use of hidden Boston ingress and service routes, extending the Nasmith / City Hall evidence architecture without changing the already locked prewar role.

LOCKED MECHANISM
Douglas P. Nasmith / Grigoryi P. Lebedev uses his City Hall access to obtain fresh municipal and contractor information about hidden ingress routes, service-access corridors, maintenance approaches, structural seams, chokepoints, closures, obstructions, and current route usability.

Nasmith does not pass this material directly to ordinary Russian field formations.

The intelligence chain is:
1. Nasmith acquires and updates current municipal route-state information through his legitimate City Hall administrative position and associated contracting / review access.
2. Nasmith passes the fresh intelligence through his clandestine channel to the FSB.
3. FSB intelligence personnel validate, curate, and operationalize the material.
4. Russian invasion / occupation commanders receive only the route information they need for specific movements, including which obscure routes are currently viable.

FIELD-LEVEL EFFECT
Ground commanders do not need to know Nasmith exists, know how the source was acquired, or receive the complete municipal dataset.

They are simply given reliable movement instructions and vetted ingress options.

This compartmentation explains why the operational fingerprint is visible before the human source is:
- Russian forces repeatedly choose viable obscure routes on the first attempt;
- recently closed, blocked, altered, damaged, or otherwise unusable alternatives are avoided;
- there is little or no observable trial-and-error discovery process;
- individual field units can appear unusually well informed without possessing the full intelligence picture themselves.

ANALYTIC CONSEQUENCE AFTER EYES OF THE CITY
The MIT / Senseable team and AFC do not intercept a document saying that Nasmith supplied the routes.

They first identify the statistical / operational anomaly: the occupation behaves as if somebody has already removed stale and unusable routes from its choices.

That supports the hypothesis that an intelligence service is feeding commanders curated, current local knowledge rather than commanders merely consulting detailed city maps.

Only later does the contracting / administrative evidence chain point toward Nasmith as the common City Hall intersection behind many of the relevant route-status records.

NARRATIVE RULE
Preserve the separation between:
- source: Nasmith / Lebedev;
- intelligence processor and distributor: FSB;
- operational consumer: Russian invasion / occupation commanders.

Do not portray ordinary Russian commanders as personally receiving raw City Hall files from Nasmith.
Do not make the FSB pipeline so transparent that the AFC can identify Nasmith immediately from the first anomaly.
