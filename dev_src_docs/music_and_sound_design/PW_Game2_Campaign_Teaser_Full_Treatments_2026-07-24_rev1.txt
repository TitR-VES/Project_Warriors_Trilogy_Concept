PURPOSE
Preserve the complete treatment-level architecture for the three principal Game 2 campaign teasers. The compact promotional standard controls shared editorial rules; this page controls the detailed campaign-specific sequence.

SHARED FORMAT
- target length: approximately ninety seconds;
- aspect: widescreen cinematic presentation, approximately 2.39:1;
- no gameplay HUD;
- no fake menu/UI framing;
- campaign teasers are mood/narrative pieces, not feature lists;
- Romeo and Ducky teasers contain no spoken dialogue;
- GODKILLER receives a deliberate two-line dialogue exception;
- title cards are plain, restrained, and separated by full beats of silence;
- commercial music is licensing-dependent and may require replacement tracks preserving the same dramatic architecture;
- “Coming Q2 2028” is fictional in-world flavor from the legacy treatment, not a real development or release commitment.

01. AMERICAN FREEDOM — WAR HITS HOME
===============================================================================

Music candidate
Jesse Welles — Good Morning America.
Final passage may transform the melody into an unaccompanied or near-unaccompanied mixed choir treatment. The reference is communal/congregational weight, not a direct imitation of another soundtrack arrangement.

Tone
Restrained, elegiac, intimate, and gradually defiant. The campaign is introduced through grief and occupied normality before action.

ACT ONE — RECOGNITION / ORDINARY AMERICA MADE WRONG
Approx. 0:00–0:25.

- cold early-morning light over Calloway/Main Street;
- familiar storefronts, pharmacy, civic buildings, porches, road markings;
- foreign checkpoint and occupation vehicles embedded in those familiar spaces;
- civilians carrying bags, waiting, searching damaged homes, avoiding eye contact;
- Russian flags or occupation signage present without a giant villain tableau;
- no overt resistance hero shot yet;
- the camera moves like a documentary observer rather than a commercial action reel.

ACT TWO — WEIGHT
Approx. 0:25–0:50.

- occupation searches and roadside stops;
- a shuttered school or church/community space;
- Dale’s pharmacy/civic network implied through medicine boxes, notes, deliveries, or a quiet back room;
- Cody moving through a workshop/vehicle space, impatient energy visible without dialogue;
- Romeo watching patrol movement from concealment;
- the campaign’s human cost is shown through objects and routines rather than graphic violence.

PROTECTED THREE-FRAME INSERT
During the accelerating quick-cut build, insert approximately three frames of the Cody consequence photograph/loading-screen image. It must be too brief to explain itself on first viewing but readable on pause/rewatch. The insert functions as dread and future recognition, not shock advertising. Final image/rating approval remains required.

ACT THREE — THE CURRENT / RESISTANCE FORMING
Approx. 0:50–1:10.

- boots moving through mud and grass;
- a folded paper/map exchanged without eye contact;
- yellow/red local resistance marks only if compatible with the later AFC visual source;
- radios, medicine, crates, culvert/farm-road movement, improvised preparation;
- Romeo and locals moving with shared purpose but not yet a polished army;
- cuts accelerate with the song but avoid a conventional kill montage.

ACT FOUR — RECKONING
Approx. 1:10–1:28.

- Romeo stands at the edge of a tree line or ridge, back or partial profile to camera, looking down at occupied Calloway;
- the town is unmistakably American; the flags controlling it are not;
- the guitar/original arrangement gives way to the mixed choir carrying the melody;
- the choir reframes Romeo as one person inside something larger than himself;
- hard cut to black while the choir is still emotionally rising rather than after it resolves;
- complete silence for one beat.

TITLE CARDS
WAR HITS HOME.
AMERICAN FREEDOM.
Coming Q2 2028. [fictional flavor only]

Optional cross-promo seed
A two-second or shorter Russian number-station fragment may be buried under the final choir decay as an extremely subtle Glushkin/sleeper-network connection. It must remain below the threshold of explanatory foreground audio.

Avoid
- heroic flag-waving montage;
- generic patriotic advertising;
- clean esports/faction graphics;
- graphic exploitation of Cody’s death;
- dialogue explaining occupation or resistance.

02. PERSISTENT THREAT — THE SYSTEM FELL. WE DIDN’T.
===============================================================================

Title correction
The legacy treatment used AEGIS FALLS as a working campaign title. Current canon:
- campaign: PERSISTENT THREAT;
- AEGIS FALLS: final mission title.
All teaser end cards must use PERSISTENT THREAT unless a specific finale promo is being made.

Music candidate
Apashe and Alina Pash — Kyiv.

Tone
Institutional collapse, European systems war, professional endurance, and increasing visual instability.

VISUAL GRAMMAR
The teaser uses two image systems:
1. composed cinematic footage;
2. raw first-person/drone/body-camera-adjacent footage.

The raw footage does not appear immediately. It intrudes progressively as the campaign’s systems and certainty degrade. Its frequency rises with musical intensity, approaches near-alternation at the peak, then recedes for the final emblem image.

ACT ONE — THE INSTITUTION
Approx. 0:00–0:25.

- entirely cinematic;
- European coastline/industrial infrastructure in cold weather;
- AEGIS operators moving through transport, operations rooms, maritime or urban service spaces;
- pristine AEGIS emblem shown clearly for the first time;
- Halliday/Walker/command architecture may appear as glimpsed operational presence, not expository character introductions;
- no raw POV yet.

ACT TWO — HAIRLINE FRACTURE
Approx. 0:25–0:50.

- first raw-footage intrusion during an operational movement or contact beat;
- cinematic shots of EW hardware, convoy movement, damaged civilian infrastructure, frozen terrain, rail/industrial nodes;
- raw fragments from close movement, drone angle, helmet-level entry, or casualty handling;
- pristine emblem returns with one hairline crack or subtle material fault;
- the effect should be readable but not loudly symbolic.

ACT THREE — THE SYSTEM CANNOT HOLD
Approx. 0:50–1:10.

- faster alternation between cinematic and raw grammars;
- crossing water/ice, close urban movement, CORYS/AEGIS parallel operations, technical-site destruction, hurried exfiltration;
- cracked command displays, severed links, documents photographed before destruction, wounded operator moved under fire;
- no single mission must be copied literally if later campaign placement changes; the treatment expresses campaign identity rather than a locked montage chronology;
- at the musical peak, raw and cinematic shots can alternate cut for cut;
- as the track hollows out, the raw footage recedes.

ACT FOUR — THE EMBLEM
Approx. 1:10–1:28.

- final steady cinematic image: the AEGIS emblem shown for the third time;
- now fractured in a safety-glass/spider-web pattern but still holding its recognizable form;
- motto remains readable;
- the image communicates a system fundamentally compromised but not erased;
- hold against the exposed vocal/quiet end of the music;
- cut to black and full silence.

TITLE CARDS
THE SYSTEM FELL.
WE DIDN’T.
PERSISTENT THREAT.
Coming Q2 2028. [fictional flavor only]

Avoid
- using first-game mission footage as literal Game 2 continuity after current campaign assets exist;
- ending on AEGIS FALLS as campaign title;
- making the emblem explode or disappear entirely;
- raw footage from the first frame;
- undifferentiated “war footage” without technical/institutional identity.

03. GODKILLER — YOU CAN’T KILL A DEAD MAN
===============================================================================

Music candidate
Metallica — King Nothing.

Tone
Investigative pressure, compromised institutions, Goldberg’s false death, and the realization that the conspiracy survived its apparent exposure.

Dialogue exception
This is the only one of the three teasers with spoken dialogue. The exception is intentional because the tagline is a character exchange and Goldberg’s voice is more unsettling than a clear hero/villain reveal.

ACT ONE — OFFICIAL DEATH
Approx. 0:00–0:25.

- Goldberg photograph or case image stamped/marked DECEASED;
- official records, closed case files, memorial/news language, institutional certainty;
- Spook receives or studies material that should end the question;
- the montage feels procedural and final.

ACT TWO — THE RECORD DOES NOT CLOSE
Approx. 0:25–0:50.

- inconsistencies accumulate: mismatched dates, shell-company links, financial routing, surveillance stills, a face half-seen, an old access record still active;
- Tripper/Smoke may appear in investigation texture without becoming trailer narrators;
- Spook follows a chain through offices, vehicles, waterfront/airport/corporate spaces;
- the official story begins to feel manufactured.

ACT THREE — DEAD MAN WALKING
Approx. 0:50–1:15.

- compressed investigation/action build;
- Goldberg’s influence visible through other people, protected spaces, and erased records;
- East McFarlane or related island/waterfront imagery may appear as a visual rhyme rather than a full replay of the Game 1 finale;
- final reveal: Goldberg alive, shown from behind or partial angle on a terrace/waterfront, completely unhurried;
- do not give a clean glamour close-up. His calm certainty is the reveal.

ACT FOUR — THE EXCHANGE
Approx. 1:15–1:28.

Music strips to near silence.

Spook, low and flat:
“You can’t kill a dead man.”

One beat.

Goldberg, older and quietly amused:
“No.”

Cut to black. Music stops. Full beat of silence.

TITLE CARDS
YOU CAN’T KILL A DEAD MAN.
GODKILLER.
Coming Q2 2028. [fictional flavor only]

Avoid
- explaining Goldberg’s escape mechanism;
- showing a full conspiracy board long enough to become exposition;
- turning Goldberg into a jump-scare reveal;
- adding additional dialogue;
- presenting Spook as a conventional action hero rather than an investigator entering a deeper system.
