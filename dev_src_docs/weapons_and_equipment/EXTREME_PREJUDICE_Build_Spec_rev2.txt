1. BUILD IDENTITY
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Build name:   EXTREME PREJUDICE
Platform:     CheyTac M200 Intervention
Caliber:      .408 CheyTac
Weapon class: Long-range precision / anti-materiel-capable sniper rifle

EXTREME PREJUDICE is the named CheyTac Intervention carried by CRIMSON TWO,
Lawrence "Rip" Mansell, during the Riptide Red campaign finale.

The July 22 owner-directed reconciliation preserves the existing CRIMSON TWO
precision encounter slot and moves Mansell into it. It does not convert CRIMSON
THREE's support slot into a sniper role.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
4. DISTINCTNESS FROM OTHER INTERVENTIONS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
EXTREME PREJUDICE is distinct from:
- the CheyTac M200 hidden in the East McFarlane Island security post;
- generic Atlas / War Wolf precision-rifle inventory;
- any separately serialized security or recovery weapon.

The older phrase "CRIMSON TWO's separately serialized personal M200" now refers to
this weapon because Mansell is CRIMSON TWO.

No ammunition count, optic, suppressor, finish, stock treatment, markings or pickup
behavior beyond the inherited encounter baseline is newly locked by this rev2.
Those details require a dedicated finale encounter/build pass and must not be
invented by downstream files.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
5. NAME LOGIC
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
EXTREME PREJUDICE points backward to Mansell's CIA / Oscar Four Delta history rather
than forward to the War Wolves' masks.

The phrase should read as old clandestine-action language: cold, deniable and
bureaucratically violent. It is not wolf branding and should not be paired with
wolf-head engraving, claw marks, occult decoration or theatrical trophy treatment.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
6. INSPECT / NAMING RULE

universal named-build rule.

The physical placement or inscription of the name remains open. Do not assume an
engraving, paint-marker treatment or receiver marking until specified by a later
visual/build source.

7. NARRATIVE / PRESENTATION RULE

Presentation should emphasize disciplined range control, prepared geometry and the
sensation that Mansell has already judged the player an acceptable target.
