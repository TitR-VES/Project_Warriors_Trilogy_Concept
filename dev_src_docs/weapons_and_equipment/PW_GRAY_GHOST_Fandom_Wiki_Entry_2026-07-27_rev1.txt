GRAY GHOST
================================================================================

GRAY GHOST is a named assault carbine and the signature primary weapon of Trevor
“Spook” Gartner in the Project Warriors series. It is based on a select-fire Noveske
Rifleworks N-4 Gen3 chambered in .300 AAC Blackout and combines the accuracy of a
high-quality AR-pattern rifle with the compact dimensions of a close-quarters carbine.

The weapon is distinguished by its 10.5-inch barrel, solid dark-gray finish,
suppressor-ready configuration, and combined red-dot and flip-to-side 3x magnifier.
Although designed primarily for covert operations and close-to-intermediate-range
engagements, it can function as a lightweight marksman’s weapon in the hands of a
skilled player.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
SPECIFICATIONS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Weapon class:      Assault carbine / compact marksman carbine
Platform:          Noveske Rifleworks N-4 Gen3
Caliber:           .300 AAC Blackout
Barrel length:     10.5 inches
Feed system:       30-round detachable STANAG magazine
Fire modes:        Semi-automatic and fully automatic
Primary user:      Trevor “Spook” Gartner
Primary optic:     Aimpoint Micro T-2 1x22 red-dot sight
Magnifier:         Flip-to-side Aimpoint 3x magnifier
Backup sights:     45-degree offset MBUS Pro iron sights
Weapon light:      SureFire Mini Scout Light M300C, 1 o’clock mount
Suppressor:        SureFire SOCOM300-SPS
Standard finish:   Solid dark-gray Cerakote
Default ammunition:.300 BLK subsonic
Alternatives:      .300 BLK supersonic and specialized penetration loads

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
OVERVIEW
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

GRAY GHOST occupies the middle ground between a suppressed close-quarters weapon and
a conventional precision rifle. Its short barrel and relatively low overall weight
make it highly maneuverable inside buildings, vehicles, stairwells, and other confined
environments. Its high-quality barrel and magnifier also allow accurate fire well
beyond the distances normally associated with compact carbines.

The weapon is especially effective from point-blank range to approximately 300 yards.
Within this envelope, it combines high damage, rapid target acquisition, exceptional
accuracy, and little unnecessary bulk. Spook can engage multiple close targets through
the unmagnified red-dot sight and flip the 3x magnifier into place when a more distant
or partially exposed target appears.

At longer ranges, GRAY GHOST remains capable but becomes increasingly dependent on
player skill. Its projectiles follow a noticeably arcing trajectory, particularly when
subsonic ammunition is loaded. Accurate hits beyond 300 yards require the player to
judge distance and compensate for bullet drop rather than placing the reticle directly
over the target.

Around 600 yards, the weapon reaches the practical outer edge of its gameplay role. A
sufficiently skilled player can still engage exposed personnel at this distance with
supersonic ammunition, but GRAY GHOST is not a replacement for a dedicated sniper or
precision rifle. Its low magnification, pronounced trajectory, and declining projectile
velocity make long-range hits difficult and situational.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
DESIGN
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

GRAY GHOST uses a select-fire Noveske N-4 Gen3 receiver set with a 10.5-inch .300
Blackout barrel and an NWS-9 M-LOK handguard. Its compact dimensions make it easy to
shoulder rapidly and prevent the muzzle from becoming obstructive when moving through
doorways or working around vehicle interiors.

The weapon is finished almost entirely in solid dark-gray Cerakote and carries no
decorative pattern or prominently displayed personal markings. During the weapon-
inspection animation, the player can see a faint factory marking reading “300 BLK” on
the dust cover. The marking has been nearly erased by years of handling rather than
intentionally removed.

Its standard attachments include:
- Aimpoint Micro T-2 red-dot sight on a 0.8-inch riser;
- flip-to-side Aimpoint 3x magnifier;
- 45-degree offset MBUS Pro backup sights;
- SureFire Mini Scout Light M300C tactical white light mounted at the 1 o’clock position
  near the front end of the handguard;
- tactical dual pressure switch mounted on the top Picatinny rail approximately two
  inches behind the handguard’s front edge;
- milled aluminum low-profile angled skeleton grip with an integrated barrier stop;
- SureFire SOCOM300-SPS suppressor; and
- BE Meyers MAWL-DA laser aiming module once unlocked.

The M300C and its pressure switch are arranged around Spook’s preferred support-hand
position. The forward 1 o’clock light mount keeps the white-light body clear of his
sight picture and minimizes interference with cover. The dual pressure switch remains
reachable without breaking his firing grip, while its placement on the top rail lets
the switch housing serve as a tactile thumb stop when Spook uses a C-clamp grip.

The MAWL-DA becomes available after the events of BURNING DAYLIGHT. Once installed,
it receives a unique inspection animation in which Spook briefly manipulates its
activation dial and pulses the infrared aiming laser.

Earlier development material incorrectly listed a 5.56 mm SureFire SOCOM556-RC2 as
the weapon’s suppressor. The canonical suppressor is the .30-caliber SOCOM300-SPS,
with the correction applying retroactively to every appearance of the weapon.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
GAMEPLAY
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

HANDLING

GRAY GHOST has one of the shortest aim-down-sight times among the game’s full shoulder
weapons when the player uses the red dot alone. Its light receiver group, short barrel,
and compact handguard allow Spook to bring it onto target quickly, including after
sprinting, climbing, or transitioning out of another interaction.

Its ADS performance is state-dependent:

- Red dot only: VERY FAST
- 3x magnifier engaged: FAST

Engaging the magnifier therefore carries a small but deliberate handling penalty. Spook
must establish a more precise eye position behind the magnified optic, and the reduced
field of view makes rapid close-range target acquisition less forgiving. The magnifier
is a situational tool for identification and precision at intermediate distance, not a
catch-all upgrade that should remain deployed in every engagement.

Switching between GRAY GHOST and Spook’s sidearm is similarly fast. This makes the
carbine especially useful in missions where the player alternates between deliberate
suppressed shooting and sudden close-range combat.

Its reload is relatively quick, although reload and weapon-transition animations
become marginally slower as heavier accessories are added. The suppressor, magnifier,
weapon light, and laser module collectively make the final configuration more front-
heavy than the base weapon. The penalty remains minor, and GRAY GHOST stays compact
and responsive even when fully equipped.

ACCURACY

GRAY GHOST is one of the most accurate automatic weapons available in Spook’s
campaigns. Semi-automatic fire produces extremely tight groups, while short automatic
bursts remain controllable at close range.

Its accuracy is most noticeable between 50 and 300 yards. At these distances, the
player can move between rapid close-range aiming through the bare red dot and more
deliberate precision fire through the magnifier without changing weapons.

Automatic fire becomes progressively less useful beyond intermediate range. Although
the rifle remains mechanically accurate, projectile drop and limited 3x magnification
make carefully paced semi-automatic fire more effective.

DAMAGE

The weapon inflicts very high damage at close and moderate range. A small number of
well-placed hits can incapacitate most unarmored enemies, while head, neck, and exposed
pelvic shots remain effective against opponents wearing hard body armor.

Supersonic ammunition retains damage more effectively over distance and gives GRAY
GHOST its strongest extended-range performance. Subsonic ammunition carries greater
projectile mass but loses velocity and trajectory rapidly, making it most effective
inside the weapon’s intended close-to-intermediate engagement envelope.

At approximately 600 yards, GRAY GHOST can still inflict lethal damage against exposed
personnel, but shot placement becomes considerably more important. Poorly placed hits
may wound rather than immediately incapacitate, especially against heavily equipped
enemies.

PENETRATION

GRAY GHOST possesses a high penetration rating when loaded with the correct ammunition.
Supersonic and specialized penetration loads can defeat helmets, light protective
plates, vehicle bodywork, wooden cover, and some thin structural materials.

Its armor performance is ammunition-dependent. The standard projectile does not
automatically defeat every armor type, and the weapon cannot reliably penetrate heavy
rifle plates or reinforced structural cover simply because it is chambered in .300
Blackout.

Within the game’s damage model, heavy subsonic ammunition retains unusually strong
close-range barrier performance despite reduced velocity. This allows suppressed shots
to remain dangerous against helmets, light armor, and intermediate cover without making
stealth ammunition ineffective. Its penetration and damage fall off more quickly at
extended range than those of the supersonic load.

The weapon’s strongest armor-piercing behavior at long range is a gameplay abstraction
associated with specialized in-universe ammunition rather than an inherent property of
all real .300 BLK cartridges.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
AMMUNITION
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

GRAY GHOST can use multiple .300 Blackout ammunition types. Changing ammunition
requires replacing the complete magazine through the weapon-inspection interface; the
player cannot convert a partially loaded magazine from one ammunition state to another.

SUBSONIC AMMUNITION

Subsonic ammunition is GRAY GHOST’s default covert load. It offers:
- the lowest possible sound signature;
- no supersonic ballistic crack;
- reduced muzzle flash;
- high close-range damage;
- strong close-range barrier performance; and
- easier recoil control during rapid fire.

Its primary disadvantage is its curved trajectory. Bullet drop becomes significant
beyond ordinary intermediate range and severe at longer distances. The player must
apply substantial holdover well before reaching the weapon’s theoretical maximum range.

Subsonic ammunition is best suited to interiors, urban infiltration, guarded compounds,
and engagements inside approximately 200 to 300 yards.

SUPERSONIC AMMUNITION

Supersonic ammunition gives GRAY GHOST:
- a flatter trajectory;
- greater retained velocity;
- improved damage at extended range;
- stronger long-range penetration; and
- a more forgiving point of aim between approximately 200 and 600 yards.

It is considerably louder than subsonic ammunition. Even with the suppressor installed,
the projectile creates a distinctive sonic crack as it passes through the air. Enemies
may not immediately identify Spook’s exact firing position, but nearby personnel are
more likely to recognize that suppressed rifle fire is occurring.

In several missions, Tripper carries a limited supersonic magazine as a “break glass”
option. Loading it represents a change in tactical posture: the operation may remain
suppressed, but it is no longer truly quiet.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
SOUND PROFILE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

UNSUPPRESSED

When fired without its suppressor, GRAY GHOST is punishingly loud. Its short barrel
produces an abrupt muzzle blast and strong concussion, particularly inside rooms,
tunnels, and vehicles.

Unsuppressed fire immediately compromises stealth and may temporarily obscure nearby
dialogue or environmental audio. The effect is most pronounced in enclosed spaces,
where the report reflects from surrounding surfaces and makes the weapon feel more
violent than its compact dimensions suggest.

SUPPRESSED WITH SUPERSONIC AMMUNITION

The SOCOM300-SPS substantially reduces muzzle blast, flash, and the apparent location
of the shooter. It does not remove the supersonic crack created by the projectile.

The result is quieter and less disorienting than unsuppressed fire, but still
recognizable as rifle fire to enemies near the projectile’s flight path or impact point.
This configuration is intended for controlled combat rather than complete concealment.

SUPPRESSED WITH SUBSONIC AMMUNITION

Combining the suppressor with subsonic ammunition produces GRAY GHOST’s lowest sound
profile. The player primarily hears the weapon’s mechanical action, a moderated muzzle
report, and the projectile’s impact.

The weapon is not literally silent. Enemies standing near Spook, near the target, or
inside the same enclosed room may still hear the shot. More distant enemies are much
less likely to identify the shooter’s position, allowing the player to eliminate
isolated targets without automatically alerting an entire area.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
OPTICS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

GRAY GHOST uses a hybrid optical arrangement consisting of a non-magnified red-dot
sight and a hinged 3x magnifier.

With the magnifier folded aside, the Aimpoint T-2 provides a wide field of view and
extremely rapid target acquisition. This is the default configuration during room
clearing, movement through narrow spaces, and engagements against multiple close
targets. It also preserves the weapon’s VERY FAST ADS rating.

The magnifier can be moved behind the red dot without opening a customization menu.
When deployed, it provides enough magnification to identify partially exposed targets
and place precise shots at intermediate range. Its ADS rating becomes FAST, its field
of view narrows, and close-range target transitions become slightly less forgiving.

The transition does not change the weapon’s point of impact or reticle. Instead, it
allows the player to choose between two engagement priorities while retaining the same
aiming reference:

- red dot folded clear of the magnifier: speed, awareness, and close-range flexibility;
- magnifier engaged: target identification, precision, and intermediate-range control.

Offset iron sights provide an emergency backup if the primary optic is damaged or
temporarily obstructed.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
AVAILABILITY
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

GRAY GHOST is Spook’s signature primary and is normally available from the beginning
of his campaign. It cannot be permanently discarded, although mission scripting may
temporarily replace it when Spook must use a locally acquired or cover-appropriate
weapon.

Unlike 5.56x45 mm ammunition, .300 Blackout is deliberately scarce. Most hostile
factions do not carry it, preventing the player from treating GRAY GHOST as a weapon
with unlimited battlefield resupply.

Limited compatible ammunition can occasionally be recovered from Atlas personnel
equipped with .300 BLK MCX Rattler-type weapons. Mission caches, Victor Two Bravo
supply cases, and ammunition provided by Tripper remain its most reliable sources.

This limited ammunition economy offsets the weapon’s otherwise exceptional combination
of damage, stealth, accuracy, and handling.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
STRENGTHS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

- Exceptional accuracy for a compact automatic carbine.
- Very high close-to-intermediate-range damage.
- Very fast ADS with the bare red dot.
- Fast ADS with the magnifier engaged.
- Rapid weapon-transition time.
- Highly maneuverable inside buildings and vehicles.
- Red dot and 3x magnifier support rapid tactical range changes.
- Excellent performance with a suppressor.
- Subsonic and supersonic ammunition provide distinct tactical roles.
- Capable of precision hits well beyond its normal engagement range.
- Strong barrier and light-armor penetration with appropriate ammunition.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
WEAKNESSES
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

- .300 Blackout ammunition is comparatively scarce.
- Subsonic ammunition has severe bullet drop.
- Supersonic ammunition retains an audible ballistic crack when suppressed.
- Unsuppressed fire is extremely loud and concussive.
- Fully equipped configuration is slightly slower to reload and transition.
- Engaging the magnifier reduces ADS speed from VERY FAST to FAST.
- Magnification narrows peripheral awareness and is less forgiving at close range.
- The 3x magnifier is limited compared with a dedicated marksman optic.
- Extended-range performance depends heavily on player skill.
- Cannot reliably defeat heavy armor without appropriate ammunition and shot placement.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
REAL-WORLD BASIS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

The real Noveske N4 is an AR-pattern platform associated with compact configurations,
high-quality barrels, and strong mechanical accuracy. Noveske describes its .300
Blackout systems as suited to both subsonic suppressed and supersonic operation.

The SureFire SOCOM300-SPS is a real .30-caliber suppressor designed for .300 BLK and
optimized particularly for short-barreled, subsonic use while remaining compatible
with supersonic ammunition.

Real magnifiers introduce practical trade-offs similar to those represented in the
game. They improve identification and precision at distance but narrow the sight picture
and generally require more deliberate eye alignment than an unmagnified red dot. The
state-dependent ADS rating is therefore intentional: the magnifier expands capability,
but it does not improve every handling characteristic.

The game exaggerates the weapon’s effectiveness against armor at extreme range. Real
.300 BLK performance depends heavily on projectile construction and velocity; heavy
subsonic bullets do not become armor-piercing merely because of their mass. The
fictional weapon’s extended-range armor performance is a characteristic of specialized
in-universe ammunition and the game’s damage model.

TRIVIA
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

- GRAY GHOST and Spook’s HK45CT, NOTHING, share a matching dark-gray finish.
- The build name appears in the lower portion of the weapon-inspection interface.
- The faded “300 BLK” marking on the dust cover is visible only during inspection.
- The top-rail pressure switch is positioned to double as Spook’s thumb stop when he
  uses a C-clamp grip.
- Subsonic ammunition is the default load, while supersonic magazines are commonly
  treated as an emergency escalation option.
- The MAWL-DA is not initially available and must be acquired after BURNING DAYLIGHT.
- Switching from subsonic to supersonic ammunition often serves as an auditory signal
  that a stealth operation has changed into a direct engagement.
- Despite its long-range capability, GRAY GHOST is classified as a carbine rather
  than a designated marksman rifle.
- The 3x magnifier deliberately lowers ADS speed by one tier, reinforcing that it is
  used when additional identification and precision are worth sacrificing some speed.
