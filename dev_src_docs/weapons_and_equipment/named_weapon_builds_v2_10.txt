0. v2.10 ADDITION / GOVERNANCE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

1. SANDMAN fills Hawthorne's previously open persistent sidearm slot.
2. SANDMAN and DEAD BLOW are distinct Hawthorne builds:
   - SANDMAN = 2019 Hudson H9 personal sidearm.
   - DEAD BLOW = black HK MP5SD-style suppressed 9x19mm SMG.
3. SANDMAN predates Hawthorne's New American Frontier / Vanguard involvement and
   has been carried since his early Kill Devil Hills Detachment period.
4. Base accessory lock: SANDMAN carries a Streamlight TLR-3 weapon light mounted
   distinctly low beneath the bore on the pistol's accessory rail.
5. The TLR-3 switch paddle sits high enough relative to the light body, trigger
   guard and firing hand to remain naturally reachable despite the low mount.
6. The threaded barrel is locked physical configuration. The suppressor is removable
   rather than permanently fitted.
7. SANDMAN's canonical suppressor is a black, flat-sided, just-under-seven-inch
   SilencerCo Osprey 45 2.0. Do not substitute a generic cylindrical suppressor in
   build references.
8. The Osprey 45 2.0 provides exceptional shot-report dampening, especially with later
   9x19mm subsonic ammunition.
9. The suppressor's forward mass shifts the center of gravity toward the muzzle,
   making ADS noticeably slower and repeated follow-up shots slightly less stable
   than the bare-pistol state.
10. The tan/FDE finish visually reinforces the SANDMAN name, while the Hudson H9's
    unusual futuristic profile evokes the Logan's Run namesake without replica styling.
11. When suppressed, the elongated flat-sided muzzle profile creates SANDMAN's
    strongest and most distinctive silhouette.
12. The literary name is a nod to Logan's Run, one of Hawthorne's favorite novels.
13. Unsuppressed handling lock: exceptionally soft recoil, very low muzzle rise,
    great ergonomics, very fast ADS, high aim stability and rapid accurate follow-up shots.
14. Ammunition progression lock: common low-cost standard 9x19mm remains effective
    against soft and lightly armored targets; later 9x19mm subsonic ammunition makes
    suppressed SANDMAN exceptionally quiet at a slight damage/range cost.
15. Reliability lock: SANDMAN is slightly less reliable than a regular maintained
    M1911 reference lane. Malfunctions remain condition-driven and uncommon rather
    than a flat random-jam tax; Hawthorne clears simple stoppages rapidly through the
    contextual restore-to-ready input.
16. Registry counts increase by one named identity and one substantially specified
    source-associated identity.
