0. v2.10 ADDITION / GOVERNANCE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

1. SANDMAN fills Hawthorne's previously open persistent sidearm slot.
2. SANDMAN and DEAD BLOW are distinct Hawthorne builds:
   - SANDMAN = 2019 Hudson H9 personal sidearm.
   - DEAD BLOW = black HK MP5SD-style suppressed 9x19mm SMG.
3. SANDMAN predates Hawthorne's New American Frontier / Vanguard involvement and
   has been carried since his early Kill Devil Hills Detachment period.
4. The threaded barrel is locked physical configuration. A suppressor-compatible,
   mission-specific configuration is canonical, but no suppressor is permanently
   fitted by default.
5. The tan/FDE finish visually reinforces the SANDMAN name, while the Hudson H9's
   unusual futuristic profile evokes the Logan's Run namesake without replica styling.
6. When suppressed, the elongated muzzle profile creates SANDMAN's strongest and
   most distinctive silhouette.
7. The literary name is a nod to Logan's Run, one of Hawthorne's favorite novels.
8. Handling lock: exceptionally soft recoil, very low muzzle rise, great ergonomics,
   very fast ADS, high aim stability and rapid accurate follow-up shots.
9. Ammunition progression lock: common low-cost standard 9x19mm remains effective
   against soft and lightly armored targets; later 9x19mm subsonic ammunition makes
   suppressed SANDMAN exceptionally quiet and stable at a slight damage/range cost.
10. Reliability lock: SANDMAN is slightly less reliable than a regular maintained
    M1911 reference lane. Malfunctions remain condition-driven and uncommon rather
    than a flat random-jam tax; Hawthorne clears simple stoppages rapidly through the
    contextual restore-to-ready input.
11. Registry counts increase by one named identity and one substantially specified
    source-associated identity.
