HANDLING IDENTITY
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
TROOPER should communicate compact 7.62x51mm authority with real handling cost.

Unsuppressed state:
- fast enough for aggressive SAS movement, but heavier and more demanding than a
  compact 5.56mm carbine
- strong recoil impulse and muzzle disturbance
- effective semi-automatic precision from close to medium range
- controlled short automatic bursts possible at close range
- sustained automatic fire rapidly reduces practical accuracy and visual control

Suppressed state:
- lower visible and audible signature than the unsuppressed state
- added forward mass and slower presentation
- preserved full-power recoil character
- not Hollywood-quiet and not a universal handling upgrade

The 1-4x LPVO should support a genuine trade-off between rapid low-magnification
close work and deliberate magnified identification / engagement. It should not be
treated as a catch-all optic with no speed or field-of-view cost.
