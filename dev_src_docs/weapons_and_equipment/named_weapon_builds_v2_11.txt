0. v2.11 ADDITION / GOVERNANCE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

1. MACE is locked as the primary playable protagonist of Project Warriors: Who Dares Wins.
2. TROOPER is Mace's persistent signature primary / main campaign weapon.
3. TROOPER is a DSA SA58 OSW-pattern 7.62x51mm NATO select-fire compact
   full-power rifle in the light automatic rifle lane.
4. TROOPER uses a 1-4x LPVO. Exact manufacturer, reticle and mount remain open.
5. TROOPER carries a tactical white light at 3 o'clock on the forward section of
   the front handguard side rail.
6. TROOPER has a removable suppressed configuration available when mission
   signature requirements justify it.
7. The suppressor is not permanently fitted and does not erase the handling and
   signature consequences of a short full-power 7.62x51mm rifle.
8. Exact barrel length, suppressor, muzzle interface, light model, control routing,
   furniture, sling, finish, magazine presentation and tuning remain open.
9. TROOPER is conflict-free within the prior authoritative registry chain; no
   existing registered named weapon identity uses this build name.
10. Registry counts increase by one named identity and one substantially specified
    source-associated identity.
