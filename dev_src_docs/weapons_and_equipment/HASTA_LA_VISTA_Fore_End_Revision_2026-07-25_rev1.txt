================================================================================
02. CANON REPLACEMENT
================================================================================

The shortened pump/forend now carries two separate components:

A. REAR ANGLED GRIP
- compact CNC-machined aluminum grip
- approximately 8-degree angle
- mounted on the rear portion of the shortened M-LOK forend, closer to the shooter
- near-vertical visual read with a slight rearward cant
- optimized for positive pump manipulation and stable shouldered control

B. FORWARD HAND STOP
- separate compact hand stop
- mounted farther forward on the underside of the shortened forend
- positioned to index between or against the support-hand index and middle fingers during a conventional or partial C-clamp-style hold
- provides forward retention and repeatable hand placement during cycling

The hand stop is not integrated into the rear grip.

================================================================================
03. HANDLING FUNCTION
================================================================================

REAR-GRIP HOLD
Chris wraps the support hand around the compact 8-degree grip. This gives strong leverage for pump manipulation, stable aiming, and positive control during rapid follow-up shots.

PARTIAL C-CLAMP HOLD
Chris may grip the shortened forend more conventionally and use the separate forward hand stop as an indexing/retention point. This improves access to the already established momentary and constant-on light controls positioned along the upper/fixed handguard area.

The two-component arrangement supports quicker re-indexing and better switch access than the previous one-piece angled foregrip while preserving the shotgun's compact visual identity.
