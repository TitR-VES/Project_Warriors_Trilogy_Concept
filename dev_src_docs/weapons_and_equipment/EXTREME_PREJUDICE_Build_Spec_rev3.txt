--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
2. DEFINING OPTIC LOCK
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

EXTREME PREJUDICE is equipped with a HIGH-POWERED THERMAL-IMAGING OPTIC.

This is a protected named-build characteristic and is the principal configuration
feature that distinguishes EXTREME PREJUDICE from the separate unnamed CheyTac M200
available in Goldberg's Temple Pavilion during SINS OF OUR FATHERS.

Encounter consequence:
- ordinary foliage and partial visual concealment are less dependable against Mansell;
- the player should favor true line-of-sight breaks, solid terrain, structures, berm
  geometry or other hard cover when avoiding Mansell's observation and fire;
- the thermal optic does not authorize impossible vision through arbitrary solid
  geometry;
- exact thermal palette, sensor refresh, zoom range, reticle, UI treatment and
  obstruction model remain production-tuning details unless later locked.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
3. TEMPLE PAVILION M200 DISTINCTION
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

The Temple Pavilion CheyTac M200 remains a separate, unnamed rifle.

Its optic is a REGULAR HIGH-POWERED PRECISION OPTIC WITH NO THERMAL-IMAGING
CAPABILITY.

Therefore:
- EXTREME PREJUDICE = Mansell / named build / high-powered thermal optic;
- Temple Pavilion M200 = unnamed / regular high-powered optic / no thermal imaging.

The Temple rifle never inherits the EXTREME PREJUDICE name or thermal-optic lock.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
4. SINS OF OUR FATHERS ENCOUNTER PLACEMENT
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

The current detailed East McFarlane map places Mansell and his mobile observation
post inside the fortified bermed I-Dome air-defense site at the island's highest
point in pre-transform reference geometry.

The encounter remains an overwatch / precision slot. The player may solve it through
multiple approaches, including direct assault, the Temple Pavilion counter-sniper
lane, or the long-range southern-flagpole firing position developed in the current
map source.

or configuration of this weapon.
