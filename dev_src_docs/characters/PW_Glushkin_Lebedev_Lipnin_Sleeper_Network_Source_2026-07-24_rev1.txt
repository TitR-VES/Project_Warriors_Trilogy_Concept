01. VYACHESLAV “SLAVA” GLUSHKIN
Full working name: Vyacheslav Timofeevich Glushkin.
Birth year: 1972 — LOCKED.
Operational role: FSB field officer attached to the occupation/counter-resistance structure in Calloway, Redbud, and the wider Harlan County lane.
Narrative role: principal professional antagonist of Romeo’s AMERICAN FREEDOM local-resistance arc.

COVER IDENTITY
Name: Brent Henry Tibbets.
Profession: aerospace electrical engineer.
Employer: Specter Aerospace, Peabody, Massachusetts.
Home/identity origin: Haverhill, Massachusetts.
Education cover: MIT electrical-engineering/aeronautics pathway.

The cover is not a superficial alias. Glushkin has lived inside it long enough to attend block parties, maintain workplace rhythms, understand regional American speech, and appear ordinary to people who have known him for years.

STOLEN-IDENTITY FOUNDATION
The real Brent Tibbets was a vulnerable young man with no close family safety net whose disappearance did not produce a clean institutional alarm. The illegal program’s cruelty is shown through administrative absence: the real person can vanish, while the operative wearing his life becomes documented and socially legible.

Do not overdefine the real Tibbets’s final fate without a dedicated scene. The important current truth is that Glushkin’s cover depends on another man being erased.

EDUCATION / TRAINING DIRECTION

- Russian linguistic/foreign-service education;
- intensive clandestine language and cultural training;
- FSB officer training;
- native Russian and exceptionally convincing American English;
- broad multilingual capability appropriate to a long-prepared illegal;
- conventional infantry/weapons familiarity and strong chess ability.

CHARACTER REGISTER
Glushkin is sophisticated, composed, observant, and capable of sudden decisive violence. The Christoph Waltz/Hans Landa comparison is an archetype note, not a likeness or imitation instruction. His distinguishing feature is an internal professional belief system: he respects competence, understands the human appeal of the country he infiltrated, and still chooses his assigned position until the institution burns him.

03. DOUGLAS NASMITH / GRIGORYI LEBEDEV
Working biography:
- born March 11, 1970, Nizhny Novgorod, USSR;
- long-duration U.S. cover as Douglas Paul Nasmith;
- approximately thirty-three years embedded in the Boston area by 2028;
- senior finance/administrative function inside Boston City Hall;
- activated roughly three months before PANDORA;
- collects infrastructure, service-access, contract, structural, route, and chokepoint information useful to later invasion/occupation planning;
- tasked to activate Glushkin.

CHARACTER FUNCTION
Lebedev is not primarily a field-action antagonist. He represents the institutionally embedded illegal who has become part of the machinery of a city. Removing him is politically and evidentially harder than defeating a uniformed operative.

GAME 3 POTENTIAL
Lebedev may become an institutional antagonist or evidence node in later Boston/restoration-era material, but survival, exposure, mission placement, and final disposition remain open.

04. GENNADIY FYODOROVICH LIPNIN
Role:
- senior KGB officer who continues into the FSB after the Soviet collapse;
- strategist/architect of the long-duration illegal program that produced assets such as Glushkin and Lebedev;
- recruited Glushkin in 1995, when Glushkin was twenty-three;
- Moscow-based institutional operator with close oversight of subordinate officers and deep-cover assets;
- known for burning liabilities rather than compassionately reassigning them.

Keep Lipnin deliberately incomplete. His power comes from being the institutional shape behind the program, not from appearing in every field scene.

KOROLYOV IMPLICATION
A senior man associated with Glushkin’s clandestine preparation may be implied as Lipnin without Glushkin necessarily being given a transparent personal relationship or full program picture. Do not force an explicit reveal earlier than the story needs.

05. FACE TO FACE — OPTIONAL PLAYER REVEAL
FACE TO FACE is an optional/conditional AMERICAN FREEDOM mission asset.

Setup:
- Glushkin arranges a meeting through a channel Romeo trusts enough to verify;
- location: the grain-elevator pull-off associated with earlier contact history;
- Glushkin requests Romeo alone; Aldridge may remain unseen in overwatch as insurance;
- civilian clothing and reduced cover-performance allow Romeo to see something closer to the real man.

Conversation function:

- Glushkin argues that the occupation can slowly compress the resistance without a decisive battle;
- Romeo confronts him with the fact that Brent Tibbets lived among Americans long enough to understand what is worth defending;
- Glushkin quietly admits that he understands the value without changing sides in that moment;
- both leave with better reconnaissance on the other.

06. BURN NOTICE / NUMBER STATION
Late in the campaign, after the AFC survives and Glushkin’s assignment is failing, a number-station message informs him that the institution has burned him.

Protected details:
- sequence: 8-3-1-6-5-0-2-7-4-1;
- Glushkin writes “Понятно” (“Understood”) in a notebook;
- he folds and keeps the page instead of destroying it;
- the retained page is a small act of private ownership after a life built around instructions and disposable identities.

The burn scene should be staged before the final confrontation, not treated as a posthumous lore dump.

PERSONAL CHOICE
After the burn notice, Glushkin’s final actions are his own. Lipnin has discarded him. The story must not reduce the confrontation to “he was only following orders.” He knows the cost of what he did and does not ask Romeo to absolve him.

FINAL-CONFRONTATION REGISTER
- Glushkin’s American cover accent falls away enough for his natural Russian speech pattern to become perceptible;
- he acknowledges that he did what he believed he had to do and would have repeated it;
- he recognizes that the end has arrived and that Romeo must act;
- no operatic begging, ideological speech, or sudden sentimental redemption.
