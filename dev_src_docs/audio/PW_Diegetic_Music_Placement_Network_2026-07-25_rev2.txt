01. SYSTEM RULES
================================================================================

Every placement requires:
- a believable in-world source
- a clear narrative function beyond taste or literal lyric matching

Most cues should be partial, muffled, interrupted, displaced by action/dialogue, or heard from an imperfect source. Music is world texture first and never replaces campaign score identity.
