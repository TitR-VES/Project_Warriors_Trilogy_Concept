PURPOSE

Capture the owner-confirmed ThreeHalf heavy autonomous UGV behavior introduced through
the detailed SINS OF OUR FATHERS East McFarlane map, separating protected gameplay
logic from lower-level hardware and tuning details that remain open.

IDENTITY / ROLE

ThreeHalf is a heavy centaur-like quadrupedal autonomous unmanned ground vehicle used
as part of Goldberg's private island security architecture.

Core gameplay identity:
- extremely tough to disable once active;
- not a mandatory combat encounter;
- can be avoided entirely by a careful player;
- normally remains docked and in standby until the local security state escalates;
- functions as a consequence for reckless escalation rather than a required boss fight.

SINS OF OUR FATHERS — ACTIVATION LOGIC

ThreeHalves begin the mission docked / dormant.

A sufficiently high local alarm level can wake them into combat mode. In particular,
reckless repeated aggressive engagement of Goldberg's armed enforcer UAVs can escalate
the defense system far enough that ThreeHalves are activated and deployed.

A direct attack on a docked ThreeHalf may also provoke activation unless the unit has
already been safely disabled through the stealth sabotage route below.

The intended systemic message is that destroying every automated security asset is not
always the safest strategy. Excessive aggression can make the island's defense response
materially heavier.

STEALTH DOCK-SABOTAGE ROUTE

If Victor Two Bravo can approach a ThreeHalf docking station without triggering the
unit while it is still docked and on standby, Tripper can sabotage the unit.

The sabotage action fries / disables the ThreeHalf's power-management circuitry,
causing the docked unit to shut down without waking into combat mode.

Protected behavior:
- requires the ThreeHalf to remain docked and dormant long enough for the team to reach
  the station stealthily;
- the sabotage does not wake the unit first and then require a combat shutdown;
- a successfully sabotaged unit is removed as an active threat;
- the route is optional and rewards reconnaissance, stealth and restraint;
- the mission remains completable without fighting or sabotaging a ThreeHalf if the
  player avoids the escalation state entirely.

COMBAT / DURABILITY RULE

Once fully active, a ThreeHalf should read as a serious heavy-security threat and be
extremely difficult to disable through ordinary direct fire. Exact hit points, armor
zones, mobility kills, component damage, ammunition vulnerability and heavy-weapon
counters remain production tuning unless separately locked later.

Do not tune the unit so weakly that activating one feels equivalent to fighting a normal
security guard or light drone. Conversely, do not make direct combat mandatory for the
canonical mission flow.

CURRENT MAP-LEVEL HARDWARE NOTE

The detailed East McFarlane KML currently depicts active ThreeHalves carrying modified
belt-fed AR-15-pattern automatic rifles with spare linked ammunition on the rear body.
This is retained as the current map-level visual/loadout concept, but the exact weapon
model, ammunition capacity, armor package, sensor suite, locomotion specification and
animation set remain open unless later promoted into a dedicated hardware lock.

SECURITY-SYSTEM SCOPE

ThreeHalf belongs to Goldberg's privately sourced property-security architecture.
That architecture remains:
- local to the island/property;
- fully under Goldberg's control;
- protected from / isolated from PANDORA;
- not a backdoor into AEGIS, U.S. military or wider strategic systems.

FICTIONALIZATION / MAP RELATIONSHIP

The detailed SINS OF OUR FATHERS KML is pre-transform planning geometry. Production
applies the locked east-west fictionalization mirror and may subtly alter shoreline,
architecture and secondary terrain while preserving mission topology. ThreeHalf dock
placement may move with that coherent transform so long as the stealth-approach and
optional-sabotage function survives.
