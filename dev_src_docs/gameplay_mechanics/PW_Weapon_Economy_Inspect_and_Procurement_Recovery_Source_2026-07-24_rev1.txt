1. WEAPON-SYSTEM NORTH STAR
- Mostly realistic real-world weapons appropriate to the 2028 setting, with a small, clearly bounded near-future prototype layer.
- Rarity uses both exploration/side-objective discovery and named/elite-enemy drops.

2. FACTION WEAPON LANGUAGE
US / NATO / DAOG
- Broad 5.56 NATO availability is the baseline.
- AEGIS/DAOG should visibly carry a multinational European procurement character rather than reading as a reskinned U.S. unit.
- Ground Branch favors compact, suppressed, deniable platforms.

Russian forces
- Line units retain recognizable AK-family, PK-family, SVD, and RPG texture.
- Elite VDV/FSB/Vympel encounters step into newer or specialized platforms.
- AS Val/VSS/SR-3M/9A-91 presence is a readable escalation signal: the player is no longer fighting ordinary line troops.
- Shipboard Vympel kit should be compact, suppressed/subsonic, and optimized for confined steel spaces.

KPA / DPRK
- Older and indigenous designs should make KPA formations feel materially different from Russian formations.
- Type 58/68-family weapons support the 7.62x39 scavenge economy.
- Elite indigenous systems should feel unfamiliar and unsettling rather than merely exotic.

Los Sietes
- Standard forces mix cartel-accessible AK/AR/shotgun/handgun stock.
- Elite sicarios use professionally sourced military-grade hardware.
- K Lounge and Bisutri-aligned teams must read as capable paramilitary professionals, not costume-gangsters.

Atlas / War Wolves
- Platform overlap with AEGIS and Ground Branch is deliberate. Shared procurement creates an uncomfortable institutional mirror.
- War Wolves remain among the most individually dangerous human enemies in Game 1.

3. AMMUNITION ECONOMY
- 5.56 NATO: universal baseline; most reliable resupply across campaigns.
- 7.62x39: richest scavenge pool. Russian, KPA, and Los Sietes drops make the CMMG Mk47 reward from DOWN RODEO a campaign-spanning infrastructure choice rather than a one-level novelty.
- 9x39: self-contained specialist economy introduced with Vympel/FSB platforms; sustained through enemy drops when those forces are present.
- .300 BLK: premium and scarce. GRAY GHOST should feel expensive to feed. Limited Atlas MCX Rattler drops may provide rare battlefield resupply; it is not a normal common-enemy caliber.
- Ammo availability should reinforce faction geography and mission progression, not behave as a universal abstract pool.

4. INSPECT WEAPON MECHANIC
Purpose
- Inspection is tactile characterization, readable weapon information, and field-modification access.
- It must feel like the playable character handling a physical object, not opening a floating inventory page.

Class grammar
- Rifles/carbines/DMRs/LMGs: arms-length rotation, natural support-hand repositioning.
- Pistols/compact SMGs: strong-side hand profile; weapon-specific checks where mechanically appropriate.
- Shotguns: controlled rack-and-check motion.

Signature behavior
- Signature builds may receive longer, more specific animations.
- Build-name overlays are selective. Absence can be a character statement; do not force every weapon into the same presentation rule.
- Current named-build sources decide which weapons display a name and how.

Field modification
- Semi-transparent attachment labels appear only at relevant points.
- Field-removable items may include QD suppressors/optics, lights, laser/IR modules, foregrips, and ammunition type where supported.
- Fixed or permanently mounted hardware receives no false interaction prompt.
- Physical removal and ammunition changes take real time and are not instant/skippable tactical menu actions.
- GRAY GHOST subsonic/supersonic switching requires a magazine change and should carry contact risk.

Gunsmith boundary
- Full between-mission workbench supports broader categories, including internal performance parts.
- Signature weapons can be tuned, but their core identity cannot be cosmetically erased.
- Character cleaning grammar remains: Romeo fast/functional; Ducky methodical/checking; Spook slow/meditative. Other characters require authored treatment before use.
