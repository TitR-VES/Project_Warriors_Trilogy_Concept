01. COMPATIBLE WEAPONS
================================================================================

The unique animation applies to compatible pump-action shotguns with:

02. EMPTY-RELOAD SEQUENCE
================================================================================

When the shotgun is empty, Nick:
1. brings the weapon inward and rolls it so the loading port faces upward and slightly right in first-person view;
2. opens the action, or begins with it already open after the final shot;
3. places one emergency shell directly through the ejection port;
4. drives the fore-end forward, closing the action and chambering that shell;
5. keeps the weapon inverted and acquires four shells in a competition-derived loading grip;
6. performs a fast two-plus-two sweep into the magazine tube;
7. returns the weapon to a firing-ready orientation.

MECHANICAL CORRECTION
A pump-action shotgun does not "drop the bolt" like a semiautomatic. After the ejection-port load, Nick closes/chambers the action by driving the fore-end forward before loading the tube.
