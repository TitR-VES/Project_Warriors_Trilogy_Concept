PURPOSE
This source locks the player-facing and internal designation hierarchy for the DAOG intelligence / counterintelligence element formerly referred to primarily as TRU/SIGHT.

CANON LOCK
MINERVA is the normal player-facing, spoken, and unit-identity name for the DAOG intelligence / counterintelligence element whose formal internal designation remains TRU/SIGHT (also acceptably styled TRUSIGHT).

TRU/SIGHT expands to:
Tactical Reconnaissance Unit / Signals Intelligence and Geospatial Heuristics Team.

TRU/SIGHT is NOT retired and is NOT replaced as the formal organizational designation. The new hierarchy is additive:

- MINERVA — normal unit identifier used by DAOG personnel, allied characters, mission briefings, and player-facing references where a concise team name is appropriate.
- TRU/SIGHT / TRUSIGHT — formal internal designation used in classified documentation, personnel records, technical systems, intelligence products, administrative references, and other contexts where bureaucratic precision is appropriate.
- MINERVA Detachment — acceptable expanded spoken or written form when the text needs to make clear that MINERVA is a DAOG element rather than a software system, operation, or abstract capability.

PERSONNEL CONTINUITY
Jaeger / Karl Alexander Stanzler remains personnel of this same organization. Existing references that place him in TRU/SIGHT remain valid; player-facing dialogue may naturally describe him as MINERVA personnel.

USAGE PRINCIPLE
Use MINERVA for the people and the recognizable unit identity. Use TRU/SIGHT when the writing foregrounds the formal apparatus, product, record, or organizational designation.

Examples of valid in-world phrasing:
- "Send it to Minerva."
- "Minerva picked up the same traffic overnight."
- "Get Minerva on the feed."
- "TRU/SIGHT assessment came back ten minutes ago. Minerva thinks it's real."

NAMING / MYTHOLOGY NOTE
Minerva is the Roman goddess associated with wisdom, knowledge, strategic warfare, and crafts; Athena is the Greek counterpart. MINERVA is intentionally retained because DAOG's naming family is mythological rather than a strict Greek-only taxonomy, and because the name better fits the unit's cold institutional intelligence identity.

SUPERSESSION / NON-SUPERSESSION
- The earlier PYTHIA designation remains retired.
- ORACLE remains Lt. Tara Halliday's callsign and is unaffected.
- The July 6, 2026 canon lock establishing TRU/SIGHT remains valid except that MINERVA is now layered above it as the normal player-facing/unit identifier.
- Do not rewrite TRU/SIGHT into MINERVA in every archival or technical reference. The distinction between unit identity and formal designation is intentional.
