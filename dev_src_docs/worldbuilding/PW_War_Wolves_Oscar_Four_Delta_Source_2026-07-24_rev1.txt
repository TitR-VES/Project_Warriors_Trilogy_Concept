1. CORE ORIGIN LOCK
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

The War Wolves are the three surviving core members of OSCAR FOUR DELTA, a highly
compartmentalized CIA Global Response Staff / Ground Branch-adjacent action cell
most operational during the mid-2000s.

Oscar Four Delta operated at approximately the same clandestine-action tier as
Victor Two Bravo, but as its moral inverse: exceptionally capable, operationally
useful and protected for too long because the results arrived through channels
that discouraged scrutiny.

Their collapse was not one clean rogue mission. It was cumulative. The cell crossed
lines repeatedly, escalated from unauthorized violence into atrocity and criminal
service work, and eventually faced a credible risk of exposure or consequence.
Goldberg's KINGMAKER / Shadow Underground architecture did not corrupt otherwise
clean men. It recognized men who had already demonstrated what they were willing
to do, protected them and continued using them as deniable wet-work assets.

Officially, the men are missing, dead, administratively unresolved or attached to
records that effectively never existed. In practice, Oscar Four Delta became the
War Wolves.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

"Digger," "Lucky" and "Rip" predate the War Wolves identity. They come from the
men's earlier clandestine-service histories and were used by people who knew them
before the wolf masks, CRIMSON designations and CFIM package.

The nicknames must not be treated as War Wolves branding or explained through
front-facing villain dialogue. They belong in old personnel fragments, burned
rosters, Regent annotations, PYTHIA cross-references or testimony from someone old
enough to remember Oscar Four Delta.

No single literal nickname-origin story is locked here. Do not invent one as hard
canon without a later source pass.

4. THE PACT WITH POWER / WRIST MARKS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Each War Wolf carries a small, old and deliberately plain Roman numeral tattoo on
the inside of the left wrist: I, II or III according to the CRIMSON designation.

The marks originate in the private pact through which the three men accepted
protection and continued employment from Goldberg's power structure. They are not
occult symbols, large wolf insignia or theatrical gang marks. They are closer to
inventory or ownership marks that the owners willingly accepted: the moment they
chose impunity over consequence.

Game 1 may expose the marks only through restrained inspect-adjacent or looting
presentation. Their meaning is not explained during SINS OF OUR FATHERS.

5. "ORGANS FOR DOLLARS" — INSTITUTIONAL SCAR-TISSUE RUMOR
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

A very small circle inside the Agency carried an old black joke that "Oscar Four
Delta" meant "organs for dollars."

The phrase was never official, never part of normal records and never repeated
outside closed rooms. It functions as institutional scar tissue: people close to
the compartment knew enough to understand that something was profoundly wrong,
but the institution found ways not to confront it.

The conceptual truth beneath the rumor is that Oscar Four Delta likely provided
cover, facilitation, protection, movement, site security, witness removal or direct
action support for a domestic body-exploitation, organ-harvesting, trafficking and
abduction pipeline connected to Goldberg's KINGMAKER / Shadow Underground network.

The exact mechanics are intentionally not fully exposed. The story should establish
what the men were willing to enable without turning the subject into procedural or
exploitative lore-dumping. The load-bearing truth is moral and institutional:

- human life and bodily autonomy were expendable when an objective, access or profit
  demanded it;
- Oscar Four Delta crossed from clandestine violence into criminal atrocity as
  service work;
- people inside the system knew enough to make a joke, but not enough people were
  willing or able to stop them;
- Goldberg's network protected useful monsters rather than creating them from
  innocence.

A suitable later-file presentation model is a short annotation rather than a full
expository dossier:

  O4D. Graves / Underwood / Mansell.
  Old joke was "organs for dollars."
  Nobody laughed unless they already knew.

This wording is a presentation model, not mandatory final dialogue.
