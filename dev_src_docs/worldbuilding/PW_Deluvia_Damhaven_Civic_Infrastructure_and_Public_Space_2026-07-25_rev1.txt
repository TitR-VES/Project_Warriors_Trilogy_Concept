================================================================================
01. CIVIC PREMISE
================================================================================

Deluvia is permissive in trade and personal conduct but severe about threats to collective survival. Its residents do not see a contradiction between broad freedom and narrow, ruthlessly enforced existential rules.

Core principle:
Freedom in Deluvia exists because certain acts are treated as attacks on the continued existence of the city.

Protected interests include:
- physical integrity of the Damhaven structure
- digital/control-system integrity
- water, power, heat, food, and buoyancy systems
- freedom from human trafficking and coercive ownership
- internal security against treason, sabotage, murder, and deliberate catastrophic endangerment
- the platform city's ability to remain a neutral commercial and refuge space

================================================================================
02. GOVERNING LEGAL STRUCTURE
================================================================================

HARBOR COUNCIL
The principal civic/executive leadership body. It sets high-level rules, emergency restrictions, trade controls, and collective-security policy.

HARBOR MAGISTRATES
Handle ordinary criminal, civil, commercial, and port disputes. Their authority is practical and records-driven rather than ceremonial.

TRIBUNAL COUNCIL
Convenes for the gravest cases, especially treason, human trafficking, murder, deliberate sabotage, or actions that knowingly endanger Damhaven's population, freedom, or physical/electronic integrity.

Capital punishment is rare and reserved for the most severe proven offenses. Deluvia's justice system should feel harsh, procedural, and survival-oriented rather than sadistic or arbitrary.

================================================================================
03. OPHANGINGSKADE
================================================================================

NAME
Ophangingskade — "Hanging Quay."

LOCATION
Remote northwestern industrial edge of Damhaven. Sparse, wind-beaten, and largely uninhabited outside guards, maintenance personnel, officials, and occasional spectators.

FUNCTION
The district contains the facilities used for the rare administration of capital sentences:
- a harbor-crane hanging site associated especially with trafficking and predatory offenses
- a separate open firing-squad structure with a substantial backstop, associated especially with treason, armed sabotage, security betrayal, and attacks on critical infrastructure

PUNISHMENT PHILOSOPHY
Deluvia does not celebrate execution as civic entertainment, but it does not hide sovereign punishment. The remote location keeps it out of ordinary public life while preserving its function as a visible boundary of the social order.

The source thread establishes that bodies from major public hanging sentences may remain displayed for twenty-four hours before disposal at sea, with denial of burial treated as part of the sentence. Use this detail sparingly. It is a worldbuilding marker of Deluvia's severity, not a repeated spectacle or collectible set piece.

04. POWER SYSTEM
================================================================================

Damhaven uses a redundant mixed-energy ecology rather than one miracle source.

PRIMARY / ROUTINE GENERATION
- extensive solar-panel fields across sun-exposed upper surfaces
- multiple wind-turbine installations integrated into upper decks and exposed edges
- tidal/current generation where local marine conditions support it
- distributed battery/storage systems and local microgrids
- prioritized load shedding for critical systems during shortages

The visible result is an engineered survival city: cables, inverters, maintenance catwalks, replacement panels, patched turbine housings, and constant repair activity.

RELIABILITY RULE
No source is perfectly reliable. Weather, salt, corrosion, battle damage, seasonal demand, and spare-part scarcity require redundancy and disciplined energy accounting.

================================================================================
05. WATER AND FLOATING AGRICULTURE
================================================================================

Damhaven's freshwater system includes a small isolated near-shore/off-platform tank installation holding filtered water. The stored volume is gradually replaced and cycled rather than treated as an untouched reserve.

Functions:
- emergency buffer against treatment or intake disruption
- physical separation from the main platform's contamination risks
- visible proof that water security is a permanent civic concern

Floating or semi-floating wheat-growing installations supplement food supply. They should read as expensive, limited, engineered production rather than utopian self-sufficiency.

The city still depends on trade. Local agriculture improves resilience and reduces immediate famine risk; it does not eliminate import dependence.

================================================================================
06. EMERGENCY BACKUP POWER
================================================================================

Several modified decommissioned tugboats are permanently anchored near the core and wired into Damhaven as diesel backup generators.

Visual identity:
- old workboats converted into emergency power stations
- heavy cable runs and shore connections
- maintenance scaffolds and exhaust stacks
- engines kept serviceable for rare full-grid emergencies

Fuel is finite. A large underwater or submerged-protected concrete storage silo holds a meaningful diesel reserve, but the city cannot run indefinitely on it.

Narrative rule:
Once the tugboat generators start, every hour matters. Fuel accounting becomes crisis-command pressure rather than a magic fallback.

07. HEAT / WASTE SYSTEM
================================================================================

OFFICIAL ENGLISH NAME
Damhaven Thermal Recovery Plant

OFFICIAL DUTCH NAME
Damhaven thermische terugwinningsinstallatie

ABBREVIATION
DTT

COLLOQUIAL NAMES
- the Fire Pit
- de Vuurplaats

The DTT is a reinforced lower-level waste-to-heat incinerator operated as critical infrastructure. It burns sorted general refuse to produce usable heat for:
- winter district heating
- communal cooking infrastructure
- workshops and drying rooms
- sanitation support
- emergency shelters

The plant is not environmentally clean. Its large smokestack is one of Damhaven's most visible industrial scars. That ugliness is accepted because the plant reduces solid-waste buildup, limits overboard dumping, lowers electric-heating demand, and keeps the city habitable in cold conditions.

Wastewater and human waste are handled through separate rudimentary treatment and controlled-discharge systems. Limited sanitary solids may enter the incineration stream where appropriate, but general refuse is the primary feedstock.

08. PUBLIC-SPACE PHILOSOPHY
================================================================================

Damhaven's accessible public areas should combine:
- inherited maritime-industrial structure
- improvised additions and repairs
- multilingual civic markings
- resident humor and irreverence
- memorial restraint
- practical landmarks that double as navigation and meeting points

Public space should prove that Deluvia is a lived city, not only a strategic platform or criminal market.

09. I [PROPELLER] DAMHAVEN CIVIC SIGN
================================================================================

COMMON NAME
The Damhaven sign / the propeller sign.

CONCEPT
A public sculpture echoes the real-world tourist-sign grammar of Amsterdam while becoming its own Deluvian object.

Reading:
I [SHIP PROPELLER] DAM

The surviving/reconstructed letters and inserted propeller visually imply "I love Damhaven" without using a heart.

PREFERRED CONSTRUCTION
- large freestanding I
- full-scale salvaged ship propeller in the heart position
- surviving or reconstructed letters DAM
- robust materials suitable for salt, wind, climbing, photographs, and repeated repair

COLOR / FINISH
- letters may be white or blue
- propeller may be gold, brass, bronze, or silver-toned
- visible wear, patching, and repainting are desirable

FUNCTION
- public meeting point
- player navigation landmark
- tourist/resident photo spot
- civic joke and genuine affection at once
- visual statement that Damhaven's identity comes from maritime salvage and adaptation

Avoid presenting it as pristine corporate placemaking. It should feel adopted by residents.

- Public landmarks should humanize the city and support navigation.
- The city remains dependent on trade, engineering labor, and political compromise.
