PREMISE
By the Nightfall era, the former Port of Los Angeles has become the Freeport of Los Angeles: a fortified, semi-autonomous traveler, merchant, and transit colony built into and around the old port infrastructure first visited by Romeo in Game 1.

It is not fully integrated into the California Republic, a Federal Loyalist enclave, or the neighborhood crime-lord territories around greater Los Angeles. It survives because every major actor benefits from a neutral Pacific gate that can move people, information, goods, and services.

================================================================================
01. PHYSICAL IDENTITY
================================================================================

Core fabric:
- surviving terminals, warehouses, cranes, yards, and service roads
- stacked shipping containers used as walls, buildings, and elevated platforms
- abandoned adjacent structures converted into housing, barracks, administration, workshops, and security posts
- scaffolded additions built above usable port infrastructure
- fortified gates and inspection lanes
- lookout towers, elevated walkways, and controlled approaches
- improvised utilities, markets, drinking spaces, lodging, repair shops, and storage

Outer defenses may include:
- containers stacked in reinforced patterns
- wrecked vehicles integrated into barriers
- concertina or razor wire
- controlled firing positions and observation points
- layered entry checks rather than one theatrical front gate

The Freeport should look heavily defended but commercially active, with constant evidence that security and trade are inseparable.

================================================================================
02. POLITICAL STATUS
================================================================================

The Freeport is a collective self-governing authority whose legitimacy comes from neutrality, throughput, and enforcement rather than national sovereignty.

It is tolerated by surrounding powers because it:
- connects Pacific trade routes to the former U.S. mainland
- handles travelers, couriers, and deniable transit
- provides fuel, repair, storage, information, and brokerage
- gives factions a place to trade without formally recognizing one another
- reduces pressure on neighboring territories that cannot independently maintain the same port capacity

No faction-based violence is tolerated inside. Political enemies may trade or pass through under Freeport rules, but cannot openly settle their conflict within the port.

================================================================================
03. ECONOMY
================================================================================

Common trade:
- information and route access
- fuel, batteries, medicine, parts, and food
- weapons, ammunition, and restricted equipment
- salvage and industrial services
- identity, transport, communication, and accommodation services
- regulated dangerous goods where containment rules are met

Payment may use cryptocurrency, commodity exchange, service credit, or other mutually accepted instruments because no single post-federal legal tender is universally trusted.

The port is not lawless. It is a rules-heavy market whose rules are designed to keep trade moving.

================================================================================
04. ANTI-TRAFFICKING RULE
================================================================================

Human trafficking is one of the Freeport's clearest absolute prohibitions.

Covered offenses include:
- forced labor
- debt-bondage transport
- coerced recruitment
- abduction and sale
- sex trafficking
- treating any person as cargo or transferable property

The policy is moral and practical. A port that permits people to be bought and sold cannot remain a trusted neutral market; it becomes a predatory zone that serious traders and travelers avoid.

Punishment is severe and may reach the highest level available under Freeport law. Exact sentencing procedure remains open for a dedicated legal-system pass.

================================================================================
05. REGISTERED ADULT SEX WORK
================================================================================

The Freeport permits adult voluntary sex work under registration and oversight.

Registration is intended to establish recognized status and a traceable protection chain, not ownership by the authorities.

The system may record:
- chosen/legal working identity
- workplace or operating zone
- emergency contact or advocate
- health-service access
- reporting route for coercion, assault, disappearance, or exploitation
- voluntary entry and ability to leave

The Freeport authority monitors patterns of violence and disappearance because rape, murder, coercion, and predation would otherwise become common in the port's environment and destroy its claim to neutrality.

Protection rationale:
- safeguard workers' lives and health
- distinguish voluntary adult work from trafficking
- create evidence when someone disappears or is moved under coercion
- make abusive clients, managers, or gangs visible to enforcement
- preserve the port's business legitimacy

The system should not be portrayed as perfectly benevolent. It is bureaucratic, self-interested, and capable of corruption, but its controlling principle is that voluntary adult workers are recognized persons under Freeport protection, not untracked disposable bodies.

================================================================================
06. SECURITY / ENFORCEMENT
================================================================================

Security is a hybrid port authority rather than a conventional police department.

Functions:
- gate and cargo inspection
- mediation and rapid suppression of factional violence
- anti-trafficking investigation
- protection of registered workers and vulnerable travelers
- enforcement of containment rules for hazardous goods
- defense of port infrastructure and neutral status
- intelligence collection sufficient to identify predatory networks

The authority should feel capable enough to sustain the Freeport's existence but not omniscient. Smuggling, bribery, and covert activity remain possible; blatant violations of the few existential rules provoke concentrated response.

================================================================================
07. NARRATIVE USE
================================================================================

The Freeport can function as:
- a late-timeline hub or transit point
- a place where Spook, Ducky/Lupin, or other protagonists can enter/exit the former United States
- a visual callback to Romeo's Game 1 Port of Los Angeles missions
- a neutral intelligence exchange
- a location where incompatible factions coexist under enforced restraint
- a way to show how ordinary commerce reconstitutes itself after federal collapse

The strongest emotional effect comes from continuity: the player recognizes the bones of a place once fought over in Game 1, now transformed into a new political organism.

================================================================================
08. RESTRAINT / OPEN FLAGS
================================================================================

Do not depict the Freeport as:
- a consequence-free pirate carnival
- a utopian sanctuary
- a single crime lord's personal kingdom
- an excuse for repetitive vice-district spectacle

Open:
- collective-leadership roster and titles
- exact relationship to the California Republic and loyalist strongholds
- map extent beyond the former port
- currency/credit system
- exact anti-trafficking investigative and sentencing process
- health-service model and privacy rules for registered workers
- campaign and mission placement
