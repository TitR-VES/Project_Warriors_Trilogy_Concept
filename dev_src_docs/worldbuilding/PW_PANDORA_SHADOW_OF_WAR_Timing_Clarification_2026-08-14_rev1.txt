================================================================================
01. DATE / TIME CONTROL
================================================================================
- PANDORA day remains Thursday, September 7, 2028.
- Broad Palisade/SentinelMesh maintenance and staged payload propagation may begin
  on September 4, 2028 as established by the existing technical architecture.
- At 0230 EDT on September 7, 2028, Palisade Systems pushes the final compromised
  enabling update/component that transitions the already staged compromise into
  the attack-ready state required for the PANDORA launch sequence.
- The 0230 event is therefore not intended to erase the multi-day staging model;
  it is the final enabling/synchronization step within that model.

Narrative rule:
Do not describe PANDORA as one magical malicious update created and distributed
from scratch at 0230. The architecture remains an accumulated, prepositioned
compromise. The 0230 Palisade event is the final enabling action that makes the
coordinated attack possible on the intended timetable.

================================================================================
02. SHADOW OF WAR DEPENDENCY
================================================================================
SHADOW OF WAR begins at 2243 EDT on September 6, 2028 at Kings Bay, Georgia.

The Russian seven-man combat-diver/sabotage team is ordered to complete the
mission and be clear within three hours:
- mission start: 2243 EDT;
- three-hour clear-by point: 0143 EDT, September 7;
- PANDORA final enabling update: 0230 EDT, September 7;
- operational buffer between team clear-by and enabling event: 47 minutes.

The Russian tactical team is NOT told what will happen after its three-hour
window. Their dialogue only establishes that Command imposed the deadline and did
not explain the reason.

================================================================================
03. FOREIGN FOREKNOWLEDGE / COMPARTMENTATION
================================================================================
The timing relationship establishes a specific intelligence implication:
A highly compartmented Russian GRU/GU command layer possesses enough detailed
advance knowledge of the PANDORA schedule to synchronize SHADOW OF WAR and other
Russian preparations around the attack window.

This does NOT establish that:
- ordinary Russian servicemen know about PANDORA;
- Malinin or his six teammates know about PANDORA;
- every GRU/GU directorate knows the full architecture;
- the wider Russian military knows Palisade's technical mechanism;
- Russia controls the PANDORA system itself.

The intended model is compartmented foreknowledge: selected senior/intelligence
participants receive enough timing information to coordinate opportunistic or
preplanned operations while downstream units receive only operational deadlines.

This remains compatible with the existing PANDORA architecture rule that foreign
partners receive enough timing knowledge to synchronize operations without
receiving the whole architecture.

================================================================================

advance knowledge of the attack timetable.

The mission therefore functions as an evidence seam, not an exposition dump.
